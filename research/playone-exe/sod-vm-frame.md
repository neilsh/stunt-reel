# `PLAYONE.EXE`: Per-Frame Routine and Scene Composition

Part of the SOD VM notes: [`sod-vm.md`](sod-vm.md) lists the files and states the binary and method; section numbers run across the files.

## 9. Per-Frame Routine and Scene Composition

`[Supported Inference]`, static. The per-frame routine at `0x0EE9` (entered from `0x4A35` with `BX = 0x4888`, the snapshot copy of the film state made at `0x39B0`) builds the sun vector (`0x196C`), sets the view matrix (`0x11FC`), makes one 3D scene draw through `0x824:0x0A05`, then draws the credits string at `+0xFE` with `0x58BD`. There is no second view or window pass, so a scene the evaluator does not place has no extra layer to supply it.

`DS:0x2275`, which selects `0x824:0x07DC` with `AX = 0xE8`, `CX = 0xD0`, is a video-mode flag set next to an `int 10h` mode change (`0x6E1B`, `0x737B`), not a window compositor. The window in the `COL3-2.FLM` reference is therefore part of the one scene, not a composited inset.

_Found 2026-10-22 at `ff274e7`; see the [parity log](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md)._

**The credits text.** `[Established]`, static, and pixel-exact on the `INTRO.FLM` title cards of two captures ([`pfont.md` § 3.2](../formats/auxiliary/pfont.md#32-drawing-the-credits)). After the scene, `0x0F7B` skips the text when its first byte at `state + 0xFE` is negative, measures each line with `0x58D0` (the sum of `PFONT.BIN` widths at `DS:0x4F02`), centres it in the clip rectangle at `DS:0x2294..0x229A` as `left + ((right - left - width) >> 1)`, puts line 1 at `top + ((bottom - top - 28) >> 1)` and line 2 twenty rows lower (skipped when empty), and calls `0x58BD` with colour `0xFF`. `0x58BD` draws the string through the text driver slot `[0x225A]` twice: at `x + 1` in colour 0, then at `x` in the given colour, so the shadow lies under the ink. The font layout and the whole rule are in [`pfont.md`](../formats/auxiliary/pfont.md); the renderer draws it over offscreen pages ([`credits.rs`](../../crates/stunt-core/src/credits.rs)).

### 9.1 The Actor Shadow Draw

`[Established]` for the instruction sequences and the `work:0x806C` readers and writer listed here (disassembled, not run); `[Hypothesis]` for what they draw at run time (the shadow is the actor flattened onto the plane and filled in index 0: no live check, no render scored against the capture). Static, read from `build/re/playone.img` (2026-11-05, [#99](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/99)); the live breaks since are marked where they are given. The scene walk `0x1DD5` loops over the film's slots (list at `state + 0x2E`, count at `state + 0x1E`) at `0x1E07..0x1E3A`. For each slot it skips unless the flag byte `[si+1]` has bit 1 (draw) and bit 0 (exists) and the shape word `[si+2]` is nonzero, queues the actor with `0x1A90` (not for the slot equal to `[0x22DE]`, the view camera's, `0x1E26`; that skip leaves the shadow test below, so a view slot with draw, exists and shadow set and a shape still queues its shadow, which the cull keeps or drops like any other: `[Bounded Observation]`, read live below under **The view slot's shadow**; 151 corpus films view from such a slot by the #510 census, 160 by #478's), and when `[si+1]` bit 2 is set also calls `0x19FC` at `0x1E35`. Those three bits are `SIF_DRAW`, `SIF_EXISTS` and `SIF_SHADOW` ([`flm.md`](../formats/flm.md#32-object-type-flags-type_flags-u8)): the create handler `0x3CF2` stores the create's `type_flags` byte verbatim at slot `+1` (`0x3CFD`, `[Established]`, static).

| Image offset | Role |
|---|---|
| `0x19FC` | Shadow queue. Takes the height from slot `+0x20/+0x22` when `+0x22` is nonzero. Otherwise reads the plane through the pointer at slot `+0x1E` (`a`, `b`, `c` at `+0`, `+2`, `+4`; 32-bit `D` at `+6`) and solves `y = (D - a*x - c*z) / b` at the actor's x (`+4/+6`) and z (`+0xC/+0xE`); a null pointer gives y = 0 (`0x1A12`). Then it transforms that point, looks up the slot's own shape (`[si+2]`, `0x4C41`), culls (`0x1B0C`) and queues it at `0x1AD8`. |
| `0x1A90` | Actor queue: the same, at the actor's own y (`+8/+0xA`), with `or al, 0x80` at `0x1AD6`. The shadow entry skips that `or`, so the operation is skipped; whether the resulting bit `0x80` is set is inferred, not traced, because the byte it reads comes from byte 9 of the shape header, which is untraced (`[Supported Inference]`; see the sort row). |
| `0x1B88..0x1BFF` | Entry sort. Branches on bit `0x80` of byte `+0xF` (`0x1B9F`, `0x1BA5`), a binary-tree insertion (children at `[node]` and `[node+2]`) walked right subtree, node, left subtree by `0x1BE6`. At a node with the bit clear (a shadow) every new entry goes left, drawn after it; at a node with the bit set (an actor) a new shadow goes right, drawn before it, and a new actor goes by depth (`0x1BAB..0x1BC7`, the larger depth value right, a tie left). Given that bit clear on a shadow entry, every shadow is drawn before every actor, whatever their depths. `[Established]` (disassembled, not run) for the sort and walk. The bit's value on a shadow entry is `[Supported Inference]`: `0x19FC` enters `0x1A90`'s tail at `0x1AD8`, past the `or al, 0x80`, so byte `+0xF` is `al` as `0x1B0C` leaves it (`mov al,[es:9]` at `0x1B6C`, byte 9 of the shape's segment header); what that byte holds is not traced, and a shape with bit 7 set there would sort as an actor. Whether a larger depth value is farther is not read. |
| `0x1C00` | Draws one entry. Bit `0x80` set goes to `0x1CFB` (the actor, light as `R · L` on the non-stored-matrix path, see [`terrain-shade.md`](terrain-shade.md) section 4.2). Clear goes to `0x1C0D`: builds the rotation matrix (`0x04E5`, `work:0x80D2`), halves it, replaces the Y row (second triple, `work:0x80D8..0x80DC`) with `-(a*row0 + c*row2) / b` (`idiv` by the plane's `b`; a null plane pointer zeroes it, `0x1C4B`), combines with the view matrix (`0x060A`), sets `work:0x806C = 0xFF`, calls `0x1D5F` (which runs the display list through `0x9100`) and clears the byte. |

`work:0x806C` is read at least at `0x7FD2`, `0x7FFB`, `0x8015`, `0x8040`, `0x92B2`, `0x93C2`, `0x97B1`, `0x9896`, `0x995C`, `0x9A24`, `0x9EC5`, `0x9F29`, `0xA040`, `0xA05C` and `0xA9DC`. Nonzero changes the VM as follows, reader by reader (handler = the opcode's nine-byte slot `imm16 + 0x8240`, [section 1](sod-vm.md#1-dispatch)). The far gateways, which are not opcodes: the line gateway `0x7FD2` loads `DI = 0` instead of `work:0x41EA` before driver slot `[0x2244]`; `0x7FFB` returns without calling driver slot `[0x2248]`, so the one-pixel draws that call it draw nothing: the radius-0 branch of `OP_SPHERE` (`0x0FC`, `0x9E21`) and of opcode `0x2D9` (`0xA476`), and all of opcode `0x2D0` (`0xA3FC`); the flat-polygon gateways `0x8010` (test at `0x8015`) and `0x8040` load `CX = 0` instead of `work:0x41E8` before driver slot `[0x2246]`. The polygon handlers take the flat branch (`call [0x8080]`, then `lcall 0:8010`) instead of the Gouraud filler: `GVPOLY` (`0x168`, `0x995C`) and `GVPOLY16` (`0x2BE`, `0x9A24`) then step over their shade words; opcodes `0x333` (handler `0x97AA`, test `0x97B1`) and `0x33C` (handler `0x988F`, test `0x9896`), which act only while `work:0x806B` is set (else `0x333` skips ten bytes and `0x33C` eight), go straight to the next opcode, with no shade words to step over. These skip their work: the normal builders `0x15F` (`0x92B2`) and `0x132` (`0x93C2`), which skip `4 + 6·count` bytes and do not rewrite their opcode word to the `0x156` handler as a first normal run does; `OP_DISC` (`0x105`, handler `0x9EBF`, test `0x9EC5`), which draws no disc in the shadow (`OP_SPHERE`'s fill at `0x9DF3`, opcode `0x2D9`'s at `0xA448` and opcode `0x252`'s span table do not test the mode, so they still draw, in index 0); `OP_PERFDISK` (`0x10E`, `0xA040`); `OP_ANIMATE` (`0x1F8`, `0xA9DC`); and the sliced discs `OP_DISCSL` (`0x25B`, `0x9F29`) and `OP_PERFDISCSL` (`0x264`, `0xA05C`). Outside shadow mode each sliced disc builds its plain op's span table (`0xD11D`; through `0xA08E` for the perforated disc) without filling it, stores its start at `work:0x423E + n` for a last operand `n` (the third for `OP_DISCSL`, the fourth for `OP_PERFDISCSL`) and moves the span arena pointer `work:0x1952` past it. It stores 0 there instead in shadow mode, and when the build fails: for `OP_DISCSL` when the centre register's flag byte is set (`0x9F36`), `work:0x4218` is 0 (`0x9F5E`) or `0xD11D` finds the disc off screen, for `OP_PERFDISCSL` when `0xA08E` sets carry (centre depth under `0x80`, the high-word tests at `0xA156`/`0xA1AB`, or off screen). Of the ops at `0xA47F..0xA517`, `0x276` and `0x27F` push and pop `work:0x1952`, `0x288` fills one entry through `0:8040` and skips a zero entry (`0xA498`), so a stored 0 draws nothing, and `0x291`..`0x2AC` combine entries. So in shadow mode every polygon is flat palette index 0, drawn from the actor's own shape flattened onto the ground plane. [`memory-map-vm.md`](memory-map-vm.md) listed `0x806C` as an `OP_ANIMATE` gate only; that is one of its readers. Its only writer found is `0x1CE7`/`0x1CF4`.

**Where the plane and height come from** (`[Established]`, static). The only writer of slot `+0x1E` and `+0x20/+0x22` is the probe `0x240A`, which `0x1463` calls (`0x4A17` runs it on the snapshot `0x4888` before the frame draw `0x0EE9`) for each slot with `SIF_MOVES`, a shape, and `SIF_DRAW` or byte `+0` bit 3. It zeroes `+0x1C`, `+0x1E` and `+0x22`, then walks the scenery tree through a second dispatch table, `DGROUP:0x584`. At a ground leaf (`0x2631`) it counts edge crossings of the leaf polygon (anchor `x` and `z`) against the slot's integer `x` and `z` and on an odd count stores the leaf's plane address (leaf `+4`) at `+0x1E` (`0x26BE`); later leaves in walk order overwrite it, and a slot over no leaf keeps 0, a shadow on `y = 0`. At an object node (`0x257A`) it walks the shape's collision boxes (header word `+0xA`; box words `+2/+4`, `+6/+8`, `+0xA/+0xC` are the `x`, `y` and `z` ranges, `0x3047`); a box with code 8 at `+0xE` stores the object's `y` plus the box's least `y`, its top in the `y`-down axes, at `+0x20/+0x22` (`0x2A5B..0x2A8F`), so the shadow lies on that box, still tilted by the `+0x1E` plane. A slot without `SIF_MOVES` is never probed: its fields keep what they held, zero in the load image's records (`DS:0x2B2A`, 2 × 40 of `0x5A` bytes), since allocation clears only bytes `+0` and `+1`.

**Straight down** (`[Established]`, static). Row 1 of `R` yields a vertex's world `y` offset (`R` row-major on column vectors, draw matrix `0x80C0 · R`, [`memory-map-main.md`](memory-map-main.md) `0x8725`, `0x884A`), so `0x1C0D`'s replacement keeps each vertex's world `x` and `z` offset and puts it on the plane parallel to the leaf's through the shadow point: a projection along world `y`.

**No altitude test in the draw path** (`[Established]`, static). Nothing from `0x1E2F` through `0x19FC`, the cull `0x1B0C`, `0x1C0D` and `0x1D5F` reads the actor's own `y` (`+8/+0xA`). The display-list run inside `0x1D5F` (`0824:0EC0`, image `0x9100`) gets the shape, the slot's `+0x16` (`work:0x8124`) and, at `work:0x8084..0x8088`, the entry's view-space point, which for a shadow is the ground point; the slot pointer is gone by the far call (`0x1D81`). Its entry compares only that point and the shape radius, to pick one of two polygon-routine sets (`0x914F`, `0x9167`) that both run the stream, its 12 relocated `DGROUP` loads reach the span arena, the pending-object heads `0x2346` and the tick `0x22B8`, and its `SS` reads go to the scenery segment (`SS = 0x2AAA` live, `DS = 0x1575`), so none reaches a slot record. The probe's tree walk does test `y` (cube nodes, `0x2547`): a slot above a node's range finds no leaf there, and with no height box the shadow goes on `y = 0`, not nowhere. The films' flag changes follow no one height (`[Bounded Observation]`): 827 re-creates in 129 corpus films change only `SIF_SHADOW` (435 set it, 392 clear it). 397 of them, in 55 films, have `SIF_DRAW` clear, so the walk skips them at `0x1E13`; of the 430 on drawn shapes, in 104 films, the 239 sets and the 191 clears have median stream `y` −4.30 and −4.72 and reach −8060 and −6034.

**The view slot's shadow** (`[Bounded Observation]`, by breakpoint and capture; films, ticks and slots below). The view slot's shadow point lies straight below the eye, at view depth about 0. In the three slots read, the cull kept it for the two low eyes (0.15 m and 0.40 m, slot 0 of `BRETT.FLM` and `NEWFX.FLM`) and dropped it for the 3.3 m one (slot 2 of `TERMIN.FLM`); where the cut-over lies against the shape's radius was not sampled. `BRETT.FLM` (`corpus/siarchive/Films/OldPLAY/Vault/`) ticks 633 to 648, view slot 0 (a car 0.15 m up, record `0x393A` = `[0x22DE]`): the point (0, 2472, 0) passes the cull and `0x1C0D` draws the shadow entry whose word `+4` is the slot record in each sampled frame; `NEWFX.FLM` (`NewPLAY/Vault/`) ticks 1831 to 1855, view slot 0 (0.40 m up), the same. In the `BRETT.FLM` capture the shadow is a black band across the bottom of the view, 4,856 index-0 pixels in rows 150 to 184 on each of the 20 captured frames in the two spans where the stream's view slot casts it (stream ticks 400 to 444 and 595 to 758; the captured frames in them fall at ticks 409 to 452 and 606 to 761), with no car drawn. The breaks cover only the second span (armed from tick 600); the first rests on the capture and the stream census. `TERMIN.FLM` (`OldPLAY/Vault/`) ticks 840 to 897, view slot 2 (a prop 3.3 m up, no `SIF_MOVES`): the point (0, 8446, −14 or −8) is outside the cull's `y` limit of 2348, so the shadow is not queued. The `NEWFX.FLM` run also showed slot 8 (flags `0x17`, no `SIF_MOVES`, never probed) with `+0x1E` = `0x0320`, a level plane, left in its record by a writer not traced.

**A high actor's shadow is lost in the view cull** (`[Bounded Observation]`, by breakpoint; ticks and slots below). The handbook says an airborne prop's shadow "will only appear when it's close enough to the ground" (`SIManual.txt:5039-5066`). In the frames sampled, every shadow kept its leaf's plane (the `0x1A18` branch, never the null `0x1A12`) with its actor 1.2 to 383.5 m up, and each one checked at the cull, its actor 10 to 383.5 m up, was rejected there: `0x1B0C` rejects it at `0x1A8A` and `0x19FC` returns at `0x1A8F` without queueing it; no shadow entry reached `0x1C0D`. The cull takes the shadow's view-space point (`DX`, `BP`, `DI`) and the shape radius `r` (header `+4`, at the point's exponent) and rejects a point behind the eye (`0x1B3E`), or one whose `|x|` exceeds `(z + (r >> [0x229E])/2)·[0x22A0]/65536 + r·[0x229C]/65536` or whose `|y|` exceeds the same with `[0x22A2]` (`0x1B2C..0x1B67`; live: `0x22A0` = 40960, `0x22A2` = 23552, and `0x229C`, `0x229E` = 32767, 0 in `CTRY.FLM` and 32726, 3 in `FLIP.FLM`). `CTRY.FLM` ticks 131 to 144, slot 0 about 10.5 m up: the ground point is below the frame (tick 131: view `y` 10032 at depth 9728 against a limit of 5600), while the actor is queued and drawn. `FLIP.FLM` (`corpus/siarchive/Films/NewPLAY/Vault/`) ticks 150 to 156, slots 8 and 29 at 382 and 383.5 m: behind the eye (depth −262 to −283). Ticks 1036 to 1043, slot 8 at 222 to 226 m: below the frame (tick 1036: `y` 884 at depth 1400, `r` = 15, so `(r >> 3) / 2` = 0 and the limit is 503 + 7 = 510). Every plane read was level (`a = c = D = 0`); no frame with a high actor over raised ground was found, so the cube-node miss above was not seen live. The cutoff observed in these frames is therefore geometric: the cull drops a shadow whose ground point is out of view, and in these frames the camera framing a high actor does not show the ground beneath it. That this is the whole of the handbook's cutoff is `[Hypothesis]`: no frame was found with a high actor whose ground point is in view, and `STUNT.EXE` draws film shadows with the same code and sets the flag with no height compare ([`stunt-exe/shadow.md`](../stunt-exe/shadow.md)). Apart from its distance-exponent test, the cull drops a shadow when the sphere of the actor's radius around its ground point is out of view, so a renderer that clips the shadow to the view would draw the same frames (`[Hypothesis]`, not scored).

## Ruled out

* **An altitude test in the shadow draw's setup** (section 9.1). No instruction from `0x1E2F` to `0x1D5F`'s setup reads the actor's `y`, though the probe `0x240A` does (section 9.1) ([the #478 static read](../../work/2026-11/478_shadow-plane/journal.md#2026-11-14-the-shadow-plane-writer-the-view-slot-and-the-0x806c-readers-read-statically)).
* **A `y` compare in the display-list run inside `0x1D5F`** (`0824:0EC0`, section 9.1). The VM is handed the ground point in view space, not the slot, and its `DGROUP` loads reach no slot record ([the #512 read](../../work/2026-11/512_altitude-cutoff/journal.md#2026-11-16-the-shadows-cull-the-display-list-run-and-stuntexes-shadow-draw)).
* **The probe dropping a high actor's plane, at 383 m or less over level ground** (section 9.1). `CTRY.FLM` slot 0 and `FLIP.FLM` slots 8 and 29 kept their leaf plane at 1.2 to 383.5 m; untested over raised ground ([the #512 breaks](../../work/2026-11/512_altitude-cutoff/journal.md#2026-11-16-the-shadows-cull-the-display-list-run-and-stuntexes-shadow-draw)).
