## 1. Parity Loop (Capture vs. Render)

The parity loop compares what the original player scans out against what the native engine rasterizes for the same film. All evidence lands under `build/parity/` (gitignored); `mise run capture-ref` drives the capture.

The capture side (control socket direction, frame gating on the post-retrace trap, dialog automation, load safety, the withdrawn `VRT` route) is documented once in [`capture.md`](playone-exe/capture.md); it is not repeated here. What remains is how the captured pages are read and compared:

* **Page extraction (`[Established]`):** `PLAYONE.EXE` scans out the Mode 13h page at `A000:0000` during playback (`VGA MODE` reports `disp-start=0h`). `MEMDUMPBIN A000:0000 FA00 <path>` writes the 64000-byte page; the debugger parses both addresses and lengths in hex, and resolves host paths only when given forward-slash absolute paths without quotes. DOS 8.3 normalization uppercases the written filename.
* **Palette (`[Established]`):** `VGA DACPAL` prints 8 entries per line as 6-hex-digit RGB triples in 6-bit DAC units, scaled to `0..=255` before luminance comparison with the capture scaling defined in [`palette-and-shading.md` § 1.1](formats/concepts/palette-and-shading.md#11-vga-hardware-dac-principles), which also records how it differs from the renderer's authored palette.
* **Comparator (`[Established]`):** `stuntreel parity` reduces both sides to Rec. 601 luminance, stretches $320 \times 200 \rightarrow 320 \times 240$ per `[I6]`, scores `compute_frame_ssim` (window 11, stride 8), and reports the IoU of each side's non-sky mask plus `contact_NNNN.png` sheets (`reference | rendered | |difference|`).
* **Tick counter (`[Established]`):** `parity` matches each capture to the render at the same `film_tick` (`DS:0x4896`, with `global_tick` from `DS:0x22B8`). Offsets and the live-sampling evidence (including the film-clock/global-counter offset, which is not constant): [`MEMORY_MAP.md` `# [MEM-FLM-TICK]`](../docs/MEMORY_MAP.md#-mem-flm-tick) and [`memory.md` § 3.2](playone-exe/memory.md#32-tick-counter-evidence).
* **Interval frame extraction (`[Established]`):** To prevent disk exhaustion and accelerate continuous regression auditing across long film choreographies, `stuntreel --out-dir <dir> --frame-step <N>` samples the 50 Hz timeline every $N$ ticks. `--frames <A..B>` is a half-open range of 1-based tick ordinals; extraction writes `tick_%05d.png` named by the 0-based 50 Hz tick rendered, which is the film clock `stuntreel parity` looks up. With `--sample-count` (or `--video`) the samples need not fall on whole ticks, so they are written as `sample_%04d.png` (1-based sample index) and are not parity inputs. When `--video` is also present, video sampling at `--fps` drives the loop and `--frame-step` does not apply.
* **Video container export (`[Established]`):** Complementing static PNG inspection, `stuntreel --video <path>` encodes rendered frame streams directly into standard container formats (`.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mkv`, `.avi`) via an asynchronous frame pipe to `ffmpeg`. Frames are sampled from the authored 50 Hz timeline at `--fps` (default $30$) with $4:3$ aspect correction ($320 \times 240$ per `[I6]`), interpolating between slice keyframes. `--frames <A..B>` selects a half-open range of 1-based tick ordinals. The container is trimmed to the authored span via ffmpeg's `-t`, so `CTRY.FLM` (140 slices, 1,342 ticks, $26.84\text{ s}$) samples 806 frames at $30\text{ fps}$; ffmpeg encodes 805 and reports $26.833\text{ s}$ (nearest whole frame; $26.84\text{ s}$ exactly at `--fps 50`). If `ffmpeg` is not detected on `PATH`, the engine terminates gracefully with an informative diagnostic without abnormal termination. Implemented under `T11` in [`TEAM_SHEET.md`](../docs/TEAM_SHEET.md).

---
* **Fade floor (`[Bounded Observation]`):** `PLAYONE` fades its palette to black over the end of `CTRY.FLM` (about tick 990 to 1337), and the renderer applies the same authored fade, so renders are scored unscaled. The comparator measures a per-frame fade level from the captured palette; frames below `--min-fade-level` (default 0.25, ticks 1260 onward for this capture set) are reported but not scored, because SSIM of near-black frames says little. The headline is `mean_ssim_scored`, quoted with its scored-frame count; the end-hold captures are the faded tail, not a blank scene. The render's clear colour for the IoU sky mask comes from its `palette_controls.json`. Method and fields are in [`research/parity/method.md`](../research/parity/method.md#21-fade-floor). The fade itself is authored (`[Established]`): film variable 14 is palette brightness, 15 saturation and 13 a night term, interpolated per frame by `PLAYONE` and applied by the renderer ([`research/playone-exe/palette-fade.md`](../research/playone-exe/palette-fade.md), Q-FLM-09).

## 2. Camera Look Direction & Partial Model Fidelity (`CTRY.FLM`) (`[Bounded Observation]`)

* On `CTRY.FLM` tick 1 the view slot sits at `(-3306.62, -14.0, 2614.11)` m, 7.0 m from the drawn actor at `(-3313.4, -12.3, 2614.53)` m, both in DOS axes (GLOSSARY.md). The evaluator maps DOS axes to `[I1]` by a 180° rotation about Z (positions `(-x, -y, z)`, orientations `q_z180 * q`; `[Bounded Observation]`, consistent with § 3), so the evaluated eye is `(3306.62, 14.0, 2614.11)` m and every `TrajectoryRecord` is `[I1]` with no consumer-side heuristic. A camera target at the world origin is 4215 m away and behind the scene, so the view slot's BAM rotation — not an origin default — defines the look direction: `target = eye + rotate_quat(rotation_quat, [0, 0, 1]) * 1000 m`.
* The stored slot rotation maps camera space to world space with `+Z` pointing into the screen, and its pitch angle `rx` (`rot_bam[1]`) is the negative of `quat_from_euler`'s `pitch`. With both conventions applied, the tick 1 look direction lands **1.4°** off the bearing to the drawn actor; every other combination of axis sign and pitch/yaw sign tested lands 8°–178° off. Without the pitch negation the camera misses the actor entirely and every rendered frame is sky.
* `CTRY.ALL` holds 127 `MODEL_*.SOD` chunks. With the IPG subtype-4 operand width, SHADE16, and TAP decoding corrected, 126 decode to `RETURN`/EOF and only `0x0107` stops early at byte `0x16` (opcode `0x01F8`, ANIMATE). `0x0402`–`0x0408` now reach `RETURN` with non-degenerate geometry: `OP_TAP` (`0x022E`) copies `count` 28-byte anchor records into the vertex register bank, using the operand form `reg_off, count, count × anchor_byte_offsets` (`# [FMT-SOD-TAP]`, `# [MEM-SOD-ANCHOR-TABLE]`) `[Established]`. `T09` is complete; `T10` (ANIMATE) remains open in [`TEAM_SHEET.md`](../docs/TEAM_SHEET.md). The drawn actor's chunk (`shape_id 0x7F37`, 7604 bytes) now reaches `RETURN` at `0x11F2`, recovering the two facets after `0x02B5` (SHADE16; two-word operand width `[Established]`, lighting effect `[Bounded Observation]`). The parity renderer keeps the geometry decoded before any stop (`transpile_sod_lenient`) rather than discarding the model.
* **`OP_ANIMATE` (`0x01F8`) (`[Bounded Observation]`):** The saved `PLAYONE.EXE` disassembly shows a variable-length resource table and a screen-space pixel blit, not a 3D joint-angle update. `CTRY.ALL` chunk `0x0107` emits no polygons, but that alone does not establish that it has no visible effect. The `.FLM` `animate: u16` values co-occur with SOD resource tables in `Ac`, but the dataflow into the selector remains unresolved; see [`sod-animate.md`](playone-exe/sod-animate.md). T10 remains open.

## 3. Static World Scenery & Terrain Assembly

The terrain heightmap (`ANCHORPT.DAT`), surface mesh (`MAP.BIN`), scene graph (`SCENERY.scn`) and ocean quad are specified and evidenced in [`research/formats/auxiliary/map.md` § 3.5](formats/auxiliary/map.md#35-empirical-island-terrain-ingestion--scenery-traversal-established) and [§ 3.1](formats/auxiliary/map.md#31-global-ocean-water-quad); they are not repeated here. Parity result:

* **Visual Parity Realization (`CTRY.FLM`)**: Rasterizing world scenery and static props alongside actor aircraft boosts geometric non-sky IoU from $\approx 0.01$ to **$0.822$** and frame 1 SSIM to **$0.741$**, eliminating the empty solid-green background defect.
