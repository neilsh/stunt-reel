# Parity: Actor Ground Shadow

`[Bounded Observation]`: `CTRY.FLM` (114 scored frames) and `INTRO.FLM` (209), `mean_ssim_scored`. Covers the black ground shadow the capture draws below each shadow-casting actor. Position and body are in [`hero-aircraft-placement.md`](hero-aircraft-placement.md), orientation in [`hero-aircraft-orientation.md`](hero-aircraft-orientation.md). Method: [`method.md`](method.md); score history: journal entries, and before them the parity log.

## 1. Ground shadow

* **Ground shadow.** The capture draws a black monoplane-shaped silhouette on the ground below the aircraft (the aircraft is a biplane; only one actor draw exists at tick 448). 65,982 black pixels over the 114 scored frames (up to 1,588 in one, present in 83), offset from the actor centroid by median dx -29, dy +41 px. Nothing in our renderer drew it until [#206](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/206) (**Ground shadow: drawn**, below); the measurements in this bullet predate that. Its source is narrowed in the next bullet: the shape does not contain it; the engine draws it. A flattened copy at a guessed constant height or along a sun direction does not reproduce it (see Ruled out). Re-measured 2026-11-02 on the current reference capture (`vault-ctry` 004): capture index 0 covers 64,596 scene pixels over the 114 scored frames (peak 1,596), present in every frame because index 0 also draws distant detail; within our actor's bounding box widened 60 px each side and extended 100 rows down, and outside our actor, 54,685 pixels, 20 or more on 72 frames, centroid offset from our actor's centroid median dx -10, dy +57. These are upper bounds: the window is not a shadow mask, so any distant index-0 detail inside it is counted too, and it can pull the centroid. The offset moved with our aircraft (now 8 rows higher and no longer left), not with the shadow. After #132 (`793895e`), same window and the same caveat: 54,663 pixels, 20 or more on 71 frames, median offset dx -9, dy +59. The readout grades `shadow-missing` 2 or more on 6 of 12 CTRY sheets and 5 of 12 INTRO sheets ([`attribution.md`](attribution.md#2-current-breakdown)), graded before the shadow was drawn ([#99](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/99)).
* **Ground shadow: the source, narrowed** (2026-11-05, static, [#99](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/99); full reading in [`sod-vm.md` § 9.1](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#91-the-actor-shadow-draw)). `[Bounded Observation]` that the hero shape holds no shadow: `CTRY.ALL` chunk `0x7F37` (7,604 bytes; the `stisldem/VAULT` copy, the other copy gives the same) has no `0x0171` (`OP_AIRBRUSH`) word at any even offset, so no airbrush op sits in it, reachable or not. `[Established]` (disassembled, not run) for the instruction sequences below, in the load image `build/re/playone.img` (`mise run unlzexe`): the actor loop at `0x1E07..0x1E3A` tests each slot's flag byte `[si+1]` for bits 1, 0 and 2 in that order. `[Hypothesis]` that these are `SIF_DRAW`, `SIF_EXISTS` and `SIF_SHADOW` ([`flm.md`](../formats/flm.md#32-object-type-flags-type_flags-u8)): `flm.md` defines `type_flags` in a create record, and the create handler that copies it into `[si+1]` is not traced; the support is the bit order and the flag census (slot 0 of both films carries `0x04`, camera rigs do not). The loop draws the actor with `0x1A90` (skipped for the slot equal to `[0x22DE]`, the view camera's, at `0x1E26`; the skip leaves the shadow test, but the view slots read live on `INTRO.FLM`, 1 and 4, have flags `0x19` and shape 0 and reach neither, `[Bounded Observation]`, question 10), and when bit 2 is set calls `0x19FC` at `0x1E35`. `0x19FC` finds the ground plane under the actor's x and z (a null pointer at slot `+0x1E` gives y = 0, `0x1A12`, and `0x1C4B` zeroes the Y row, so the shadow lies flat on y = 0) and queues a second draw of the **same shape** (`[si+2]`) at that point, without the `0x80` flag the actor's queue entry carries (`0x1AD6`; the shadow entry's bit is then byte 9 of the shape header, so clear is `[Supported Inference]`). `0x1C00` draws such an entry through `0x1C0D`: it builds the object's matrix, replaces its Y row (the second triple, `work:0x80D8..0x80DC`) with `-(a*row0 + c*row2) / b` from the plane's coefficients, so the model's height no longer lifts points off the plane, and runs the normal display-list draw with the byte `work:0x806C` set to `0xFF`. In that mode the polygon gateways `0x8010` and `0x8040` draw with `CX = 0` (palette index 0, black) instead of the polygon colour `work:0x41E8`, `GVPOLY` and `GVPOLY16` (`0x995C`, `0x9A24`) take their flat path and skip their shade words, and the `OP_ANIMATE` image draw (`0x92B2`) takes its skip path. `[Hypothesis]` when written (no live check and no render scored against the capture yet; since scored, see **Ground shadow: drawn** below) for the reading that follows: the capture's black silhouette is the aircraft itself, flattened straight down onto the terrain plane beneath it and filled flat in index 0. That also explains the shape: seen from above, a biplane's two wings overlap into the "monoplane" outline, and the ground offset (dy +41 to +59 below the actor) is the aircraft's height. `[Hypothesis]` for the plane being the terrain leaf polygon (the pointer at slot `+0x1E`), and for the shadow queue entry drawing behind the actor: neither is traced (the draw order has since been worked out, question 9). Nothing was run; the draw was read, not observed.
  **What it licenses.** `[Hypothesis]` when written, superseded: the rule built and scored (next bullet) flattens onto one plane under the actor's origin, not the terrain under each vertex. The proposal was to carry `casts_shadow` (slot create flag `0x04`) on `ActorTransform` and the scene-frame actor; for each such actor, draw its shape a second time in [`scene_renderer.rs`](../../crates/stunt-render/src/scene_renderer.rs)'s actor loop with every vertex's world Y replaced by the terrain height at its own x and z (the engine's plane is one terrain polygon, so a tile-wise height query is the nearest equivalent), every polygon flat in palette index 0 with no shade and no `OP_ANIMATE` image, as an `[I5]` decal so it does not z-fight the ground. The earlier sweeps (Ruled out) flattened to one constant height or sheared along a sun direction, which this rule is neither of: it projects along the vertical onto the sloping terrain. Expected gain stays small, about 0.005 to 0.01 SSIM on `CTRY.FLM`. **It waits on** three things the static read leaves open (Open questions 6 to 8 and 11): where the ground plane at slot `+0x1E` comes from, what the `+0x20/+0x22` override is, and the handbook's altitude cutoff, which `0x19FC` does not show.
* **Ground shadow: drawn** (2026-11-05, [#206](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/206), adopted, evidence-backed). `casts_shadow` (create flag `SIF_SHADOW`) rides on `ActorTransform`; the scene frame gives each such actor a shadow matrix, the actor matrix flattened straight down onto the plane of the terrain triangle under its origin (`y = 0` off the map, as for a null plane) and lifted the `[I5]` 1 mm, and the renderer draws the shape again through it, every polygon index 0, in the decal pass. One plane under the origin, not the terrain under each vertex, because that is what `0x1C0D` does (it rewrites the matrix once). Scores (`mean_ssim_scored`): CTRY 0.8642 -> 0.8722, INTRO 0.8166 -> 0.8217, IoU unchanged. At CTRY tick 444 the shadow matches the capture's in shape and place. What the sweep settled and left open: the terrain plane and the level plane `y = 0` give the same mean scores on both films, and every sampled actor position (CTRY ticks 100, 444, 895; INTRO 48, 1009, 3306) is over a level terrain triangle at `y = 0`, so this sweep does not tell the plane source apart; no per-frame comparison was made (question 6 stays open). An altitude cutoff of 3, 8, 15 or 30 m above the plane scores the same as none, and 0.01 m (no shadow) gives back the old scores, so all of the gain comes from an actor within 3 m of the ground; the score cannot place the handbook's cutoff (question 8). Raising the lift to 1, 3 or 10 cm: CTRY 0.8738 / 0.8739 / 0.8667, INTRO 0.8214 / 0.8187 / 0.8144; 1 cm is within the noise floor and was not adopted. INTRO tick 1009 (a triplane on the ground, eye near the ground) first showed the shadow broken into specks where the capture's is solid. That was back-face culling, not the depth test: the shadow went through the actor pipeline, which culls by screen winding, and a face flattened onto the ground keeps its winding, so every face wound downward (the wings' undersides here) was dropped. Drawing the shadow through a pipeline that culls nothing makes it solid and matches the capture's outline at that tick: CTRY 0.8722 -> 0.8725, INTRO 0.8217 -> 0.8273, IoU unchanged (adopted, heuristic track; no image offset says how PLAYONE's shadow draw treats facing, only the capture).

## Ruled out

All scores `mean_ssim_scored`, CTRY / INTRO unless a single film is named. Baselines moved between rounds (0.771 / 0.676 on 2026-10-22, 0.7708 / 0.6762 at `d90a047`, 0.7732 / 0.6799 after page timing); each sweep is read against its own baseline. Nothing below was adopted.

**Shadow as geometry or an airbrush op inside the hero shape** (2026-11-05, `[Bounded Observation]`). `CTRY.ALL` chunk `0x7F37` has no `0x0171` word; the shadow is drawn by the engine from the slot flag (section 1), not by the shape. `OP_ANIMATE` is skipped, not used, in shadow mode.

**Ground shadow at a constant height** (2026-10-22). A black copy of each actor flattened onto a horizontal plane (swept at -1, 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 6 and 10 m, layer and palette index 0) lowered CTRY `mean_ssim_scored` from 0.769 to between 0.762 and 0.766 at every height: the capture's shadow is not a flat copy at a guessed ground height.

**Ground shadow as a flattened copy** (decal bias correct, 2026-10-23). The actor geometry sheared onto the ground along five sun directions (0.3,0.9,0.3 and sign variants, plus straight down), forced to index 0 and lifted 0.1 m: off 0.7713 / 0.6775; presets 0.7648 / 0.6727, 0.7663 / 0.6712, 0.7677 / 0.6715, 0.7665 / 0.6691, 0.7677 / 0.6716. Every direction loses on both films. An earlier flat black shadow at several ground heights also lost 0.003 ([log](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md)).

## Open questions

6. The ground plane for the shadow (the score cannot settle it: under every sampled actor on both films the terrain is level at `y = 0`, so the terrain plane and a null plane draw the same; [#206](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/206)): `0x19FC` reads three coefficients through the pointer at slot `+0x1E` (`a`, `b`, `c` at `+0`, `+2`, `+4` of the target and a 32-bit constant `D` at `+6`) and solves `y = (D - a*x - c*z) / b` at the actor's x and z. Who writes slot `+0x1E`, and whether it is the terrain leaf polygon the ground draw uses, is untraced ([#99](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/99)). Answered 2026-11-14 (`[Established]`, static): the probe `0x240A` stores the plane of the ground leaf whose polygon contains the slot's `x`, `z`, for slots with `SIF_MOVES` only; a slot over no leaf, or never probed, keeps 0 ([`sod-vm.md` § 9.1](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#91-the-actor-shadow-draw)). Whether to follow it in the renderer: [#499](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/499). _Source: [the #478 static read](../../work/2026-11/478_shadow-plane/journal.md#2026-11-14-the-shadow-plane-writer-the-view-slot-and-the-0x806c-readers-read-statically)._
7. Slot `+0x20/+0x22` is a precomputed shadow height that `0x19FC` uses when `+0x22` is nonzero. Its writer and when it is set (an object resting on a surface?) are untraced. Answered 2026-11-14 (`[Established]`, static): the same probe stores the top of an object's collision box with code 8 that the slot overlaps ([`sod-vm.md` § 9.1](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#91-the-actor-shadow-draw), [#499](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/499)).
8. The handbook says an airborne object's shadow appears only when it is close to the ground. `0x19FC` shows no altitude test; the cutoff may be in the cull `0x1B0C` or the ground-plane lookup. Narrowed 2026-11-05: on both films a cutoff anywhere from 3 to 30 m scores the same as none ([#206](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/206)), so the score cannot place it; a live probe of a high-altitude `INTRO.FLM` frame would settle it. Narrowed 2026-11-14: `PLAYONE`'s shadow path reads no actor height (`[Established]`, static), and the films' `SIF_SHADOW` flips follow no fixed height (`[Bounded Observation]`); the cutoff, if real, is in `STUNT.EXE`, still unread. _Source: [the #478 static read](../../work/2026-11/478_shadow-plane/journal.md#2026-11-14-the-shadow-plane-writer-the-view-slot-and-the-0x806c-readers-read-statically)._

10. What `[0x22DE]` holds. The loop skips the actor draw for that slot (`0x1E26`) but not its shadow test. Answered 2026-11-06: the view camera's slot record, `[0x4888 + 2 × view_obj + 0x2E]`, stored by the view setup at `0x1206` and read live on `INTRO.FLM` ([`memory-map.md`](../playone-exe/memory-map.md) § 1). The skip at `0x1E26` is of the actor draw only, so the slot reaches the shadow call at `0x1E35` only with draw, exists and shadow flags set and a nonzero shape `[si+2]`. `[Bounded Observation]`: the two view slots read live (slot 1 at ticks 2678-2766, slot 4 at 2801) had flag byte `0x19` (bits 1 and 2 clear) and shape 0, so neither reaches the shadow queue; no evidence yet shows a `[0x22DE]` slot that does. Whether our renderer should draw one stays open. Narrowed 2026-11-14 (`[Supported Inference]`): 160 corpus films, none a reference film, view from a slot with flags `0x1F` or `0x17` and a shape, which reaches the shadow queue; our player drops the view slot, shadow included ([#499](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/499)).
