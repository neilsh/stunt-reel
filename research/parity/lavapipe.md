# Parity: Rendering on Lavapipe

* **Scope**: how Mesa's software Vulkan driver, lavapipe, renders our frames differently from a GPU, and what the renderer does about it, and how long a lavapipe render takes. Cloud sessions ([ADR 0039](../../docs/adr/0039-cloud-sessions-fetch-pinned-game-archives.md)) and CI ([ADR 0006](../../docs/adr/0006-ci-renders-on-lavapipe.md)) both render on it. The findings name the Mesa build they come from; three builds were measured: Ubuntu's `mesa-vulkan-drivers` 25.2.8 (`llvmpipe (LLVM 20.1.2, 256 bits)`, cloud), and mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 (`llvmpipe (LLVM 21.1.7, 256 bits)`) and 26.2.6 (`llvmpipe (LLVM 23.1.4, 256 bits)`) on the RTX 4070 laptop, set up by [`render-on-lavapipe.md`](../../docs/howto/render-on-lavapipe.md).
* **Sources**: Mesa source (`src/gallium/auxiliary/draw/draw_pipe_clip.c` at `mesa-25.2.8`, `mesa-26.2.6` and `main`; `src/gallium/drivers/llvmpipe/lp_context.c`), `cdb` stack samples and timed renders from #596, parity runs logged in [`log.md`](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md) on 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-15, and the #265 journal.
* **Evidence tier**: stated at the start of each section.

## 1. Near ground polygons drew palette index 0

`[Bounded Observation]`, 2026-11-07, on `9f04164`, Ubuntu's Mesa 25.2.8; fixed by a guard in `fragment_index`. Rendered on lavapipe (a cloud session with no GPU; CI's adapter too, [ADR 0006](../../docs/adr/0006-ci-renders-on-lavapipe.md)), CTRY and INTRO scored 0.6446 and 0.6040 against the GPU rows' 0.8725 and 0.8273, with IoU identical. The loss was in the props class: in 52 of 114 CTRY and 69 of 209 INTRO scored frames, large ground decals close to the eye (the field under the hero at CTRY 389-444, the sand at INTRO 1295-1506 and 2632-2786) drew palette index 0 over their whole area, while the same props far away drew their own index (CTRY 1257). `fragment_index` in `mode13h.wgsl` rounds the `@interpolate(linear) shade` varying, and that varying arrived as NaN, which `u32(clamp(floor(NaN + 0.5), 0, 255))` turns into 0. Ruled out at CTRY 422: the actor shadow draw (black with it off) and the decal depth bias (black with slope 0); also ruled out: crossing the near plane as the reason here (section 2). `fragment_index` now falls back to the flat `palette_index` when `shade` is not finite (candidate (c), section 3), and the GPU test `a_ground_polygon_under_a_low_eye_keeps_its_index` fails on lavapipe without it (25,866 of 51,840 ground pixels at index 0). Capture, then lavapipe on `9f04164`, then with the guard on `72028d1`: [`ctry-422-lavapipe-nan-shade.png`](../../work/2026-11/lavapipe-nan-shade/images/ctry-422-lavapipe-nan-shade.png), [`intro-1495-lavapipe-nan-shade.png`](../../work/2026-11/lavapipe-nan-shade/images/intro-1495-lavapipe-nan-shade.png) ([`log.md`](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md), 2026-11-07).

## 2. Mechanism: the clipper's screen-space weight is 0/0

`[Supported Inference]`: Mesa source read, its clipper replayed on the frame's own triangles, and a live NaN trap agree; no trap inside Mesa itself. Mesa 25.2.8 (Ubuntu `25.2.8-0ubuntu0.24.04.4`) llvmpipe, under lavapipe, clips in software through the gallium `draw` module and turns the guard band off (`lp_context.c` line 339), so a triangle is clipped against x = -w, x = w, y = -w, y = w, then the half-z near plane, in that order (`draw_pipe_clip.c` `do_clip_tri`). For each new vertex, `interp()` (`draw_pipe_clip.c` lines 183-209) interpolates perspective attributes with the 3D weight `t`, but `noperspective` ones with a screen-space weight `t_nopersp = (dst_x/dst_w - out_x/out_w) / (in_x/in_w - out_x/out_w)`. It picks the axis by testing the clip-space x of the endpoints for inequality, then divides by their screen-space difference. An edge that an earlier pass left lying on a side plane x = ±w has both endpoints at screen x ±1, so the divisor is 0. If that edge reaches behind the eye, the opposite x plane cuts it at w = 0, x = 0, and `dst_x/dst_w` is 0/0. Either way `t_nopersp` is not finite, and `LINTERP(t, a, a) = a + t * 0` is NaN even for an attribute that is the same at every vertex. The near-plane pass then interpolates from that vertex, and the post-clip polygon is emitted as a fan from its first vertex, so every fan triangle carries a NaN vertex and the polygon's whole visible area reads NaN. That is the case observed here, a large ground polygon under a low camera, but not the only one: the divisor is 0 for any edge whose endpoints share a screen x while their clip-space x differ, so the near plane alone can produce it, for example on the edge from (1, -1, -1, 2) to (2, 1, 1, 4) (screen x 0.5 at both ends and at the cut; review on #264). The guard catches every non-finite `shade`, whichever edge produced it. A GPU clips only near and far, with a guard band for x and y. Evidence: (1) a temporary sentinel in `fragment_index` (NaN 250, infinity 251, out of range 252/253, more than 0.5 from `palette_index` 254) marked every layer-3 sample in rows 120-184 at CTRY 422 (10,134) and 400 (9,791) as NaN, and none at 1257. (2) A float32 port of `do_clip_tri`/`interp` run on the 2,203 world-prop triangles within 1,500 m of the eye, with the tick's dumped `view_proj`: 184 need clipping and 62 of them cross the near plane, yet exactly one gives NaN. That one is the index-196 triangle of the 128 m field tile under the eye (x 3200-3328, z 2432-2560). Its first NaN comes where an edge on x = w (both endpoints at screen x 1.0) is cut by the opposite x plane at w = 0, and its clipped quad has 2 NaN vertices. The other 61 near-crossing triangles stay finite, so the near-plane clip alone is not the cause in this frame. Related upstream history: Mesa bug 98851 (2016, `noperspective` attributes wrongly interpolated after clipping) concerns the same `t_nopersp` code; no report of this zero divisor was found.

The same fault shows on both mesa-dist-win builds, and upstream has not changed the code (`[Bounded Observation]`, 2026-11-15, `crates` `52d84e6`). With the guard removed, CTRY 400 draws 41,933 index-0 pixels on 25.2.8 and 41,934 on 26.2.6 (none on the GPU), and LOCATION 3994 44,580 and 44,427. A sentinel build (non-finite `shade` 250, below -0.5 251, above 255.5 252) draws 250 on those pixels, all but the at most five per tick that differ from the GPU with the guard on too, and draws no 251 or 252, on either build, at CTRY 400 and 422 and LOCATION 3994, 4005, 4445 and 4467. Which polygons it hits moves with the build: at CTRY 422 it draws index 0 on 43,117 pixels on 25.2.8 and 410 on 26.2.6, at LOCATION 4467 on 41,057 and 100. `interp()` reads the same in `mesa-26.2.6` as in `mesa-25.2.8`; the one difference in `draw_pipe_clip.c` between the two tags is a `SWAP` macro in `do_clip_tri` (`8a1a4103`, 2025-07-23), which is also the file's last commit on `main` as read on 2026-11-15. _Source: [Lavapipe beside the GPU on one crates tree](../../work/2026-11/265_lavapipe-parity/journal.md#2026-11-15-lavapipe-beside-the-gpu-on-one-crates-tree)._

## 3. Candidate fixes

Lavapipe only; a `DBG_SHADE` sweep plus one `mise run parity` per candidate, on `b0c90c2` and `9f04164` (identical renderer); (c) adopted. `mean_ssim_scored` CTRY / INTRO, IoU 0.9897 / 0.9856 in every row, then index agreement:

* base (`linear`, rounded): 0.6446 / 0.6040; 0.663 / 0.612.
* (a) `shade` `@interpolate(perspective)`: 0.8722 / 0.8281; 0.8893 / 0.8289. Not byte-identical on a GPU: a Gouraud (`GVPOLY`) polygon's index becomes perspective-correct instead of screen-linear, which departs from PLAYONE's filler ([`terrain-shade.md`](../playone-exe/terrain-shade.md) section 6). Its lavapipe stores differ from (c)'s.
* (b) flat `palette_index` unless the polygon is Gouraud, marked by a temporary vertex flag bit 3 set in `tessellate.rs` when `vertex_palette` is non-empty: 0.8722 / 0.8281; 0.8893 / 0.8289. On a GPU it should be byte-identical: flat polygons already round to their own index, and Gouraud ones keep `linear`. It needs a `SodVertex` flag (a seam-contract change), and Gouraud polygons stay exposed on lavapipe: its INTRO store differs from (c)'s, its CTRY store does not.
* (c) guard, adopted: a non-finite `shade` (exponent bits all set) falls back to the flat `palette_index`: 0.8722 / 0.8281; 0.8893 / 0.8289. Shader-only; the RTX 4070's index planes are byte-identical with and without it (section 4). On lavapipe a Gouraud polygon hit by the bug draws its provoking vertex's index instead of its gradient (section 5).
* (b) and (c) together: byte-identical to (c) on lavapipe on both films.
* flat `palette_index` everywhere (diagnostic, drops `GVPOLY` interpolation): 0.8721 / 0.8231; 0.8895 / 0.8282.

## 4. Lavapipe beside the GPU on one `crates` tree

`[Bounded Observation]`, 2026-11-15, all seven reference films, `crates` tree `52d84e6` (the `--software-only` branch, whose index planes on the GPU match `origin/main`'s tree `e1f9af7` on every tick), RTX 4070 laptop and mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 on the same machine; the cloud rows are #455's `expected.lavapipe` in [`references.toml`](references.toml), on `e1f9af7`.

**The guard leaves the RTX 4070's index planes byte-identical (20,621 ticks).** With the guard removed, the GPU renders every tick of all seven films with the same index plane (sha256 of each 64,000-byte plane: 0 of 20,621 ticks differ). A sentinel build draws no non-finite, negative or over-255 `shade` on the GPU at the eleven ticks of sections 2 and 5.

**Lavapipe rows match the GPU rows on all seven films**, within the 0.002 adoption margin, which is a policy bound and not a noise model ([`evidence-limits.md`](evidence-limits.md) section 3). `mean_ssim_scored`, local lavapipe minus GPU: CTRY +0.0000212, INTRO -0.0004507, LAND +0.0004120, BALLOON -0.0006492, TRAINTNL -0.0002315, BOMB +0.0000422, LOCATION +0.0000457. IoU agrees to 0.00002 or better on every film.

**Local and cloud lavapipe agree within 0.00004 but not identically**, on two LLVM versions and two `crates` trees: local minus cloud, CTRY +0.0000068, INTRO +0.0000006, LAND +0.0000001, BALLOON -0.0000370, TRAINTNL -0.0000002, BOMB +0.0000030, LOCATION +0.0000082; IoU within 0.00001. No SSIM is identical at the tables' seven decimals. By #265's criterion (every film within the 0.002 margin), rows from mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 stand in for GPU rows on these seven films on this tree, and the cloud's Ubuntu 25.2.8 rows agree with them; this is not shown for CI's lavapipe or for 26.2.6, and a change to Gouraud polygons or to depth order can score differently on lavapipe (section 5). Because the adapter names differ (LLVM 21.1.7 against 20.1.2), `mise run parity` reports a local run against those rows as `not comparable: other adapter`, so local renders are not drift-checked against them.

A first local LOCATION run scored 0.6344817 (563 frames down more than 0.005). Its binary was a guard-removed build left in `target/release` by the byte-identity check and reused with `--no-build`: its embedded WGSL has no guard, and its damage at LOCATION 3994, 4005, 4445 and 4467 matches a guard-removed render of those ticks pixel for pixel (43,216 of index 195 to 0 at 3994). Rendered again with the guard, LOCATION scores 0.8292054 (IoU 0.9784023, index agreement 0.8405, as the GPU's). _Source: [Lavapipe beside the GPU on one crates tree](../../work/2026-11/265_lavapipe-parity/journal.md#2026-11-15-lavapipe-beside-the-gpu-on-one-crates-tree)._

## 5. What still differs

`[Bounded Observation]`, 2026-11-15, mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 against the RTX 4070, `crates` `52d84e6`. With the guard, lavapipe differs from the GPU by 22.6 to 147.4 index-plane pixels per tick on average over each film (CTRY 22.6, INTRO 29.3, LAND 119.2, BALLOON 43.5, TRAINTNL 62.5, BOMB 98.6, LOCATION 147.4). The ticks with the largest solid regions of difference (pixels whose four neighbours also differ):

* **Gouraud polygons drawn flat** (BALLOON 145; LAND 227 inferred). The clipper's NaN hits a Gouraud (`GVPOLY`) polygon, and the guard draws its provoking vertex's index over the whole polygon. At BALLOON 145 the GPU draws a 169-171 gradient over 4,044 pixels where lavapipe draws a flat 168, and the sentinel build marks exactly those 4,044 pixels non-finite. At LAND 227, 2,686 pixels the GPU draws at 13 read non-finite, and with the guard 3,222 pixels go from 13 to 195. This is the cost candidate (c) accepted in section 3 (candidate (b) does not avoid it: it leaves Gouraud polygons unguarded, so they would draw index 0); a fix that recomputes the weight might remove it (untested). At LAND 227 the polygon type is inferred, not checked in the tick's data: 13 on the GPU against the guard's 195 on 2,686 non-finite pixels fits a Gouraud polygon drawn flat. The other 536 of the 3,222 pixels stay finite and still go from 13 to 195, unattributed.
* **Another polygon wins the depth test** (BOMB 1910, LOCATION 2599 and 2859). At BOMB 1910, 506 pixels inside actor layer 17 read 35 on lavapipe and 7 on the GPU; at LOCATION 2599 and 2859, 5,519 and 5,626 prop-layer pixels swap 14 and 169. Not the shade of the GPU's polygon: a build drawing every polygon at its flat `palette_index` gives the same differences, so another polygon of the same layer is drawn on top. At BOMB 1910 and LOCATION 2599 its `shade` is finite; at LOCATION 2859 the polygon lavapipe draws reads non-finite, so the clipper broke it, and whether it also moved its depth is open.
* **The hero's shadow at CTRY 422 is solid** on the GPU, 25.2.8 and 26.2.6 alike (1,342, 1,343 and 1,346 index-0 pixels in actor layer 16; the lavapipe frames differ from the GPU's by 25 and 23 pixels in all). The patches of the 2026-11-07 picture on `72028d1` do not recur on this tree; which of the 53 `crates/` commits since removed them is not bisected (#495, the ordered decal pass with depth bias, is one candidate).

A near-zero but finite divisor would give a Gouraud polygon a wrong but finite weight that the guard cannot catch; the sentinel found no `shade` below -0.5 or above 255.5 on lavapipe at any tick rendered, and no case is known. _Source: [Lavapipe beside the GPU on one crates tree](../../work/2026-11/265_lavapipe-parity/journal.md#2026-11-15-lavapipe-beside-the-gpu-on-one-crates-tree)._

## 6. Render time

`[Bounded Observation]`, 2026-11-15, mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 (`llvmpipe (LLVM 21.1.7, 256 bits)`) on the RTX 4070 laptop, one headless run per row at 320×200.

`SceneRenderer::prepare` wrote each draw's model matrix with its own `write_buffer`, and wgpu allocates a staging buffer per call. On lavapipe that allocation (`gpu_allocator::FreeListAllocator::allocate`) held about half of 30 main-thread samples, the rest waiting in `wait_for_fence`, with the process at 1.00 core whatever `LP_NUM_THREADS` was. Why the allocation costs more on lavapipe than on the NVIDIA driver is not measured. #596 packs a frame's matrices into one uniform buffer at dynamic offsets, uploaded with one `write_buffer`:

| Run | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| lavapipe, LOCATION ticks 12-300 | 150.5 s | 81.8 s |
| GPU, LOCATION ticks 12-300 | 20.9 s | 16.0 s |
| GPU, LOCATION's captured ticks (12-8262) | 301.2 s | 229.6 s |
| GPU, INTRO's captured ticks (12-3818) | 96.8 s | 67.8 s |

The render stores (`frames.zst`, `layers.zst`, `palettes.zst`) are byte-identical before and after, on lavapipe over LOCATION ticks 12-300 and on the GPU over every reference film's captured ticks. After the change, 16 of 20 main-thread samples sit in `wgpu_hal::vulkan::DeviceShared::wait_for_fence`, inside `vulkan_lvp`, and none in staging allocation. All-thread samples put lavapipe's work on one `vulkan_lvp` thread, the one that executes command buffers (busy in 52 of 65), while the other `vulkan_lvp` threads stay parked on a condition variable, and `OffscreenTarget::capture` waits for each frame's readback, so the main thread and that thread do not overlap. Mesa-dist-win ships no PDBs, so the thread's functions are known by offset only ([#600](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/600)). On the GPU only the main thread is busy: of 42 samples, 14 in wgpu's render-pass encoding, 11 waiting on the GPU and 8 in `GpuModel::collect_world_meshes`. _Source: [2026-11-15: where a frame's time goes after the change](../../work/2026-11/596_lavapipe-staging-buffers/journal.md#2026-11-15-where-a-frames-time-goes-after-the-change), [2026-11-15: one model-matrix upload per frame](../../work/2026-11/596_lavapipe-staging-buffers/journal.md#2026-11-15-one-model-matrix-upload-per-frame)._

## Ruled out

* **The actor shadow draw** (CTRY 422): with it off, the ground stays black.
* **The decal depth bias** (CTRY 422): with slope 0, the ground stays black.
* **Crossing the near plane as the cause in this frame**: 61 of 62 near-crossing triangles at CTRY 422 stay finite (section 2), though an edge of constant screen x can produce the NaN at the near plane alone.
* **A renderer change, #249's adapter choice included, as the cause of the lavapipe loss**: guarding the one NaN value leaves lavapipe less than the 0.002 adoption margin from the GPU at the same renderer ([`evidence-limits.md`](evidence-limits.md) section 3), too little for a renderer change to hide a 0.23 loss behind.
* **A LOCATION fault the guard misses** (mesa-dist-win 25.2.8): the 0.6344817 run used a guard-removed binary (section 4); [journal](../../work/2026-11/265_lavapipe-parity/journal.md#2026-11-15-lavapipe-beside-the-gpu-on-one-crates-tree).
* **A finite out-of-range `shade` on lavapipe, at the ticks rendered**: the sentinel drew no `shade` below -0.5 or above 255.5 at CTRY 400 and 422, LOCATION 3994, 4005, 4445 and 4467, BALLOON 145, LAND 227, BOMB 1910 or LOCATION 2599 and 2859, on 25.2.8, nor at the first six on 26.2.6 (section 5); [journal](../../work/2026-11/265_lavapipe-parity/journal.md#2026-11-15-lavapipe-beside-the-gpu-on-one-crates-tree).
* **The GPU polygon's shade at BOMB 1910 and LOCATION 2599 and 2859**: all-flat polygons still differ there (section 5); [journal](../../work/2026-11/265_lavapipe-parity/journal.md#2026-11-15-lavapipe-beside-the-gpu-on-one-crates-tree).
* **LLVM shader compilation, or `LP_NUM_THREADS`, as the cost of a lavapipe render** (mesa-dist-win 25.2.8): `GALLIVM_PERF=nopt` (47.5 s against 48.4 s over LOCATION ticks 12-100) and 1, 4 or unset threads left the time and the frames unchanged; [journal](../../work/2026-11/596_lavapipe-staging-buffers/journal.md#2026-11-15-one-model-matrix-upload-per-frame).
* **An upstream fix by Mesa 26.2.6**: its clipper still makes the NaN, and `interp()` is unchanged through `main` (section 2); [journal](../../work/2026-11/265_lavapipe-parity/journal.md#2026-11-15-lavapipe-beside-the-gpu-on-one-crates-tree).

## Open questions

1. Whether the clipper moves the depth of a polygon it breaks (LOCATION 2859, section 5), why another polygon wins at BOMB 1910 and LOCATION 2599, and what moves LAND 227's other 536 pixels ([#595](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/595)).
2. Whether to stop Gouraud polygons drawing flat on lavapipe (section 5): recomputing the weight in the shader (for example interpolating `shade` as `perspective` alongside a `linear` copy and using it only when the `linear` one is not finite), each byte-identical on a GPU only if tested so. Not filed upstream either: a report to Mesa would cite `t_nopersp`'s zero divisor (section 2). ([#595](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/595))
3. Whether Mesa 26.2.6 scores as 25.2.8 does: it was rendered at six ticks only, with no parity run; and CI's lavapipe has not been compared with these rows.
4. Which lavapipe functions hold its command-buffer thread, where most of a 0.28 s LOCATION tick goes after #596, and whether fewer draws or a readback overlapped with the next tick's work shortens it (section 6, [#600](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/600)).
5. Whether caching the world's visible mesh slices while the camera holds still is worth it once readback overlaps the next tick: `GpuModel::collect_world_meshes` depends only on the eye, target and lens, and took 8 of 42 GPU main-thread samples (section 6).
6. Whether a cloud session can reach `gitlab.freedesktop.org`: [`cloud-sessions.md`](../../docs/cloud-sessions.md) lists it in the environment's network access, but no cloud session is recorded reaching it; the reads in section 2 were made from the RTX 4070 laptop. Unchecked.
