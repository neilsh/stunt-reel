# Parity: Hero Aircraft Orientation

`[Bounded Observation]`: `CTRY.FLM` (114 scored frames) and `INTRO.FLM` (209), `mean_ssim_scored`. Covers how actors are rotated and which shapes decode upside down. Position and body are in [`hero-aircraft-placement.md`](hero-aircraft-placement.md), the ground shadow in [`hero-shadow.md`](hero-shadow.md). Method: [`method.md`](method.md); score history: journal entries, and before them the parity log.

## 1. Actor orientation

* **Orientation: actors were drawn upside down** (2026-11-01, `[Bounded Observation]`). Each actor's DOS rotation was converted like the view's, $q_{z180} \cdot q$, but shapes decode already in `[I1]` (+Y up), so every actor drew rolled 180 degrees about Z: upside down and mirrored. Converting actors by the conjugate $q_{z180} \cdot q \cdot q_{z180}^{-1}$ ([`coordinates-and-units.md` § 1.2](../formats/concepts/coordinates-and-units.md#12-right-handed-metric-basis-i1)) stands the `INTRO.FLM` triplane upright with its three wings and gear and gives the `CTRY.FLM` biplane its white fuselage and upper wing: 0.7790 / 0.5376 -> 0.7855 / 0.5208, actor overlap 0.122 / 0.099 -> 0.212 / 0.093; verdict CTRY 73 closer / 5 further, INTRO 39 / 160 (157 in the actors layer). INTRO falls because the now upright, full-size aircraft show the colour the `SHADE` rule still gets wrong (the triplane's fuselage grey where the capture is red) over more pixels, and its camera still sits lower than the capture's at tick 1185. The earlier orientation sweeps (Ruled out) found Y-flip variants best for the same reason, but on the truncated shape they could not show it. Before the fix, `INTRO.FLM` tick 1009 (capture above, ours below): [`intro-1009-upside-down.png`](../../work/2026-11/aircraft-render/images/intro-1009-upside-down.png).
* **Orientation: some aircraft shapes decode upside down** (2026-11-06, `[Bounded Observation]`, fixed; [log row](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md)). After the fix above, the `CTRY.FLM` biplane (`0x7F37`) still draws inverted at ticks 103, 499 and 697 on an RTX 4070 and on lavapipe: the gear and wheels sit above the fuselage, the red rudder hangs below the tail, and there is no cockpit. `INTRO.FLM`'s `0x7F19` does the same at tick 1295, where the capture shows the gear axle below the fuselage and ours shows it above. The bullet's "upright" verdict held for `INTRO`'s triplane; a render of `17a8e94` (#47 itself) shows the CTRY gear already pointing up, so this is not a regression. **Not the actor transform.** The film stores the CTRY slot almost level (`rot_bam` `[62866, 382, 43864]` near tick 499: roll -14 degrees, pitch +2, yaw 241), and `INTRO`'s upright triplane is `[0, 3277, 49152]`; both go through the same `dos_to_i1_actor_quat`. A 180 degree roll on every actor stands CTRY up (0.8725 -> 0.8933) but costs `INTRO` 0.8273 -> 0.7759. FreeFlight `sifilm.cpp` assigns `rot_bam[0]` and `[1]` to X and Z, the reverse of ours, but CTRY's -14 and +2 degrees stay near level under either reading. **The shape data.** `0x7F37` decodes with the wheels at the top of its `[I1]` box (y up to 0.64 m), the rudder at 0.12 to 0.27 m and the wing tips drooping. A side-view census of the 55 stock `0x7Fxx` shapes in the superset (`mise run superset-all`; nose at +z in every one) finds 14 inverted, with the fin, canopy, gondola or gear on the wrong side: biplanes `0x7F19`, `0x7F2A`, `0x7F36`, `0x7F37`, `0x7F38`, jets and transports `0x7F02`, `0x7F0F`, `0x7F10`, `0x7F20`, `0x7F23`, `0x7F27`, `0x7F30`, `0x7F35`, and the blimp `0x7F22` with its gondola on top. `0x7F18` and `0x7F21` are unclear. Upright: `0x7F0A`, `0x7F0D`, `0x7F11`, `0x7F17`, `0x7F1E`, `0x7F1F`, `0x7F26`, `0x7F2B`, `0x7F2C`, and every shape whose display list opens with `OP_IPG` (2,153 stock shapes). All the inverted ones open with `OP_VECTORS` (297 shapes), but so do upright jets, flags, vehicles and buildings, so the first opcode does not decide it. No opcode in the traces separates the two groups, and fitting the ground offset to the lowest point does not either; that fit lands the mirrored `0x7F19` and `0x7F10` exactly on the ground, but is mixed for the large jets, whose gear may be retracted. The closest correlate is in the delta vectors that the display list loads into the top registers (about 485 to 511) and then adds to vertices with `ADDV` and `INTV`. 12 of the 14 inverted shapes carry a negative pure-y delta: (0, -1500, 0) in `0x7F0F`, `0x7F19`, `0x7F22`, `0x7F2A` and `0x7F36`..`0x7F38`, and -800, -859, -216, -558 and -216 in `0x7F10`, `0x7F20`, `0x7F23`, `0x7F30` and `0x7F35`. `0x7F02` and `0x7F27` carry none. No upright shape carries one: they have +720, +1263, +950, +286 or +80 (`0x7F0A`, `0x7F11`, `0x7F1F`, `0x7F26`, `0x7F2C`) or no y delta. The unclear `0x7F18` (-2000) and `0x7F21` (-859) both carry one. These are construction deltas, overwritten later by `PUTV`, and no op reads them as a reference direction. So the sign is evidence of how the model was authored, with -y as up, not a flag the engine reads. `[Hypothesis]`: `PLAYONE` turns these shapes over by a rule not yet found, either per shape or in how it reads their vertices. Neither the corpus notes nor the community decoders (FreeFlight `siobj.cpp`, SODParse) describe a per-shape flip; both use `y + yoff` for every shape. **Sweep** (`DBG_FLIPY`, six shapes picked before the census, `0x7F18`, `0x7F19`, `0x7F2A` and `0x7F36`..`0x7F38`, mirrored at decode as y' = 2 * offset - y, normals and `FacesEye` gates mirrored): 1 reverses the winding as well and scores CTRY 0.8934 and INTRO 0.8327 (from 0.8725 / 0.8273, IoU unchanged), with `0x7F19` matching the capture at `INTRO` tick 1295; 2 keeps the winding, 0.8790 / 0.8329, and back-face culling removes most of the aircraft. Not adopted: the shape list is picked by eye and no `PLAYONE` rule backs it. With variant 1 the CTRY wing stripes (white, index 63) vanish. Their polygons (`DECAL_14` and `BASE_70`, at y = 0) store normals pointing decoded -y, which is up once the model is mirrored, but they are wound to face +y, so the mirrored winding culls them. **Cause** (2026-11-06, [`sod-vm.md`](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md) section 8.7): `vm.rs` turns the `OP_IPG` cube and `TAP` half a turn about z from `PLAYONE`'s raw frame, but stored `OP_VECTORS` data unturned, so the deltas above, and any shape built from `OP_VECTORS` alone, came out upside down. Storing `(−x, −y, z)` there stands every stock aircraft up and turns no other shape over; it scores CTRY 0.8986 and INTRO 0.8337 and keeps the wing stripes, because the winding is no longer reversed. Adopted in `vm.rs`, `[Supported Inference]`.
* The rotation composition is plausible: at tick 426 the capture shows the biplane from the side with wings edge-on and our unrotated render has the same layout (wing span, tail, strut side).

## Ruled out

All scores `mean_ssim_scored`, CTRY / INTRO unless a single film is named. Baselines moved between rounds (0.771 / 0.676 on 2026-10-22, 0.7708 / 0.6762 at `d90a047`, 0.7732 / 0.6799 after page timing); each sweep is read against its own baseline. Nothing below was adopted.

**Aircraft orientation sweeps** (CTRY, 37 frames from tick 10 to 800 for the window scores):
* All 48 signed axis permutations of the model-to-world matrix: whole-film SSIM 0.770 to 0.777 (best two Y-flip variants, 0.777), IoU unchanged 0.856. Whole-film SSIM cannot pick an orientation.
* Aircraft-window IoU (layer 16+ against capture non-sky, non-ground) over the same 48: identity 0.109, best 0.247 (swap model Y and Z, negate new Z), then 0.245, 0.215, 0.214, rest 0.19 or lower. At tick 97 the best is a plan view of a monoplane while the capture is a biplane seen from above and behind at about 45 degrees: the gain is silhouette coverage, not a match.
* Euler angles permuted inside the shared rotation routine broke the camera slots too (0 to 10 scorable frames); invalid. Pre-multiplying actor matrices by the 48 world-axis permutations: best 0.187 (all three signs negated, a reflection), then 0.177, 0.172, 0.171, 0.166; no winner. Axis remaps in either space are exhausted.
* FreeFlight order `T(-x,-y,z) * Rx(rx) * Ry(rz) * Rz(ry)` (`corpus/.../FreeFlight/src/sifilm.cpp`, stored words in file order `rz, rx, ry`), 8 sign variants: 0.100 to 0.124 against 0.109. Every variant keeps FreeFlight's axis assignment, which `PLAYONE`'s builder `0824:04E5` rules out: it turns word 0 about Z and word 2 about Y, as ours does ([`coordinates-and-units.md` § 3.4](../formats/concepts/coordinates-and-units.md#34-rotation-conventions--unit-quaternions-seam-2)). A fixed +90 degree pitch gives 0.2465 at tick 97 (plan view) but is wrong at tick 426 (side view, wings edge-on, matched by the unrotated render). The rotation composition is not the main error; the body is (section 1).
