# Parity: Framing, Camera and the Hero Aircraft Residual

`[Bounded Observation]`: `CTRY.FLM` (114 scored frames) and `INTRO.FLM` (209), `mean_ssim_scored`. Covers the camera, placement and actor-tick corrections tried against the capture, and the one open residual they point at: the `CTRY.FLM` hero aircraft (shape `0x7F37`) and its ground shadow. Method: [`method.md`](method.md); score history: the journal entry each finding cites, and before them the [parity log](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md); where the rest of the loss sits: [`attribution.md`](attribution.md). Scores: CTRY / INTRO unless one film is named, each against its own round's baseline.

## 1. The hero aircraft residual (open)

* **Placement: actors drawn without the shape's ground offset** (adopted, heuristic track, [#67](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/67)). The decoder adds the shape header's `ground_y_offset` to every vertex in model +Y, which stands a world prop on the ground it is placed at; a film actor's position is its model origin, so `actor_world_matrix` in [`scene.rs`](../../crates/stunt-core/src/scene.rs) takes the lift back out along the actor's own +Y. For `0x7F37` the offset is 2624 at scale index 13, 0.32 m, about 10 rows at the hero's usual 80 px wingspan. The lift explains the earlier errors: the hero about 10 rows low while actors drew rolled 180 degrees, 8 to 10 rows high once upright. Taking the offset out of props as well costs CTRY 0.009 (trees and buildings sink into the ground). When adopted, both films and both actor overlaps peaked at exactly one offset removed; since the absolute mesh-index fix INTRO still peaks at one offset (0.7745, against 0.7081 with none) while CTRY prefers half (0.8309 against 0.8298 at one), and its gain at one offset over none, +0.0013, is under the noise floor. It stays at one offset on the user's call (2026-11-03). The `shift` residual after it is dy -4, dx +2 against a world optimum of 0 rows, so ours sits about 4 rows low, which agrees with CTRY preferring half an offset. At CTRY tick 587 what still differs is attitude and shape (the capture's biplane rolled, both striped wings visible; ours flatter, one wing), not position. _Source: [2026-11-02](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-02-actors-drawn-without-the-shapes-ground-offset), [2026-11-02](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-02-the-hero-re-measured-after-47-48-and-132)._
* **The world's framing needs no shift** since the projection change of section 2: capture rows 0-184 resampled as `capture[y + dy]` against the unchanged render peak at 0 rows and 0 columns on both films, and CTRY ±2 rows cost 0.021 and 0.027. Before it the CTRY world sat about 2 rows low (+0.0022, at the noise bound). INTRO's `shift` readings at `794daa5`, before it (indicative only: the probe uses CTRY's background indices), spread -40 to +40 rows. _Source: [2026-11-02](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-02-the-hero-re-measured-after-47-48-and-132)._
* **Body: the full `SORTON` walk** (adopted 2026-11-01). `run_vm` once read `SORTON` (`0x018C`) as a three-word skip and stopped at the first `RETURN`, drawing only the first subtree: for `0x7F37` (7,604 bytes) 36 polygons in colours 136, 47 and 0, a 4 to 6 px grey sliver where the capture shows a white and blue fuselage about 25 px deep; `INTRO.FLM`'s triplane `0x7F04` drew a flat black prefix. `CTRY.ALL` holds a single `7Fxx` chunk, so no other aircraft LOD exists to bind. The walk as FreeFlight `siobj.cpp` reads it (words are byte offsets from the opcode: inline subtree, then `op + 4 + w2`, then continue at `op + 2 + w1`), with the centre stack, `GOSUB`/`GOTO`, the relative-matrix stack, `ROTX`/`ROTY`/`ROTZ` and `BVGT`, decodes `0x7F37` to its end (76 -> 140 polygons; the `0x15FC` section is tail, gear and nose) and the `INTRO.FLM` aircraft `0x7F09`, `0x7F19` and `0x7F1A`, which had stopped on `BVGT` or `PUSHCENTRE`. Adopted together with the `SHADE` colour rule ([`sod-vm.md` § 8.5](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#85-polygon-colour-at-shade-time)), which turns the triplane's black faces red, it scored 0.7790 / 0.5376 against 0.7808 / 0.5488 (actor overlap 0.091 / 0.062 -> 0.122 / 0.099), failing the plan's score gate, and was adopted on the user's call that a drawn but miscoloured body beats a missing one, and on the readout: `INTRO` `aircraft-absent` 9 -> 0 of 12 sheets, `CTRY` `aircraft-shape-broken` 10 -> 3. At adoption the fuselage panels were painted 136 or 23 where the capture uses the grey ramp 48..63; the colour questions still open are in [`sod-vm.md` § 8.5](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#85-polygon-colour-at-shade-time). _Source: [2026-11-01](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-01-sorton-subtrees-the-full-walk-and-shade-colour), [2026-10-22](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-10-22-the-heros-position-body-and-rotation-at-d90a047)._
* **Orientation: actors take the conjugate rotation** (`[Bounded Observation]`). Shapes decode already in `[I1]` (+Y up), so an actor's DOS rotation converts by $q_{z180} \cdot q \cdot q_{z180}^{-1}$ ([`coordinates-and-units.md` § 1.2](../formats/concepts/coordinates-and-units.md#12-right-handed-metric-basis-i1)), not by the view's $q_{z180} \cdot q$, under which every actor drew rolled 180 degrees, upside down and mirrored. The change scored 0.7790 / 0.5376 -> 0.7855 / 0.5208, actor overlap 0.122 / 0.099 -> 0.212 / 0.093; INTRO fell because the upright, full-size aircraft show the colour the `SHADE` rule still got wrong over more pixels, and its camera sat lower than the capture's at tick 1185. Before it, `INTRO.FLM` tick 1009: [`intro-1009-upside-down.png`](../../work/2026-11/aircraft-render/images/intro-1009-upside-down.png). _Source: [2026-11-01](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-01-actors-were-drawn-upside-down)._
* **Orientation: `OP_VECTORS` is turned like the cube** (`[Supported Inference]`, [`sod-vm.md`](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md) section 8.7). `vm.rs` turns the `OP_IPG` cube and `TAP` half a turn about z from `PLAYONE`'s raw frame, but stored `OP_VECTORS` data unturned, so shapes that add `OP_VECTORS` deltas to cube corners, or are built from `OP_VECTORS` alone, decoded upside down. A side-view census of the 55 stock `0x7Fxx` shapes found 14 inverted (biplanes `0x7F19`, `0x7F2A`, `0x7F36`..`0x7F38`, jets and transports `0x7F02`, `0x7F0F`, `0x7F10`, `0x7F20`, `0x7F23`, `0x7F27`, `0x7F30`, `0x7F35`, the blimp `0x7F22`), all opening with `OP_VECTORS`, as do upright shapes; 12 of the 14 carry a negative pure-y construction delta and no upright shape does, which is evidence of authoring with -y up, not a flag the engine reads. Storing `(−x, −y, z)` stands every stock aircraft up and turns no other shape over; it scored CTRY 0.8986 and INTRO 0.8337 (from 0.8725 / 0.8273) and keeps the CTRY wing stripes. Not the actor rotation: the CTRY slot is stored almost level, and the same conversion stands `INTRO`'s triplane upright. _Source: [2026-11-06](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-06-some-aircraft-shapes-decoded-upside-down)._
* **The rotation composition is plausible**: at tick 426 the capture's side view of the biplane, wings edge-on, has our unrotated render's layout (span, tail, strut side). _Source: [2026-10-22](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-10-22-the-heros-position-body-and-rotation-at-d90a047)._
* **Mesh indices are absolute** (`[Bounded Observation]`). `stunt-core` stores a mesh node's indices into the shape's whole vertex buffer (`ShapeMesh::indices`), so [`plan_upload`](../../crates/stunt-render/src/mesh.rs) draws every node of an unsplit model from base vertex 0; it had passed each node's `vertex_offset` as the base vertex, so every node after a shape's first (in `0x7F37`, its three decal meshes) drew spikes and fragments. It scored 0.8285 / 0.7081 (from 0.8291 / 0.7154) and was adopted as a contract fix, not a parity candidate: INTRO falls because the whole aircraft paints more pixels at the position and colour errors above. _Source: [2026-11-03](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-03-every-mesh-after-a-shapes-first-drew-the-wrong-vertices)._
* **Ground shadow: the engine draws the shape again, flattened** ([#99](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/99); full reading in [`sod-vm.md` § 9.1](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#91-the-actor-shadow-draw)). The capture draws a black monoplane-shaped silhouette on the ground below the aircraft (`[Hypothesis]`: from above, the biplane's two wings overlap into one outline; first measure: present in 83 of the 114 scored CTRY frames, median offset from the actor centroid dx -29, dy +41 px). `[Bounded Observation]` that the hero shape holds no shadow: chunk `0x7F37` has no `0x0171` (`OP_AIRBRUSH`) word. `[Established]` (disassembled, not run) in `build/re/playone.img`: the actor loop at `0x1E07..0x1E3A` tests each slot's flag byte `[si+1]` for bits 1, 0 and 2, draws the actor with `0x1A90` (skipped for the view camera's slot `[0x22DE]` at `0x1E26`), and when bit 2 is set calls `0x19FC` at `0x1E35`, which finds the ground plane under the actor's x and z (`y = 0` for a null pointer at slot `+0x1E`) and queues a second draw of the same shape there. `0x1C0D` flattens that draw's matrix onto the plane (it replaces the Y row), and with `work:0x806C` set to `0xFF` every polygon draws flat in palette index 0 and `OP_ANIMATE` is skipped. `[Hypothesis]` for what these sequences draw at run time: the draw was read, not observed. `[Hypothesis]` that bits 1, 0 and 2 are `SIF_DRAW`, `SIF_EXISTS` and `SIF_SHADOW` (question 11). _Source: [2026-11-05](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-05-the-ground-shadows-source-narrowed-and-drawn), [2026-11-02](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-02-the-ground-shadow-measured)._
* **Ground shadow: drawn** (adopted, evidence-backed, [#206](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/206)). `casts_shadow` (create flag `SIF_SHADOW`) rides on `ActorTransform`; the scene frame gives each such actor a shadow matrix, the actor matrix flattened straight down onto the plane of the terrain triangle under its origin (`y = 0` off the map) and lifted the `[I5]` 1 mm, and the renderer draws the shape again through it, every polygon index 0, in the decal pass, through a pipeline that culls nothing. One plane under the origin, not the terrain under each vertex, because `0x1C0D` rewrites the matrix once. No culling, because a face flattened onto the ground keeps its winding, so culling by screen winding dropped every face wound downward (INTRO tick 1009 drew specks where the capture's shadow is solid); that choice rests on the capture alone (heuristic track). Scores: 0.8642 / 0.8166 -> 0.8722 / 0.8217 for the shadow, then 0.8725 / 0.8273 without culling, IoU unchanged; at CTRY tick 444 the shadow matches the capture's in shape and place. A lift of 1, 3 or 10 cm scored 0.8738 / 0.8214, 0.8739 / 0.8187 and 0.8667 / 0.8144; 1 cm is within the noise floor and was not adopted. Questions 6 and 8 stay open, below. _Source: [2026-11-05](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-05-the-ground-shadows-source-narrowed-and-drawn)._

## 2. Projection: square raster pixels, focal length 256 (adopted)

`[Supported Inference]`. `PLAYONE.EXE` projects with square pixels on the 320 x 200 raster and a focal length of 256 raster pixels at the default lens, so horizontal and vertical focal lengths are equal; the CRT's 1.2x vertical stretch comes after projection. The vertical field of view is `2 * atan(100 / 256)` = 42.67 degrees ([`PLAYONE_FOV_Y_DEGREES`](../../crates/stunt-core/src/contracts/trajectory.rs)) at aspect 320 / 200 ([`MODE13H_PROJECTION_ASPECT`](../../crates/stunt-render/src/camera.rs)), replacing a 4:3 projection at 45 degrees (241.4 px vertically, 289.7 px horizontally). 256 matches the `x 256` screen slopes `0x22A0`/`0x22A2` ([`lod.md`](../playone-exe/lod.md)), but the rule itself has not been read from the disassembly. _Source: [2026-11-02](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-02-square-raster-pixels-focal-length-256)._

* **The horizon fit.** In the 49 CTRY and 23 INTRO scored frames where the sky/ground boundary is visible, the old projection's horizon error (capture minus ours at column 160: -8.4 px CTRY slot 3, -4.9 CTRY slot 2, -5.0 INTRO slot 2, -10.8 INTRO slot 4) followed the camera's pitch (R² 0.94), not its altitude (R² 0.10), heading or roll, and the capture's horizon slope was -1.006 tan(roll) against our -0.833 tan(roll), the 4:3 pixel aspect. A projection fit to the capture's horizon gives `fy` 256 and centre row 91.9, against the live `0x41CA` = 92, rms error 0.39 px; composing the three angles in any other order fits worse (0.71 and 1.31 px), so our Euler order and roll sign stand.
* **The score.** Square 256 px scored 0.8274 / 0.7155 against 0.7843 / 0.6902 in the sweep; each axis's scale alone gave about half the gain, and moving either by about 3% scored lower on both films. Adopted at 0.8291 / 0.7154, IoU 0.9897 / 0.9712.

## Ruled out

* A montage tick-skipping rule: the best-matching render tick follows the capture tick at slope 0.99 ([#73](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/73)). _Source: [2026-11-05](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-05-no-montage-tick-skipping-rule)._
* The shadow as geometry or an airbrush op inside the hero shape. _Source: [2026-11-05](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-05-the-ground-shadows-source-narrowed-and-drawn)._
* A global vertical correction (camera eye height, camera pitch, terrain `base_y`, a hero lift, a second `ground_y_offset` per shape): none helped both films. _Source: [2026-10-22](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-10-22-vertical-placement-sweeps)._
* An eye-height offset per view slot: no offset is shared by one slot across films or by the slots of one film; a height offset was partly compensating the old projection's pitch-dependent error. _Source: [2026-11-01](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-01-eye-height-per-view-slot)._
* A field-of-view scale, a single rigid framing offset, and a camera or actor tick lag. _Source: [2026-10-23](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-10-23-field-of-view-a-single-framing-offset-and-tick-phase)._
* A pitch offset on top of the square projection: every offset tried (-0.9, -0.4, +0.4, +0.9 degrees) cost 0.02 to 0.05. _Source: [2026-11-02](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-02-square-raster-pixels-focal-length-256)._
* Remapping the actor's axes (48 signed permutations in model or world space, permuted Euler angles, FreeFlight's `sifilm.cpp` order with 8 sign variants); `PLAYONE`'s builder `0824:04E5` rules out FreeFlight's axis assignment. _Source: [2026-10-23](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-10-23-aircraft-orientation-sweeps)._
* A 180 degree roll on every actor, and a per-shape Y mirror (`DBG_FLIPY`) on hand-picked shapes: the first costs INTRO, the second has no `PLAYONE` rule behind its shape list. _Source: [2026-11-06](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-06-some-aircraft-shapes-decoded-upside-down)._
* The shadow as a black copy flattened onto a horizontal plane at a guessed height. _Source: [2026-10-22](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-10-22-ground-shadow-at-a-constant-height)._
* The shadow sheared onto the ground along a sun direction, and every world prop moved into the biased decal pass (`DBG_PROP_DECAL`, an artifact of the then-positive bias). _Source: [2026-10-23](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-10-23-ground-shadow-as-a-flattened-copy-props-in-the-decal-pass)._
* `SORTON` subtrees drawn with their colour undecoded (black), and, as the thin body's cause, back-face culling, the `LodDistanceSq(1000)` node (`IFVIS` face 1, one wing triangle), the two-sided flag, and colour from the high byte of the `GVPOLY16` trailing word. _Source: [2026-11-01](../../work/2026-11/distill-framing-and-actors/journal.md#2026-11-01-sorton-subtrees-the-full-walk-and-shade-colour)._

6. The ground plane for the shadow (the score cannot settle it: under every sampled actor on both films the terrain is level at `y = 0`, so the terrain plane and a null plane draw the same; sampled at CTRY ticks 100, 444, 895 and INTRO 48, 1009, 3306, with no per-frame comparison; [#206](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/206)): `0x19FC` solves the plane read through the pointer at slot `+0x1E` at the actor's x and z ([`sod-vm.md` § 9.1](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#91-the-actor-shadow-draw)). Who writes slot `+0x1E`, and whether it is the terrain leaf polygon the ground draw uses, is untraced ([#99](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/99)).
7. Slot `+0x20/+0x22` is a precomputed shadow height that `0x19FC` uses when `+0x22` is nonzero. Its writer and when it is set (an object resting on a surface?) are untraced.
8. The handbook says an airborne object's shadow appears only when it is close to the ground. `0x19FC` shows no altitude test; the cutoff may be in the cull `0x1B0C` or the ground-plane lookup. On both films a cutoff anywhere from 3 to 30 m scores the same as none, and 0.01 m (no shadow) gives back the old scores, so all of the gain comes from an actor within 3 m of the ground ([#206](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/206)), so the score cannot place it; a live probe of a high-altitude `INTRO.FLM` frame would settle it.
9. Recorded as settled 2026-11-05: the sort at `0x1B88` paints every shadow before every actor; our depth test keeps the actor in front of its shadow. [`sod-vm.md` § 9.1](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#91-the-actor-shadow-draw) still lists the sort direction at `0x1B9F` as open.
10. Answered 2026-11-06: `[0x22DE]` holds the view camera's slot record ([`memory-map.md`](../playone-exe/memory-map.md) § 1). `0x1E26` skips only its actor draw; the two view slots read live on `INTRO.FLM` (slot 1 at ticks 2678-2766, slot 4 at 2801) had flag byte `0x19` and shape 0, so neither reaches the shadow queue (`[Bounded Observation]`, [`sod-vm.md` § 9.1](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#91-the-actor-shadow-draw)). Whether our renderer should draw one stays open.
11. Whether slot byte `[si+1]` is the create `type_flags`: the create handler that writes it is untraced, so `SIF_SHADOW` as bit 2 rests on the bit order and the flag census (slot 0 of both films carries `0x04`, camera rigs do not).
