# Parity: Framing, Camera and the Hero Aircraft Residual

`[Bounded Observation]`: `CTRY.FLM` (114 scored frames) and `INTRO.FLM` (209), `mean_ssim_scored`. Covers every camera, placement and actor-tick correction tried against the capture, and the one open residual they point at: the `CTRY.FLM` hero aircraft (shape `0x7F37`) and its ground shadow. Method: [`method.md`](method.md); score history: [`log.md`](log.md); where the rest of the loss sits: [`attribution.md`](attribution.md).

## 1. The hero aircraft residual (open)

* **Position** (before the orientation fix; superseded by the ground-offset bullet below). Over all 114 scored CTRY frames the silhouette probe gives a best shift of dy -6 to -14 (mostly -10) and dx 0 to +16 (mostly +8 to +14), hit fractions 0.56 to 0.91. The world's own optimum is about -4 rows, so the residual is actor-specific: the hero sits left and low relative to the terrain. Earlier measurement (ticks 10 to 800): best vertical shift -10 to -14 rows while the aircraft stays about 100 px wide; the model is 2.8 m across, so 10 rows is about 0.3 m, close to its `ground_y_offset` of 0.32 m. At tick 448 the wingspan matches (113 px against 101 px) but ours sits about 13 rows lower (rows 90 to 121 against 76 to 106). INTRO shows -10 rows in several frames and -30 to -40 in close-ups, so it is not one rule.
* **Body.** Shape `0x7F37` (7604 bytes) reaches `RETURN` at `0x11F2` once SHADE16's two-word operand is decoded, so the thin body is not an unimplemented opcode. It decodes to 388 vertices and 195 triangles in four nodes (base mesh plus three decal meshes, one gated at `LodDistanceSq(1000)`); the opcode stream is 36 polygons (20 `POLYZ`, 16 `GVPOLY`), `ground_y_offset` 2624 at scale index 13 (0.32 m). Triangles by palette index: 0 -> 17, 47 -> 10, 56 -> 104, 74 -> 24, 136 -> 40. Index 47 (162,154,154) has 0.28 m2 total; index 56 (210,206,206) lies inside a 0.22 x 0.06 x 0.50 m box around the cockpit, which is detail, not a body; index 136 (28,40,97) holds 3.37 m2 and spans the wings. The capture shows a thick white and blue tapered fuselage about 25 px deep with striped wings (blue 136 alternating with white 63 and neighbours); ours is a 4 to 6 px grey sliver, wings 136 and 47 only. At tick 273 the capture has 2,603 object pixels there, our layer plane 1,287, overlap 410. `CTRY.ALL` holds a single `7Fxx` chunk, so there is no other aircraft LOD to bind. `[Hypothesis]`: a decode path we do not model (the actor matrix, or a polygon path in `PLAYONE.EXE`, for example one-sided or dropped fuselage faces or a `GVPOLY16` path) supplies the body.
* **Body: BSP subtrees past the first `RETURN`** (2026-11-01, `[Bounded Observation]`). `run_vm` reads `SORTON` (`0x018C`) as a three-word skip and ends the shape at its first `RETURN`, so only the first subtree of a `SORTON` tree is drawn. In `0x7F37` that first list (`0x0E28`..`0x11F0`) is all 36 polygons, colours 136, 47 and 0 only; the remaining 7.6 KB (`0x11F2`..`0x1DB4`) holds 60 more polygons in `RETURN`-terminated subtrees, the only `FILLCOL 63` (white) words, and from `0x15FC` a centre-stack section (`PUSHCENTRE`). `INTRO.FLM`'s triplane `0x7F04` (slot 0 at tick 1009), vehicle `0x4150` and figure `0x3A26` are cut the same way (`returned_at` 4854 of 12,566, 3328 of 5012, 2298 of 3408 bytes) and report no stop, so they look complete. The flat black shape INTRO draws in the triplane's place is that truncated prefix: it carries no `OP_COLOR`, so every polygon is index 0. Following FreeFlight `siobj.cpp` `case 0x18c` (words are byte offsets from the opcode: inline subtree, then `op + 4 + w2`, then continue at `op + 2 + w1`) raises the triangle counts to 130, 351 and 120 at tick 1009, but most of the added faces are still black (98 of 130 for `0x7F04`) because the `SHADE16`/`GVPOLY16` colour rule is not decoded, and `0x7F37` still stops at `PUSHCENTRE`. Round 1 alone is in Ruled out.
* **Adopted: the full `SORTON` walk, the centre stack and `SHADE` colour** (2026-11-01, [log row](log.md)). On top of the walk, the centre stack, `GOSUB`/`GOTO`, the relative-matrix stack, `ROTX`/`ROTY`/`ROTZ` and `BVGT` as FreeFlight reads them decode `0x7F37` to the end (76 -> 140 polygons; its `0x15FC` section is the tail, gear and nose) and three `INTRO.FLM` aircraft that stopped on `BVGT` or `PUSHCENTRE` (`0x7F09`, `0x7F19`, `0x7F1A`); the `SHADE` rule ([`sod-vm.md` § 8.5](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#85-polygon-colour-at-shade-time)) turns the triplane's black faces red. Scores 0.7790 / 0.5376 (from 0.7808 / 0.5488), actor overlap 0.122 / 0.099 (from 0.091 / 0.062); verdict CTRY 45 closer / 75 further, all in the actors layer, INTRO 20 / 167, 164 in actors and three props-only frames (876, 888, 2125: `0x7F04`, `0x3A26`, `0x4150` 685 to 1280 m away, 2 to 7 px; at 876 the added pixels cover hill in the capture). That fails the plan's score gate; it was adopted on the user's call that a drawn but miscoloured body beats a missing one, and on the readout: `INTRO` `aircraft-absent` 9 -> 0 of 12 (now parts missing 11, shape broken 9), `CTRY` `aircraft-shape-broken` 10 -> 3. What remains: the fuselage panels are drawn but painted 136 or 23 where the capture uses the grey ramp 48..63 (the open `GVPOLY16` colour path), and the `SHADE` light vector is a guess.
* **Orientation: actors were drawn upside down** (2026-11-01, `[Bounded Observation]`). Each actor's DOS rotation was converted like the view's, $q_{z180} \cdot q$, but shapes decode already in `[I1]` (+Y up), so every actor drew rolled 180 degrees about Z: upside down and mirrored. Converting actors by the conjugate $q_{z180} \cdot q \cdot q_{z180}^{-1}$ ([`coordinates-and-units.md` § 1.2](../formats/concepts/coordinates-and-units.md#12-right-handed-metric-basis-i1)) stands the `INTRO.FLM` triplane upright with its three wings and gear and gives the `CTRY.FLM` biplane its white fuselage and upper wing: 0.7790 / 0.5376 -> 0.7855 / 0.5208, actor overlap 0.122 / 0.099 -> 0.212 / 0.093; verdict CTRY 73 closer / 5 further, INTRO 39 / 160 (157 in the actors layer). INTRO falls because the now upright, full-size aircraft show the colour the `SHADE` rule still gets wrong (the triplane's fuselage grey where the capture is red) over more pixels, and its camera still sits lower than the capture's at tick 1185. The earlier orientation sweeps (Ruled out) found Y-flip variants best for the same reason, but on the truncated shape they could not show it. Before the fix, `INTRO.FLM` tick 1009 (capture above, ours below): [`aircraft/intro-1009-upside-down.png`](aircraft/intro-1009-upside-down.png).
* **Adopted: actors drawn without the shape's ground offset** (2026-11-02, `[Bounded Observation]`, heuristic, [log row](log.md), [#67](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/67)). Re-measured after the orientation fix, the `shift` probe put the hero 8 rows *high* (median dy +8, mostly +8 to +10, dx median -2, 74 frames of at least 300 actor pixels): the pre-orientation -10 had flipped sign with the actor. That is the header's `ground_y_offset` (2624 at scale index 13, 0.32 m, about 10 rows at the hero's usual 80 px wingspan): the decoder adds it to every vertex in model +Y, which stands a world prop on the ground it is placed at, but a film actor's position is its model origin. While actors drew rolled 180 degrees the same lift pushed them 10 rows low. Sweeps, CTRY / INTRO `mean_ssim_scored` (actor overlap): the lift on every shape scaled by k, k = 1 0.7843 / 0.6902 (0.227 / 0.093), k = 0 0.7781 / 0.7287 (0.353 / 0.248), k = -1 0.7706 / 0.7143; actors only, the lift taken back out along the actor's own +Y by a fraction d, d = -0.5 0.7859 / 0.7073 (0.337 / 0.137), **d = -1 0.7871 / 0.7264 (0.353 / 0.248)**, d = -1.5 0.7826 / 0.7141, d = -2 0.7752 / 0.7108. Both films and both overlaps peak at exactly one offset removed, and only for actors: removing it from props as well costs CTRY 0.009 (trees and buildings sink into the ground). `actor_world_matrix` in [`scene.rs`](../../crates/stunt-core/src/scene.rs) now does this. Verdict CTRY 53 closer / 28 further (worst tick 829, the crash close-up, where the struck column moves a few rows), INTRO 184 / 19. Residual `shift` after the fix: dy median -2 (mostly -2 and 0), dx median +2, against the world's own optimum of about -4 rows (Ruled out, single framing offset): the hero's placement residual is gone to within the world's. Rescored on top of the square-pixel 256 px projection ([log row](log.md), section 2): fractions 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 give 0.8291 / 0.8313 / **0.8322** / 0.8286 on CTRY and 0.7154 / 0.7372 / **0.7693** / 0.7432 on INTRO, so the projection change leaves the optimum at one offset and the `shift` residual at dy -2, dx +2. `INTRO.FLM` tick 1009, capture, then ours with the offset, then ours without it (the triplane's wheels come down to the ground): [`aircraft/intro-1009-ground-offset.png`](aircraft/intro-1009-ground-offset.png). Rescored again after the absolute mesh-index fix ([log row](log.md)): 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 give 0.8285 / **0.8309** / 0.8298 / 0.8254 on CTRY and 0.7081 / 0.7317 / **0.7745** / 0.7407 on INTRO. INTRO still peaks at one offset, but the CTRY gain at one offset is +0.0013, under the noise floor, and CTRY prefers half; the `shift` residual is dy -4, dx +2, the world's own. Kept at one offset on the user's call (2026-11-03): INTRO's gain is large and the CTRY change sits inside the noise floor rather than against it. What the capture still shows differently at tick 587 is attitude and shape (the capture's biplane is rolled with both striped wings visible; ours is flatter with one wing), not position. Not yet read out of `PLAYONE.EXE`: where it consumes header word `+4`, and whether it applies it only to ground-placed objects ([`memory-map.md`](../playone-exe/memory-map.md) `DS:0x4778`/`DS:0x48B6` per-slot records are the place to start).
* **Every mesh after a shape's first drew the wrong vertices** (2026-11-03, `[Bounded Observation]`). `stunt-core` stores a mesh node's indices absolute, into the shape's whole vertex buffer (`tessellate.rs`, `ShapeMesh::indices`), but [`plan_upload`](../../crates/stunt-render/src/mesh.rs) passed each node's `vertex_offset` as the `draw_indexed` base vertex, so every node after the first read vertices `vertex_offset` too far on. A multi-node shape such as `0x7F37` (base mesh plus three decals) lost those nodes to spikes and fragments: at `INTRO.FLM` tick 1141 the old render shows the biplane's fuselage missing and stray triangles, the fixed one draws the whole aircraft. Drawing from base vertex 0 scores 0.8285 / 0.7081 (from 0.8291 / 0.7154; IoU 0.9897 / 0.9692 from 0.9897 / 0.9712). Both tracks of the adoption rule reject that, but this is a contract fix, not a parity candidate: the old draw read vertices no shape names. INTRO falls because the now-whole aircraft paints more pixels at the position and colour errors above (worst movers 1130 to 1152 and 2422 to 2676, -0.02 to -0.03 each; 177 of 346 frames move).
* **Ground shadow.** The capture draws a black monoplane-shaped silhouette on the ground below the aircraft (the aircraft is a biplane; only one actor draw exists at tick 448). 65,982 black pixels over the 114 scored frames (up to 1,588 in one, present in 83), offset from the actor centroid by median dx -29, dy +41 px. Nothing in our renderer draws it; its source (flattened projection, an `OP_ANIMATE` image resource, other) is `[Unknown]`. A flattened copy does not reproduce it (see Ruled out).
* The rotation composition is plausible: at tick 426 the capture shows the biplane from the side with wings edge-on and our unrotated render has the same layout (wing span, tail, strut side).

## 2. Projection: square raster pixels, focal length 256 (adopted)

`[Supported Inference]`, 2026-11-02. `PLAYONE.EXE` projects with square pixels on the 320 x 200 raster and a focal length of 256 raster pixels at the default lens, so horizontal and vertical focal lengths are equal; the CRT's 1.2x vertical stretch comes after projection. We had projected at 4:3 with a 45 degree vertical field of view: 241.4 px vertically and 289.7 px horizontally. The vertical field of view is now `2 * atan(100 / 256)` = 42.67 degrees ([`PLAYONE_FOV_Y_DEGREES`](../../crates/stunt-core/src/contracts/trajectory.rs)) at aspect 320 / 200 ([`MODE13H_PROJECTION_ASPECT`](../../crates/stunt-render/src/camera.rs)). 256 matches the `x 256` screen slopes `0x22A0`/`0x22A2` ([`lod.md`](../playone-exe/lod.md)), but the rule itself has not been read from the disassembly.

* **Horizon probe.** The sky/ground boundary was fitted column by column in every scored frame where it is visible: 49 CTRY and 23 INTRO frames. In the rest, mountains or framing hide it. The probe then compared that boundary with ours at the same tick. Capture minus ours at column 160 was -8.4 px (CTRY slot 3), -4.9 (CTRY slot 2), -5.0 (INTRO slot 2) and -10.8 (INTRO slot 4, eye at 500 m). It follows the camera's pitch (R² 0.94) and not its altitude (R² 0.10, coefficient about 0), heading or roll. The capture's horizon slope is -1.006 tan(roll) against our -0.833 tan(roll), which is the 4:3 pixel aspect. A projection fit to the capture's horizon gives `fy` 256 and centre row 91.9, against the live `0x41CA` = 92, with an rms error of 0.39 px. Composing the three angles in any other order fits worse (0.71 and 1.31 px), so our Euler order and roll sign stand. PLAYONE's horizon sits at infinity. Ours is the edge of finite ground, about 5 px low at a 500 m eye height, which is about half of the INTRO slot 4 offset and is still open.
* **Sweep** (`DBG_PROJ_X`, `DBG_PROJ_Y` scaling our focal lengths; `DBG_CAM_PITCH` in degrees). Square 256 px (x 0.884, y 1.0605) scored 0.8274 / 0.7155 against 0.7843 / 0.6902. Each scale alone gave about half the gain: x 0.884 alone 0.7986 / 0.7068, y 1.0605 alone 0.7934 / 0.6893. Moving either scale by about 3% scored lower on both films. The fit also suggested a -0.9 degree pitch offset, but every offset tried (-0.9, -0.4, +0.4, +0.9 degrees) cost 0.02 to 0.05, so none was adopted. Adopted at 0.8291 / 0.7154, IoU 0.9897 / 0.9712.

This is why the eye-height sweeps below found no consistent answer: a height offset was partly compensating for a projection error that changes with pitch, so its best value depended on the shot.

## Open questions

1. The hero's vertical residual was the shape's `ground_y_offset` applied to actors (section 1, adopted 2026-11-02); after it the `shift` residual (dy -2, dx +2) is within the world's own. Still open: the `PLAYONE.EXE` rule behind it (where header word `+4` is read, and for which objects), which would move the change from the heuristic to the evidence-backed track, and the hero's attitude at ticks like 587. The fuselage question is answered in section 1 (`SORTON` subtrees); what it still needs is the colour work open in [`sod-vm.md` § 8.5](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#85-polygon-colour-at-shade-time): the `SHADE` light vector and per-vertex shade interpolation. ([#67](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/67))
2. `INTRO.FLM` montage segments need the player's tick-skipping rule: their captures best-match our state at a lag that grows about 0.9 ticks per clock tick, the captures are all distinct, and no fixed page offset matches. ([#73](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/73))
3. The montage's parked aircraft (four large airframes on a sand flat in the capture) are not where our slot data puts the actors; the placement rule in (1) may explain both. ([#72](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/72))
4. The coastline band (capture sand where we draw airfield green) remains; see [`attribution.md`](attribution.md#5-coastline-and-sea). ([#71](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/71))
5. Two things the projection change of § 2 leaves open. First, the projection centre: our 185-row viewport centres it at row 92.5, while the horizon fit gives 91.9 and `0x41CA` reads 92; half a row has not been swept. Second, the finite ground: at a 500 m eye height our sea edge sits about 5 px below PLAYONE's horizon, which is at infinity. That accounts for about half of the INTRO slot 4 offset.
