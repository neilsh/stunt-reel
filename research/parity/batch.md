# Batch Parity Survey: Every Distinct Corpus Film

`[Bounded Observation]`, interim, 2026-10-30. A survey in progress, not a result: the numbers below are the ledger at the time of writing (72 of 529 distinct films scored) and the tables are regenerated by `--summary`. The plan is [`docs/process/plans/batch-parity-2026-10-30.md`](../../docs/process/plans/batch-parity-2026-10-30.md).

## 1. How to Run

```
mise run batch-parity                       # resumable: finished stages are skipped
mise run batch-parity -- --only SLUG,...    # named films
mise run batch-parity -- --limit N          # the first N films by slug
mise run batch-parity -- --retry-errors     # rerun stages whose latest record is an error
mise run batch-parity -- --summary          # counts, failing films, lowest and highest SSIM
```

Each distinct film (579 `.FLM` files, 529 after de-duplication by sha256, [`batch_films.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/batch_films.py)) goes through four stages ([`batch_parity.py`](../../python/scripts/batch_parity.py), [`batch_stages.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/batch_stages.py)): `archive` (`MAKEONE` through `make_all`; skipped when a sibling `.ALL` exists, 260 films), `capture` (`capture_playone_reference`), `render` (`stuntreel --headless`) and `score` (`stuntreel parity`). The stages stop at a film's first error. The append-only ledger is `build/parity/batch/ledger.jsonl` ([`batch_ledger.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/batch_ledger.py)), one record per stage run (`slug`, `stage`, `status`, `message`, `seconds`, `ssim`, `iou`); the latest record per slug and stage wins. Per film, `build/parity/batch/<slug>/report/` holds `report.json` and the contact sheets, copied to `frames/`; the rendered frames are deleted after a successful score because they run to 2 to 5 GB per film. Nothing here touches `references.toml`: the survey scores whatever capture it makes and pins nothing.

## 2. Interim Results

72 films scored; 35 archives generated, 12 archive errors, 11 capture errors, 1 render error (ledger of 2026-10-30).

* **Archive timeouts.** Every archive error is `exit 124: timeout after 900s` under the original `cycles = 8000` conf, on films of 323 KB or more with no sibling `.ALL` (`bondo-bondo`, `desperat-part1` to `part3`, `eagle-eagle`, `flmonly-act1`, …). `MAKEONE` plays the film in real time, so these were long films, not hangs. Under `core = dynamic` and `cycles = max` the same work takes seconds ([`../makeone-exe/driving.md`](../makeone-exe/driving.md) § 8); these films are being retried.
* **Orphaned emulators.** A timeout killed only the direct Python child on Windows, so each left a `dosbox-x.exe` and a 12 MB stage directory. The runner now kills the process tree and `make_all` gets a 90 s head start on its own timeout.
* **Capture stalls.** `no new frame after 2001 trap hits (0 frame(s) captured, 6 indices on the page)` after the dialog settled: `amcrisis-ac`, `collage4-exit`, `collage4-exit2`, `egalloc-egalloc0`, `egalloc-egalloc5`, `flmonly-bigman`. The film never started; cause unknown. Each costs about 207 s before giving up. The driver now skips them by default (`--skip` adds slugs, `--only` or `--retry-errors` runs them), recording a `skipped` capture record, and `--capture-frames K` (default 40, 0 for every frame) samples K frames per film; that bounds output size and dumps, and run time is expected to track film length.
* **Disk full.** The first full run died after 12 films (`WinError 112`): nothing deleted the render output. Fixed; see § 1.
* **Scores.** Median about 0.66; the best is `vault-balloon` at 0.9236, whose report shows IoU 0.0 and index agreement 0.0 over 3 frames, so it is a degenerate capture, not a pass. Films scoring below 0.05 (`collage4-elbarto` 0.0042, `flmonly-act5` 0.0071, `egalloc-egallo10` 0.0114, `flmonly-act1` 0.0196) had one scored frame with IoU about 0.83 and index agreement near 0.
* **Suspect captures.** The survey capture `vault-balloon/frames/002` holds 3 frames, all at `film_tick` 0, the stuck-clock signature that `nosound` reproduces ([`../playone-exe/capture.md`](../playone-exe/capture.md) § 6). Scores from such captures are not parity evidence.

## 3. Cost

At the original settings, archive generation was 44% of wall time and capture 52% (render and score 4%); timeouts and failed captures were 37.5% of the time. Capture time is linear in frames (0.25 s per frame, median 200 s per film, 4 s of boot) and no DOSBox-X setting changed it, so serial capture over 529 films is about 36 hours. DOSBox-X instances can run concurrently (each binds its own port), but tick lag was worse with two at once ([`../playone-exe/capture.md`](../playone-exe/capture.md) § 6).

## Ruled out

- **De-duplicating by name.** The same film appears under several folders (`NewPLAY`, `OldPLAY`, `Morganroth`); only the content hash identifies it.
- **Keeping the rendered frames.** Gigabytes per film; the report and contact sheets are kept.

## Open questions

1. Reject captures whose clock never advances, or with fewer than a handful of frames, before scoring; count how many of the scored films are affected.
2. Why do the six stalled films never start (`6 distinct indices` at the dialog)? A wrong list selection by first letter is the first suspect.
3. A scored single-frame film with IoU 0.83 and index agreement 0.001 suggests a palette or index-plane mismatch; look at one report's index planes.
4. Parallel whole-film jobs: add `--jobs N` with a ledger lock and a free-disk guard once a quiet-machine run shows tick lag stays within 0 to 2 ticks.
5. The archive timeouts that remain at the new speed, and whether any film needs more than 900 s.
