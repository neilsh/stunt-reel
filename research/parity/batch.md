# Batch Parity Survey: Every Distinct Corpus Film

`[Bounded Observation]`, 2026-10-30. The survey was stopped on purpose after **79 of 529** distinct films were scored (about 15%): enough to characterise the failure modes and the score spread, and the remaining films would cost tens of hours at 200 s per capture. The numbers below are the ledger at that point (`--summary` regenerates them); resuming is just `mise run batch-parity`. The plan is [`work/2026-10/batch-parity/plan.md`](../../work/2026-10/batch-parity/plan.md).

## 1. How to Run

```
mise run batch-parity                       # resumable: finished stages are skipped
mise run batch-parity -- --only SLUG,...    # named films
mise run batch-parity -- --limit N          # the first N films by slug
mise run batch-parity -- --retry-errors     # rerun stages whose latest record is an error
mise run batch-parity -- --jobs N            # N films at once (default 1)
mise run batch-parity -- --force render     # rerun render and every later stage
mise run batch-parity -- --summary          # counts, failing films, lowest and highest SSIM
mise run batch-parity -- --summary --only SLUG,...  # one report row per film
```

Each distinct film (579 `.FLM` files, 529 after de-duplication by sha256, [`batch_films.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/batch_films.py)) goes through four stages ([`batch_parity.py`](../../python/scripts/batch_parity.py), [`batch_stages.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/batch_stages.py)): `archive` (`MAKEONE` through `make_all`; skipped when a sibling `.ALL` exists, 260 films), `capture` (`capture_playone_reference`), `render` (`stuntreel --headless`) and `score` (`stuntreel parity`). The stages stop at a film's first error. A capture whose `meta.json` shows a stuck film clock (first and last `film_tick` equal) or fewer than 2 frames is recorded as `capture invalid: ...`, not ok. A capture runs the build the film's `references.toml` row names, else `<data root>/builds/patch3` from `mise run game-builds`; a film with no row whose patch3 run stalls is retried on `builds/unpatched`, and the capture prints the row to commit ([`capture_run.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/capture_run.py), [ADR 0029](../../docs/adr/0029-references-name-each-films-builds-and-reference.md)). Without the tree a capture is an error naming the task. The append-only ledger is `build/parity/batch/ledger.jsonl` ([`batch_ledger.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/batch_ledger.py)), one record per stage run (`slug`, `stage`, `status`, `message`, `seconds`, `ssim`, `iou`); the latest record per slug and stage wins. Per film, `build/parity/batch/<slug>/report/` holds `report.json` and the contact sheets, copied to `frames/`, and the render is kept: since the render store ([ADR 0027](../../docs/adr/0027-renders-store-planes-in-per-second-zstd-chunks.md)) one is a few megabytes, where per-tick files ran to 2 to 5 GB per film and were deleted after scoring. With `--jobs N` each worker runs one film's stages in order; ledger appends and progress lines share one lock, `--max-renders M` (default 2) caps simultaneous renders, and a render waits, polling every 30 s, while less than `--min-free-gb` (default 8) is free under the batch root. A run that renders or scores first builds the release binary (`--no-build` skips it), and every progress line also goes to `build/parity/batch/batch.log` in UTF-8. `--force STAGE` reruns that stage and every later one whatever the ledger says; a forced render or score first removes its old output, while a forced capture or archive only adds a new run; a later stage the run does not reach (left out of `--stages`, or after a forced stage that failed) is recorded as a `stale` error and its output removed, so a resume reruns it ([ADR 0022](../../docs/adr/0022-captures-are-add-only-beside-a-read-only-corpus.md)). Nothing here touches `references.toml`: the survey scores whatever capture it makes and pins nothing.

Since 2026-11-03 the `render` and `score` stages are the film parity operation of [`parity_pipeline.py`](../../python/stunt_reel/parity_pipeline.py), the same code the single-film loop (`mise run parity`) runs, so the survey now also runs `check_render`. The `score` stage refuses a render whose film identity conflicts with the capture's (the `.FLM` name, size or hash, or, since #361, the archive hash when both sides record one; a render or capture made before either recorded it skips the archive check silently), or that holds no planes for a captured tick, and records an `error` naming the cause instead of a score. The results below predate the check, so a rescore can turn a row from a score into an error; a score of a render the check refuses was not parity evidence.

## 2. Results

By latest record per film and stage: archive 47 ok and 40 skipped (sibling `.ALL`), no errors; capture 80 ok and 5 errors; render and score 79 ok. 79 films scored.

* **Scores** (`[Bounded Observation]`, 79 films): median 0.68, 10th percentile 0.53, 90th percentile 0.79, range 0.004 to 0.924. 35 films score 0.70 or more; one reaches 0.88 (`vault-balloon`, 0.9236). 4 are below 0.1 and 7 below 0.5. For scale, the two pinned films score about 0.78 (CTRY) and 0.68 (INTRO) with the render tuned for them, so the untuned corpus lands in the same band; no film outside those two is near the 0.90 sign-off.
* **By family** (median, count): `flmonly` 0.73 (36), `desperat` 0.69 (3), `boz` 0.66 (3), `collage4` 0.63 (7), `collage5` 0.60 (9), `egalloc` 0.56 (11); `flmonly` is the best-behaved and `egalloc` the worst, but small families and noisy captures limit what that says.
* **Degenerate scores.** (`vault-balloon` was rescored in [section 5](batch-vault-films.md#5-retail-vault-films-on-the-render-store): 0.678 over 108 frames.) `vault-balloon` scored 0.9236 with IoU 0.0 and index agreement 0.0 over 3 frames, and its survey capture `frames/002` holds 3 frames at `film_tick` 0, the stuck-clock signature ([`../playone-exe/capture-speed.md`](../playone-exe/capture-speed.md) § 1). Films below 0.05 (`collage4-elbarto` 0.0042, `flmonly-act5` 0.0071, `egalloc-egallo10` 0.0114, `flmonly-act1` 0.0196) had one scored frame with IoU about 0.83 and index agreement near 0. Neither group is parity evidence; the capture-validity check (§ 1) now rejects the stuck-clock case at capture time, but these scores predate it.
* **Capture stalls are intermittent.** `no new frame after 2001 trap hits (0 frame(s) captured, 6 to 9 indices on the page)` after the dialog settled. Five films still end in a capture error: `collage4-exit`, `collage4-exit2`, `flmonly-bigman`, `flmonly-count` (stalls) and `flmonly-colprt2` (`capture invalid: only 1 frame(s)`). But `amcrisis-ac`, `egalloc-egalloc0` and `egalloc-egalloc5` stalled on the first pass and captured fine on a retry (305 s, 146 s, 311 s), so a stall is not a property of the film. Each stall costs about 207 s. These were DOSBox-X debugger stalls (no trap hit). Re-run on dosbox-automation with both `PLAYONE` builds ([`../playone-exe/patch3.md`](../playone-exe/patch3.md) § 3), `collage4-exit`, `collage4-exit2` and `flmonly-count` capture whole (`COUNT` is only 92 ticks long). The driver's default skip list (`KNOWN_CAPTURE_STALLS`) is now `flmonly-bigman`, which replays a tick apart from boot to boot so a dump pass disagrees with its trace (`--skip` adds slugs, `--only` or `--retry-errors` runs them); `vault-location` left it once the stride plan made it capturable ([section 6](batch-vault-films.md#6-the-suspects-on-the-stride-plan)).
* **Archive timeouts** (`exit 124`, 900 s) hit films of 323 KB or more under the original `cycles = 8000` conf; `MAKEONE` plays in real time, so these were long films, not hangs. Under `core = dynamic` and `cycles = max` they were retried and none remains an error ([`../makeone-exe/driving.md`](../makeone-exe/driving.md) § 8).
* **Orphaned emulators.** A timeout killed only the direct Python child on Windows, so each left a `dosbox-x.exe` and a 12 MB stage directory. The runner now kills the process tree and `make_all` gets a 90 s head start on its own timeout.
* **Disk full.** The first full run died after 12 films (`WinError 112`): nothing deleted the render output. Fixed; see § 1.
* **Frame sampling did not work.** An experimental `--sample-frames K` (a stride in trap hits between dumps, removed again) announced a stride of 2 on `vault-balloon` yet captured 112 frames either way, 10 to 11 ticks apart: guest time follows the host clock, so skipped dumps do not widen the spacing ([`../playone-exe/capture-speed.md`](../playone-exe/capture-speed.md) § 1).

## 3. Cost

Median stage times, from the ledger: archive 68 s (mixed settings; 7 to 27 s at `cycles = max`), capture 200 s (mean 229, 90th percentile 460, max 722), render 16 s, score 1.2 s. At the original settings archive generation was 44% of wall time and capture 52%; timeouts and failed captures were 37.5% of it. Capture is wall-paced (about 0.25 s per frame, 4 s of boot), and no DOSBox-X setting, `turbo` or frame sampling cut it, so serial capture over all 529 films would take about 36 hours. Eight captures at once on an idle machine finished in 64 s with the tick spread unchanged at 0 to 2 ticks ([`../playone-exe/capture-speed.md`](../playone-exe/capture-speed.md) § 1); `--jobs 8` is therefore the lever, with simultaneous renders capped by `--max-renders`.

## Ruled out

- **De-duplicating by name.** The same film appears under several folders (`NewPLAY`, `OldPLAY`, `Morganroth`); only the content hash identifies it.
- **Keeping per-tick rendered frames.** Gigabytes per film before the render store; the compact store ([ADR 0027](../../docs/adr/0027-renders-store-planes-in-per-second-zstd-chunks.md)) is now kept with the report and contact sheets.
