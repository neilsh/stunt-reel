* **Body.** Shape `0x7F37` (7604 bytes) reaches `RETURN` at `0x11F2` once SHADE16's two-word operand is decoded, so the thin body is not an unimplemented opcode. It decodes to 388 vertices and 195 triangles in four nodes (base mesh plus three decal meshes, one gated at `LodDistanceSq(1000)`); the opcode stream is 36 polygons (20 `POLYZ`, 16 `GVPOLY`), `ground_y_offset` 2624 at scale index 13 (0.32 m). Triangles by palette index: 0 -> 17, 47 -> 10, 56 -> 104, 74 -> 24, 136 -> 40. Index 47 (162,154,154) has 0.28 m2 total; index 56 (210,206,206) lies inside a 0.22 x 0.06 x 0.50 m box around the cockpit, which is detail, not a body; index 136 (28,40,97) holds 3.37 m2 and spans the wings. The capture shows a thick white and blue tapered fuselage about 25 px deep with striped wings (blue 136 alternating with white 63 and neighbours); ours is a 4 to 6 px grey sliver, wings 136 and 47 only. At tick 273 the capture has 2,603 object pixels there, our layer plane 1,287, overlap 410. `CTRY.ALL` holds a single `7Fxx` chunk, so there is no other aircraft LOD to bind. Superseded: the `[Hypothesis]` written here, that a decode path we did not model (the actor matrix, or a polygon path such as one-sided faces or `GVPOLY16`) supplied the body, was answered by the next two bullets: the body was in the `SORTON` subtrees past the first `RETURN`.
* **Body: BSP subtrees past the first `RETURN`** (2026-11-01, `[Bounded Observation]`). `run_vm` reads `SORTON` (`0x018C`) as a three-word skip and ends the shape at its first `RETURN`, so only the first subtree of a `SORTON` tree is drawn. In `0x7F37` that first list (`0x0E28`..`0x11F0`) is all 36 polygons, colours 136, 47 and 0 only; the remaining 7.6 KB (`0x11F2`..`0x1DB4`) holds 60 more polygons in `RETURN`-terminated subtrees, the only `FILLCOL 63` (white) words, and from `0x15FC` a centre-stack section (`PUSHCENTRE`). `INTRO.FLM`'s triplane `0x7F04` (slot 0 at tick 1009), vehicle `0x4150` and figure `0x3A26` are cut the same way (`returned_at` 4854 of 12,566, 3328 of 5012, 2298 of 3408 bytes) and report no stop, so they look complete. The flat black shape INTRO draws in the triplane's place is that truncated prefix: it carries no `OP_COLOR`, so every polygon is index 0. Following FreeFlight `siobj.cpp` `case 0x18c` (words are byte offsets from the opcode: inline subtree, then `op + 4 + w2`, then continue at `op + 2 + w1`) raises the triangle counts to 130, 351 and 120 at tick 1009, but most of the added faces are still black (98 of 130 for `0x7F04`) because no `SHADE16` or `GVPOLY16` colour rule was applied then (both are now: the next bullet, and [`terrain-shade.md` § 5](../playone-exe/terrain-shade.md#5-index-composition) for the `GVPOLY16` index), and `0x7F37` still stops at `PUSHCENTRE`. Round 1 alone is in Ruled out.
* **Adopted: the full `SORTON` walk, the centre stack and `SHADE` colour** (2026-11-01, [log row](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md)). On top of the walk, the centre stack, `GOSUB`/`GOTO`, the relative-matrix stack, `ROTX`/`ROTY`/`ROTZ` and `BVGT` as FreeFlight reads them decode `0x7F37` to the end (76 -> 140 polygons; its `0x15FC` section is the tail, gear and nose) and three `INTRO.FLM` aircraft that stopped on `BVGT` or `PUSHCENTRE` (`0x7F09`, `0x7F19`, `0x7F1A`); the `SHADE` rule ([`sod-vm.md` § 8.5](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#85-polygon-colour-at-shade-time)) turns the triplane's black faces red. Scores 0.7790 / 0.5376 (from 0.7808 / 0.5488), actor overlap 0.122 / 0.099 (from 0.091 / 0.062); verdict CTRY 45 closer / 75 further, all in the actors layer, INTRO 20 / 167, 164 in actors and three props-only frames (876, 888, 2125: `0x7F04`, `0x3A26`, `0x4150` 685 to 1280 m away, 2 to 7 px; at 876 the added pixels cover hill in the capture). That fails the plan's score gate; it was adopted on the user's call that a drawn but miscoloured body beats a missing one, and on the readout: `INTRO` `aircraft-absent` 9 -> 0 of 12 (now parts missing 11, shape broken 9), `CTRY` `aircraft-shape-broken` 10 -> 3. What remains: the fuselage panels are drawn but painted 136 or 23 where the capture uses the grey ramp 48..63 (the `GVPOLY16` colour path, open then), and the `SHADE` light vector was a guess. Both have since been read (`[Established]`, static, [`terrain-shade.md` § 5](../playone-exe/terrain-shade.md#5-index-composition), [`sod-vm.md` § 8.5](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#85-polygon-colour-at-shade-time)): the `GVPOLY16` handler's per-vertex path composes each vertex's index as `base + max(0, highbyte(s >> 2) + G)` (`GVPOLY`'s uses `s >> 1`), with `base` the high byte of the word after the registers (`0x3004`, base `0x30`, in `0x7F37`), and shapes have been lit with the world light since [#233](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/pull/233). _Source: [the GVPOLY16 colour rule is decoded](../../work/2026-11/472_research-contradictions/journal.md#2026-11-13-the-gvpoly16-colour-rule-is-decoded-its-ramp-operand-is-not-traced), [GVPOLY16 shifts its shade by two](../../work/2026-11/472_research-contradictions/journal.md#2026-11-14-gvpoly16-shifts-its-shade-by-two-and-reads-its-ramp-after-the-registers)._
* **Adopted: actors drawn without the shape's ground offset** (2026-11-02, `[Bounded Observation]`, heuristic, [log row](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md), [#67](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/67)). Re-measured after the orientation fix, the `shift` probe put the hero 8 rows *high* (median dy +8, mostly +8 to +10, dx median -2, 74 frames of at least 300 actor pixels): the pre-orientation -10 had flipped sign with the actor. That is the header's `ground_y_offset` (2624 at scale index 13, 0.32 m, about 10 rows at the hero's usual 80 px wingspan): the decoder adds it to every vertex in model +Y, which stands a world prop on the ground it is placed at, but a film actor's position is its model origin. While actors drew rolled 180 degrees the same lift pushed them 10 rows low. Sweeps, CTRY / INTRO `mean_ssim_scored` (actor overlap): the lift on every shape scaled by k, k = 1 0.7843 / 0.6902 (0.227 / 0.093), k = 0 0.7781 / 0.7287 (0.353 / 0.248), k = -1 0.7706 / 0.7143; actors only, the lift taken back out along the actor's own +Y by a fraction d, d = -0.5 0.7859 / 0.7073 (0.337 / 0.137), **d = -1 0.7871 / 0.7264 (0.353 / 0.248)**, d = -1.5 0.7826 / 0.7141, d = -2 0.7752 / 0.7108. Verdict CTRY 53 closer / 28 further (worst tick 829, the crash close-up, where the struck column moves a few rows), INTRO 184 / 19. Residual `shift` after the fix: dy median -2 (mostly -2 and 0), dx median +2, against the world's own optimum of about -4 rows (Ruled out, single framing offset): the hero's placement residual is gone to within the world's. Rescored on top of the square-pixel 256 px projection ([log row](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md), [`framing-and-actors.md` § 2](framing-and-actors.md#2-projection-square-raster-pixels-focal-length-256-adopted)): fractions 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 give 0.8291 / 0.8313 / **0.8322** / 0.8286 on CTRY and 0.7154 / 0.7372 / **0.7693** / 0.7432 on INTRO, so the projection change leaves the optimum at one offset and the `shift` residual at dy -2, dx +2. `INTRO.FLM` tick 1009, capture, then ours with the offset, then ours without it (the triplane's wheels come down to the ground): [`intro-1009-ground-offset.png`](../../work/2026-11/aircraft-render/images/intro-1009-ground-offset.png); the same layout for `CTRY.FLM` ticks 587 and 785, where the biplane moves down toward the capture's position: [`ctry-587-ground-offset.png`](../../work/2026-11/aircraft-render/images/ctry-587-ground-offset.png), [`ctry-785-ground-offset.png`](../../work/2026-11/aircraft-render/images/ctry-785-ground-offset.png). INTRO still peaks at one offset, but the CTRY gain at one offset is +0.0013, under the noise floor, and CTRY prefers half; the `shift` residual is dy -4, dx +2. Since #132 the world's own optimum is 0 rows (the re-measure above), so about 4 rows remain: ours sits slightly low, which agrees with CTRY preferring half an offset. Kept at one offset on the user's call (2026-11-03): INTRO's gain is large and the CTRY change sits inside the noise floor rather than against it. What the capture still shows differently at tick 587 is attitude and shape (the capture's biplane is rolled with both striped wings visible; ours is flatter with one wing), not position. `PLAYONE.EXE` applies the lift only when it places scene-tree shapes; its actor draw (`0x1D5F`) does not read it (`[Supported Inference]`, [`scenery.md` § 3.3](../formats/auxiliary/scenery.md#33-prop-height)).
* **Every mesh after a shape's first drew the wrong vertices** (2026-11-03, `[Bounded Observation]`). `stunt-core` stores a mesh node's indices absolute, into the shape's whole vertex buffer (`tessellate.rs`, `ShapeMesh::indices`), but [`plan_upload`](../../crates/stunt-render/src/mesh.rs) passed each node's `vertex_offset` as the `draw_indexed` base vertex, so every node after the first read vertices `vertex_offset` too far on. A multi-node shape such as `0x7F37` (base mesh plus three decals) lost those nodes to spikes and fragments: at `INTRO.FLM` tick 1141 the old render shows the biplane's fuselage missing and stray triangles, the fixed one draws the whole aircraft. Drawing from base vertex 0 scores 0.8285 / 0.7081 (from 0.8291 / 0.7154; IoU 0.9897 / 0.9692 from 0.9897 / 0.9712). Both tracks of the adoption rule reject that, but this is a contract fix, not a parity candidate: the old `draw_indexed` call applied each node's `vertex_offset` as the base vertex although the indices were already absolute, so it read vertices outside the node. INTRO falls because the now-whole aircraft paints more pixels at the position and colour errors above (worst movers 1130 to 1152 and 2422 to 2676, -0.02 to -0.03 each; 177 of 346 frames move).

## Ruled out

All scores `mean_ssim_scored`, CTRY / INTRO unless a single film is named. Baselines moved between rounds (0.771 / 0.676 on 2026-10-22, 0.7708 / 0.6762 at `d90a047`, 0.7732 / 0.6799 after page timing); each sweep is read against its own baseline. Nothing below was adopted.

**`SORTON` subtrees drawn, colour undecoded** (2026-11-01, [log row](../../work/2026-10/parity-log/log.md)). The `siobj.cpp` traversal above, alone: 0.7791 / 0.5455 against 0.7808 / 0.5488; index agreement CTRY 0.515 -> 0.536; actor overlap (`parity-probe iou`) 0.091 -> 0.109 / 0.062 -> 0.084. Regression verdict: CTRY 45 closer, 75 further, all 75 in the actors layer; INTRO 26 closer, 134 further, 131 in actors and 3 (ticks 876, 888, 2125) in props only, 2 to 7 pixels each. The props frames fail the plan's score gate, so no readout was run. The traversal is very likely right (both diagnoses found the same cut, and the subtrees hold the missing parts) but the added faces are black until colour is decoded. Also ruled out on the way: back-face culling as the cause (culling off 0.7721 on CTRY), the `LodDistanceSq(1000)` node (it is `IFVIS` face 1, one wing triangle), the two-sided flag, and colour from the high byte of the `GVPOLY16` trailing word (0.7779 / 0.5449; superseded: scored before shapes were lit with the world light, and the renderer has since taken the ramp from that byte, [`sod-vm.md` § 8.5](../playone-exe/sod-vm.md#85-polygon-colour-at-shade-time)).

**Moving props between passes**, `DBG_PROP_DECAL` putting every world prop in the biased decal group: apparent gain (CTRY 0.7768, INTRO 0.6766) was an artifact. 28.8% of pixels changed, all prop (layer 3) to terrain (layer 2), because the then-positive bias hid props behind coplanar ground; index agreement fell 0.475 -> 0.215, the signature of drawing less of the capture. Reverted; the bias-sign fix gave the intended effect.
