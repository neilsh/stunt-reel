# Journal: outline cull by width

## 2026-11-20: the captures searched for a back-face sliver

- **Question**: Does any reference capture tell the outline cull's area test (a back-facing triangle over 1 px² loses its outline) from a width test (one under 1 px tall over its longest edge keeps it), by showing a thin back-facing sliver drawn ([#858](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/858), option (b))?
- **Method**: A switch `DBG_CULL_WIDTH` ([patch](switches/DBG_CULL_WIDTH.diff)) replaced `is_back_facing`'s test with the width test, keeping the area test for a corner behind the eye. `mise run sweep -- --var DBG_CULL_WIDTH=0,1 --track evidence --record work/2026-11/858_outline-cull-width --topic "outline cull width"` on the RTX 4070 Laptop GPU at `5fbfe844`, every reference film, no `--target` ([figures](sweeps/outline-cull-width.toml)). Then `mise run frame -- --var DBG_CULL_WIDTH=0,1` on 13 frames chosen as the largest movers within `intro`, `balloon` and `bomb` (`intro` 1123, 2421, 2476, 2762, 2982; `balloon` 452, 463, 474, 485; `bomb` 2773, 2850, 2905, 3345), and a count of each pixel the two variants draw differently by which variant the capture's pixel matches. The lone-apex pixel was not searched for: the switch does not change it. No DOSBox run.
- **Result**: The switch moves 88 of 1,687 scored frames, each by at most about 20 pixels of index agreement (the largest, 0.00032, on `intro:2982`): no film's mean SSIM by more than 0.000013 (`intro`), worst frame −0.0015, none down by more than 0.005. Index agreement rose on 30 of them and fell on 19. Of the 135 pixels the variants draw differently in those 13 frames, the capture matches the area test's on 48, the width test's on 55 and neither on 32. Neither rule fits the captures throughout. On `intro:2982` the capture draws a thin brown upright two pixels wide where the width test does and the area test draws one (20 of 22 pixels for width; [picture](images/intro-2982-outline-cull-width.png)). On `intro:2476` and `intro:2421` the width test adds dark pixels along the balloon's lower edge that the capture lacks ([picture](images/intro-2476-outline-cull-width.png)). On `balloon` the same dark-green edge sides with the width test at tick 452 (12 of 13 pixels) and with the area test eleven ticks later at 463 (12 of 12) ([452](images/balloon-452-outline-cull-width.png), [463](images/balloon-463-outline-cull-width.png)), so there the difference follows where our edge lands against the capture's, not the rule. The sweep's winner line reads "adopts (evidence, film means)" on a rise of 0.000013, under the 0.002 noise floor.
- **Verdict**: not found. No capture separates the two rules, so per the owner's decision on #858 the width test is not built and the issue moves to [`.out-of-scope/outline-cull-width.md`](../../../.out-of-scope/outline-cull-width.md).
