# Journal: #510 the view slot's shadow

## 2026-11-16: the view slot's shadow, live, and a reference film for it

**Question**: whether `PLAYONE` queues and draws the shadow of the view camera's own slot (`[0x22DE]`), which the scene walk skips for the actor draw (`0x1E26`) but not for the shadow test (`0x1E2F` → `0x19FC`); and which corpus film to add as a parity reference so that #499's view-slot shadow can be scored. [#510](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/510).

**Method**: at `2c60728`. A census of the corpus `.FLM` streams ([`view_shadow_census.py`](view_shadow_census.py); `mise exec -- uv run python work/2026-11/510_view-slot-shadow/view_shadow_census.py <data root>/corpus`, or one `.FLM` for its slices): per distinct film (529 by SHA-256; a film the walker rejects is skipped), the ticks whose view slot has `SIF_EXISTS`, `SIF_DRAW` and `SIF_SHADOW` and a shape, the view slot's stream height, and the drawn, shadowed slots without `SIF_MOVES`. Live: `mise run debug-x` on DOSBox-X, unpatched build, with [`tools/dosbox-x/plans/view-slot-shadow.toml`](../../../tools/dosbox-x/plans/view-slot-shadow.toml) (the `0x1A03` probe was added after the first three runs): breaks at `0x1A18` (plane branch), `0x1A8A` (the shadow's cull call, `SI` the slot), `0x1A8F` (culled), `0x1AD1` (the actor's cull) and `0x1C0D` (a shadow entry drawn), capturing `[0x22DE]` where `SI` is a slot. A shadow is the view slot's when `SI` at `0x1A8A` equals `[0x22DE]`, and a drawn entry is that slot's when its word `+4` (the slot record) does. Three films, armed where the census puts the shadowed view slot in the stream: `Films/OldPLAY/Vault/BRETT.FLM` at tick 600, `Films/OldPLAY/Vault/TERMIN.FLM` at 800, `Films/NewPLAY/Vault/NEWFX.FLM` at 1800 (all under `corpus/siarchive/`). Then `make-all`, `capture-ref`, `capture-digest` and `mise run parity -- --films brett` by the `improving-render-parity` skill's [comparing-references](../../../.agents/skills/improving-render-parity/comparing-references.md) steps.

**Result**:

* **Census.** 151 of the 529 films have such ticks (the #478 census, by another walker, counted 160); in 58 of them the view slot's median height over those ticks is 4 m or less. Short films among them: `BRETT.FLM` (1,432 ticks; view slot 0, flags `0x1F`, shape `0x415C`, 0.15 m up, at ticks 400 to 444 and 595 to 758; no drawn, shadowed slot without `SIF_MOVES`), `TERMIN.FLM` (1,930 ticks; view slot 0, `0x1F`, 3.5 to 9.4 m up, and view slot 2, flags `0x17`, shape `0x4170`, 3.3 m up, without `SIF_MOVES`) and `NEWFX.FLM` (2,522 ticks; view slot 0, `0x1F`, 0.40 m up, at ticks 1773 to 2408, and slot 8, flags `0x17`, shape `0x7E49`, without `SIF_MOVES`).
* **`BRETT.FLM`, ticks 633 to 648, view slot 0 (record `DS:0x393A` = `[0x22DE]`): queued and drawn.** At `0x1A18` the slot record holds `+0x1E` = `0x033C` and `+0x22` = 0; the plane there is `a = 0`, `b = 30933`, `c = 0`, `D = 0` (`y = 0`). At `0x1A8A` the shadow point `DX, BP, DI` is 0, 2472, 0 (straight below the eye, depth 0) with `CL` = 15, the shape's radius `[es:4]` = 9402 and `[es:8]` = 14, and `[0x229C]`, `[0x229E]` = 32767, 0. No `0x1A8F` hit followed any of the six view-slot cull calls, so the cull passed it; each frame then reached `0x1C0D` with the entry `0x4A00`, whose word `+4` is `0x393A` and byte `+0xF` is 0 (a shadow entry). Recomputed: the point is shifted right 1 to the shape's exponent (`BP` = 1236), and the `y` limit is (9402/2)·23552/65536 + 9402·32767/65536 = 1689 + 4700 = 6389, which it is inside. The actor cull `0x1AD1` saw slots `0x3A48`, `0x3AA2` and `0x3AFC`, never `0x393A`, which `0x1E26` skips.
* **`TERMIN.FLM`, ticks 840 to 897, view slot 2 (record `0x39EE` = `[0x22DE]`): culled.** No `0x1A18` hit (the slot has no `SIF_MOVES`; the null-plane branch `0x1A12` was not broken on). At `0x1A8A` its point is 0, 8446, −14 or −8 with `CL` = 13, radius 28093 and `[es:8]` = 15, and `[0x229C]`, `[0x229E]` = 20479, 3; `0x1A8F` followed every one. Recomputed: shifted to the shape's exponent the radius is 7023 and the depth term 438 − 8 = 430, so the `y` limit is 430·23552/65536 + 7023·20479/65536 = 154 + 2194 = 2348, the value `DX` held at `0x1A8F`; 8446 is outside it, so the ground point is below the frame.
* **`NEWFX.FLM`, ticks 1831 to 1855, view slot 0 (record `0x393A` = `[0x22DE]`): queued and drawn.** Point 0, 6578, 0, radius 14393, not culled in any of the six sampled frames; the entry `0x4A00` with `+4` = `0x393A` was drawn each frame. Slot 8 (record `0x3C0A`, flags `0x17`, shape `0x7E49`, no `SIF_MOVES`) was queued and drawn too (entry `0x4A20`, `+4` = `0x3C0A`), and its record holds `+0x1E` = `0x0320`, a level plane (`b` = 20342). The probe `0x240A` is not called for a slot without `SIF_MOVES` (the test at `0x146C`), so something else left that value in the record; untraced.
* **The archive and the capture.** `make-all` on `NEWFX.FLM` timed out at 600 s on patch3 (a second attempt then started, which this session's own 900 s wrapper stopped) and again at 600 s on the unpatched build, the guest screen showing the film clock at 00:16 of its 50 s: no archive. On `BRETT.FLM` the patch3 `MAKEONE` wrote the archive in 25.5 s (`manifests/all/vault-brett.toml`, `all_sha256` `4aba3a75…`). The first `capture-ref`, taken before a row pinned that archive, played the shipped `OldPLAY/Vault/BRETT.ALL` (`8128fbcc…`), which `parity` refused; the second, with the row pinning the archive, is the reference (`97136a97…`, patch3, 130 frames). In it palette index 0 covers 4,856 pixels of rows 150 to 184 on each of the 20 captured frames from tick 409 to 452 and 606 to 761, and 60 to 168 pixels on each other frame: a black band across the bottom of the view whose sides slope inward toward its top, while the car itself is not drawn (ticks 431 and 639 inspected with `mise run frame`). Our render has no black band there: it drops the view slot, shadow included ([#499](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/499)).
* **Scores.** `mise run parity -- --films brett` at `2c60728` (`crates` tree `7aeabce`): `mean_ssim_scored` 0.8479167, `mean_iou_scored` 0.9865919, 123 of 130 frames scored, on the NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU. With `--software-only`: 0.8479624 and 0.9865903, but on WARP (`Microsoft Basic Render Driver`), not the llvmpipe adapter the other rows' `lavapipe` tables name, so no `lavapipe` table was written.
* **Backdrop bands.** The [`probes.md`](../../../research/parity/probes.md) snippet, run on that render, gives `[162,164] [195,195] [229,231] [235,235] [238,238]`, 52 % of capture pixels; `mise run parity-probe -- iou ... --every 2` then reads 0.1902 over 36 frames.
* **#499 items 2 and 3 in `BRETT.FLM`.** Item 2: no drawn, shadowed slot without `SIF_MOVES` (census). Item 3: breaks on `0x1A03` (slot `+0x22` nonzero, a code-8 box's height) and `0x1A12` (null plane) from tick 1 to 1430 hit neither, so no shadowed slot took a box height or a null plane in that run.

The break runs used the unpatched build and the film's shipped `.ALL` (the stage `debug-x` makes); the capture used patch3 and the `make-all` archive. They agree on the view slot's shadow over the same ticks.

**Verdict**: adopted as findings. The view slot's shadow is queued and drawn when its ground point survives the cull: `[Bounded Observation]`, by breakpoint (`BRETT.FLM` ticks 633 to 648 and `NEWFX.FLM` ticks 1831 to 1855, slot 0 in each) and by capture (`BRETT.FLM`, the black band on all 20 captured frames of its two view-slot spans). It is culled when its ground point is outside the view: `[Bounded Observation]`, `TERMIN.FLM` ticks 840 to 897, slot 2. `BRETT.FLM` becomes the reference `[brett]`: its view slot's shadow is in frame on 20 of its 130 captured frames, and it has neither #499 item 2 nor item 3. `NEWFX.FLM`, which has item 2, was set aside because no `MAKEONE` build wrote its archive within 600 s, not by score.
