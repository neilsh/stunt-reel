## 2026-11-01: GVPOLY polygons painted flat, and the triplane before the ramp word

From section 8.5 of the note, as it stood at `4df36bf`:

`[Hypothesis]` for the `SHADE*` level law, static and read once; `[Supported Inference]` for the light, measured below. Polygon colour is a running word at `[0x41E8]` that `OP_COLOR` and the four shade opcodes all write. `SHADE1` (`0x0144`), `SHADE2` (`0x014D`), `SHADE16` (`0x02B5`) and `SHADE216` (`0x02C7`) take `norm, base` (handlers at image `0xA526`, `0xA519`, `0xA550`, `0xA55D`, shared tail `0xA52F`): they load `s = word[0x7468 + norm]`, the per-normal shade that `SHADES` (`0x9481`) wrote as `-clamp(N.L >> 16)`, halve it (twice for the 16-level forms; the `2` forms negate it first), and store `base + max(0, (s >> 8) + adj)`, with `adj = -2` when `base == 0x10`. `base` is a ramp start: in the `INTRO.FLM` palette `0x10` is red 16..29, `0x30` grey 48..53, `0x40` brown 64..79, the ramps the capture's triplane uses. FreeFlight discards the operands and paints with the last `OP_COLOR`. The light vector `L` is passed in registers to the display-list entry `0x9100` by its caller: scenery gets the world light `DS:0x49EC..0x49F0` unchanged, a rotated object that light turned by its rotation and the view and doubled (caller `0x1CFB`; [`terrain-shade.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/terrain-shade.md) section 4.2). `GVPOLY`/`GVPOLY16` give each vertex its own index, which the filler interpolates (below). Their word after the register list is `(ramp_base << 8) | count` (`0x3004` in `CTRY.FLM`'s `0x7F37`, `0x1004` on the triplane), not a normal register, and `count` shade offsets into the `SHADES` words follow, one per vertex (2026-11-01, `[Hypothesis]` from the stream shape; the handlers `0x995C` and `0x9A24` are disassembled for the index they compose, [`terrain-shade.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/terrain-shade.md) section 5, but not traced for how they read these operands). The renderer first painted each such polygon flat at `base` plus the mean of its vertices' levels: grey-ramp pixels moved toward the capture's (`CTRY` 3.7k -> 33.8k against 52.4k, `INTRO` 120k -> 44.5k against 49.8k) but SSIM did not rise ([parity log](../../2026-10/parity-log/log.md)).

Before the ramp word was read, `INTRO.FLM` tick 1009 drew the triplane's fuselage grey and its wings bright pink where the capture has a red fuselage and dark red wings (capture above, ours below): [`intro-1009-gvpoly-running-colour.png`](../aircraft-render/images/intro-1009-gvpoly-running-colour.png).

## 2026-11-05: the SHADE light swept with DBG_SHADE_LIGHT

From section 8.5 of the note, as it stood at `4df36bf`:

**The light** (2026-11-05, [#221](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/221)). `SHADES` dots `L` with the shape's raw normal words, and an unrotated object is handed the world light as stored, so `L` is in the raw model frame and needs no game-to-`[I1]` axis mapping: the decoder keeps the normals in that frame too. The mapping is derived, not chosen by score. The decoded model frame is +Y up ([`coordinates-and-units.md` § 1.2](../../../research/formats/concepts/coordinates-and-units.md#12-right-handed-metric-basis-i1)), so `(99, 32766, -17)` points up, and `s = -(N . L)` leaves an upward face at its ramp base and lifts a downward one. The old stand-in `SHADE_LIGHT = (0, -32767, 0)` had the opposite sign. Both films set film variable 13 to `0x2D` at tick 0 (`mise run flm-events`), the value `0x196C` was seen with on both, so both get `(99, 32766, -17)` ([`ShadeLight`](../../../crates/stunt-core/src/sod/light.rs)); how `0x196C` maps other values is open ([`terrain-shade.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/terrain-shade.md), open question 1). Measured with `DBG_SHADE_LIGHT`, all shapes decoded under one light: the stand-in 0.8646 / 0.7986 (CTRY / INTRO), the world light 0.8641 / 0.8128, its negation `(-99, -32766, 17)` and the 180-degree-about-Z form `(-99, -32766, -17)` both 0.8646 / 0.7986 (the 99 and 17 move no shade across a ramp step, so only the sign of y matters). The world light for scenery with the stand-in for actors scored 0.8646 / 0.7986 exactly: no scenery shade that reaches the screen moves, and the whole gain is on the aircraft. Actors are therefore decoded under the world light unrotated, the light `PLAYONE` would hand a level, unrotated object. Their own light turns with their rotation and the view every frame, and shapes are decoded once, so it is not modelled.

**Lives in** [`sod-vm.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm.md) § 8.5.

## 2026-11-12: the census walk and the section end scored

From section 8.6 of the note, as it stood at `4df36bf`:

**No census walk reaches any of them.** On the `sod-census` walk of the corpus's 3,703 unique models, which follows `SORTON`, `GOTO`, `GOSUB` and, with a temporary switch, both arms of every `BVGT`, no walk reaches `BZLT`, `BVIS`, `REJVEC` or `REJ2D`. With the corrected end, no section starts inside another. No drawing opcode stands at any section's true end. The words there are 102,536 `IFVIS`, 13,845 `RETURN`, 3,070 `SORTON`, 713 `LINECOL`, 79 `COLOR`, 37 `PUSHCENTRE`, 36 `BVGT`, 31 `IPG`, 24 `MOVETO`, 3 `GOSUB` and one `0x0333`. No opcode at a true end draws by itself, though the targets of the 3 `GOSUB` and 36 `BVGT` there were not checked, and `0x0333` is not decoded. A sweep of the old end against the corrected one scored the seven reference films identically to four decimals. `[Bounded Observation]` for this walk: `BVLT`'s branch is not followed, and code no branch reaches is not walked. A raw byte scan for `0x0318` finds hundreds of matches in the `.RES` files, but they are register offsets, since `0x0318` is register 66, not opcodes. So, on the paths the census walks, the props loss the seven reference films measure ([`attribution.md`](../../../research/parity/attribution.md) section 2) does not come from `BZLT`, `BVIS`, `REJVEC` or `REJ2D`. A gate behind a `BVLT` branch or in code no branch reaches is not covered.

## 2026-11-12: OP_PERFDISK read and its radius checked on BOMB

From section 8.8 of the note, as it stood at `4df36bf`:

**`OP_PERFDISK` read** (2026-11-12, [#392](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/392); `[Established]` for the instruction sequences, static, from the unpacked image with `mise run disasm`; nothing observed live). The entry `0xA040` reads `centre_reg, radius, axis_reg` at `0xA08E`. It skips in shadow mode and when the centre's view depth is under `0x80`. It scales the radius as `(radius · work:0x8070) >> 16`, the normal's depth difference by `work:0x8108` shifted by `work:0x810C + 1`, and normalises the radius and centre to one shift (`0x9FD9`) and the normal to another (`0x9FAB`). It forms products of the normal's components in 32-bit arithmetic and calls `0x0D11D` with a screen centre, two extents and two offsets (`si`, `di`) that shear the mirrored half. `0xD11D` clips against `work:0x41C4`/`0x41C6`, returns carry set off screen, and builds a table of row spans with a stepped 32-bit decision variable (`0xD440`, `0xD4AF`), not a closed form; the flat filler `0x8010` fills them. A sibling entry `0xA05C` runs the same builder `0xA08E` but stores the span-table pointer in the register's slot (`[bx + 0x423E]`) instead of filling; its use is unread. In the entry `0xA040` no second fill or hole appears, so it fills one solid disc; whether `OP_PERFDISK` ever perforates through the `0xA05C` pair is `[Unknown]` (the name suggests it). The tests at `0xA156` (high word of a derived term not above 0) and `0xA1AB` (high word exactly 0) skip the disc; `[Supported Inference]` that they drop a degenerate disc, edge-on or tiny, which `vm.rs` does not reproduce. No branch tests the sign of a normal component.

`vm.rs` draws the disc as a 16-gon (16 is a guess at roundness; the radius is checked, below) in the plane perpendicular to the raw register difference `regs[centre] − regs[axis]`, one face for each side, in the current colour, with `radius` in register units. **Scale checked against a same-camera capture** ([#425](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/425)). `OP_SPHERE` shifts the radius product by 8 and divides by depth; this handler shifts by 16 (`0xA0A2..0xA0A6`: `imul [0x8070]`, high word) and leaves the perspective to the conic it builds, so the two shifts do not by themselves say the disc is 256 times smaller. `BOMB` tick 2058 has a capture and a render from the same camera (the car body's left edge and the disc's centre column agree to 2 px), and the wheel disc's width agrees with `radius` in register units to about 2 px (one shape, ticks 2058 and 2070; other shapes and depths unchecked): at the rows 172 and 184 the capture is 77 and 91 px wide, the 16-gon 72 and 87 px, and a 64-gon 1 to 2 px under the capture; at tick 2070 the capture and the 64-gon differ by 1 to 2 px over four rows (`[Bounded Observation]`: two ticks, one shape, widths read off the palette index of the disc's flat colour). The 16-gon is 4 to 6 % narrow and the 64-gon closes most of that gap; the remainder is unexplained, perhaps the stepped ellipse of `0xD11D`. A 32-gon moved no film by more than 0.00015 SSIM and BOMB, LOCATION, LAND and INTRO fell (BOMB -0.00014), so it is not adopted and 16 stays. The `work:0x8108` rescale of the normal's depth difference (`0xA0DB..0xA0E8`) is `dz · 0x8108 · 2^(0x810C+1) >> 16`; with the view matrix's two screen rows scaled by `0x8108` in Q15 and the depth row shifted right by `0x810C` ([`memory-map.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/memory-map.md)), that makes the normal's three components share one scale, which `vm.rs` already has because it keeps the normal in the model frame, so no rescale is added (`[Supported Inference]`: read from the two sites, not verified live; the tilt looks similar, and a 2 px height gap at tick 2058 is unexplained). The near-depth cut, the shadow-pass skip and the early exits are not reproduced. Scored against the seven reference films with `mise run parity` (RTX 4070). _Source: [the handler read and the scores](../392_perfdisk/journal.md#2026-11-12-the-handler-read-to-its-end-and-drawn)._

**Lives in** [`sod-vm.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm.md) § 8.8.

## 2026-11-12: thin polygons measured and outlines drawn

From section 8.11 of the note, as it stood at `4df36bf`:

**What it does to thin polygons** (`[Bounded Observation]`, `INTRO.FLM` tick 3301, [#426](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/426)). The pines `0x03A3` and `0x03A7` are a type-8 quad (section 8.8) whose `INTV` midpoints make two `POLYZ` triangles: the whole tree, and a sliver outside its right edge at most a sixth of its half-width across. The capture draws a six-pixel-wide pine's sliver as a strip of light green one to two pixels wide down 14 rows (21 pixels); our renderer, whose rasteriser fills only pixels whose centres a triangle covers, drew 2 pixels of it. Across the top-left 100 × 100 pixels the four tree colours covered 1,471 pixels in the capture and 815 in ours, with every tree checked in a 60 × 60 part of the crop at the same place in both. `[Supported Inference]` that the inclusive spans are why the sliver measured is drawn.

The renderer follows it in part. `stunt-render` draws every edge that exactly one filled triangle of a mesh node uses as a one-pixel screen line (section 8.10), in its end vertices' colours, after the node's triangles ([`outline.rs`](../../../crates/stunt-render/src/outline.rs)), so a polygon also covers the pixels its outline crosses. It does this as line quads for the shapes drawn per frame (actors and view-facing props such as these pines), and as a line list for the world mesh's props (below). With it, the pine's sliver draws 23 pixels and the crop's tree colours 1,188. Every reference film's score rose but `LOCATION`'s, which fell; the journal entry cited below has each film's scores. The outline lines are wound both ways, like every line here, so a back-facing polygon's outline still draws where nothing hides it, which `PLAYONE` does not do.

**The world mesh.** Line quads cost four vertices and twelve indices an edge: outlining `LOCATION`'s world that way grew it from 6.7 million vertices and 7.5 million indices to 32.4 million and 84.7 million, and with terrain excluded its render failed with a wgpu out-of-memory error. The world now draws the same edges, those exactly one filled triangle of a mesh slice uses, as a `LineList` index buffer over its own vertices, two indices an edge, after the world draw's triangles and with their depth test (a decal slice's outline compares `LessEqual` without the `[I5]` bias, which wgpu allows only on triangles; [`mesh.rs`](../../../crates/stunt-render/src/mesh.rs) `GpuModel::from_ir_outlined`). Terrain and ocean triangles are not outlined. The line list is drawn one pixel wide by the backend's line rules, which need not draw the end pixels a line quad does (not measured). A slice split over chunks (a world over `max_buffer_size`; `LOCATION`'s is one chunk) is outlined per run, so its cut is outlined too. On `LOCATION` the outline is 6.4 million edges, 51.5 MB of indices, and a whole-film render took about 16 % longer (two runs each). Every reference film's score rose; the journal entry cited below has each film's scores. In `LOCATION` tick 4170 the derrick's legs draw a pixel wider, as in the capture (judged by eye).

**Lives in** [`sod-vm.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm.md) § 8.11.
