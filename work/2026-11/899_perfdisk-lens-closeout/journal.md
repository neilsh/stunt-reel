# Journal: OP_PERFDISK squeeze at other lenses

## 2026-11-22: the squeeze switch over seven films, and the disc outline

- **Question**: Does any reference film hold a disc the lens squeeze moves, at a lens other than 42.67° or 50.95° ([#899](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/899))? Does an actor disc's outline escape the squeeze at a non-default lens?
- **Method**: No new capture or render; `origin/main` `c512d05c`, local-windows. Re-read the figures of [#754](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/754)'s sweeps, which scored `DBG_DISCZOOM=0` (squeeze off) against `DBG_DISCZOOM=1` (on) on the captured ticks of the seven reference films on lavapipe: [`perfdisk-lens-squeeze-rebased.toml`](../754_perfdisk-lens-squeeze/sweeps/perfdisk-lens-squeeze-rebased.toml) (tree `191fa1a`, the later sweep) and [`perfdisk-lens-squeeze.toml`](../754_perfdisk-lens-squeeze/sweeps/perfdisk-lens-squeeze.toml) (tree `15a615e`). `mise run judge -- --recheck` on each; then a scratch script (Python `tomllib`) paired the two variants' frames by tick and counted a frame as changed when its recorded `ssim` or `index` differed at all (the file keeps 7 decimals), and took the largest absolute `ssim` difference per film. The disc outline was read in `crates/stunt-render/src/outline.rs` (`outlines`, test `a_disc_is_not_outlined`) and `shaders/mode13h.wgsl` (`vertex_output`). An attempt to read `BOMB`'s and `BALLOON`'s lens per tick by rendering (`mise run frame -- --software-only`) failed here with `no compatible GPU adapter (one with clip distances) found`, so their lens is as #754's journal states it, not rechecked.
- **Result**:
  - *Recheck.* Both files judge again to `winner: DBG_DISCZOOM=1 adopts (evidence, film means; target idx +0.0423 attribution; worst target frame +0.0297 location:6007)`, their recorded winner line; `frames outside the target moved >0.005: ctry 0, intro 0, land 0, balloon 0, traintnl 0, bomb 0, location 0`. The two files give the same frame counts and changed-frame counts below.
  - *Frames.* In the rebased sweep, captured ticks (scored ticks) and changed ticks: `CTRY` 121 (114) and 0; `INTRO` 347 (209) and 43; `LAND` 103 (97) and 4; `BALLOON` 108 (107) and 0; `TRAINTNL` 112 (112) and 21; `BOMB` 339 (316) and 0; `LOCATION` 745 (732) and 22. That is 1,875 captured ticks (1,687 scored), 90 changed. `LOCATION`'s 22 are 20 ticks within 5953 to 6161 and ticks 386 and 518.
  - *Size.* The largest absolute `ssim` change on a changed tick outside `LOCATION`'s ticks 5953 to 6161, over the 70 changed ticks there: 0.00117 (`INTRO`), 0.00048 (`LAND`), 0.00042 (`TRAINTNL`), 0.00004 (`LOCATION` 386 and 518). Inside the window the largest is 0.0499.
  - *Lens of the zero-change films.* [#754's journal](../754_perfdisk-lens-squeeze/journal.md#2026-11-18-the-disc-squeezed-along-depth-by-the-lens-zoom) puts `CTRY`, `BALLOON` and `BOMB` at the default lens, where the squeeze is 1 to 1 part in 32,768 (not rechecked for `BALLOON` and `BOMB`), so their zero changes follow from the lens and test no other one; [#841's search](../841_perfdisk-third-shape/journal.md#2026-11-20-intros-triplane-wheels-at-the-default-lens-and-at-1776) found only 42.67° in `CTRY`'s captured ticks. The films at other lenses are `LAND` (11.16°), `TRAINTNL` (14.84°), `INTRO` (17.76° and 8.28°) and `LOCATION` (22.10°, 46.93°, 56.97°, 58.33°, 50.95°), with the pixel counts of #841 (discs at most 356 px of a frame at a lens other than 42.67° and 50.95°; the squeeze moved at most 31 px on the ticks tested).
  - *The outline.* `outlines` returns false for a vertex with `VERTEX_FLAG_DISC`, so a disc's triangles get no outline quad since [#898](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/898) (`a1ec46c1`), and `a_disc_is_not_outlined` asserts a disc's vertex and index counts do not change. So no corner of a disc carries a line flag for the shader to test first; `vertex_output`'s disc branch squeezes the fill. The gap triage described, a fill squeezed with its one-pixel ring not, needed the outline corners #898 removed.
- **Verdict**: control, no code change. At `[Bounded Observation]` (lavapipe, tree `191fa1a`, the captured and scored ticks of the seven films, SSIM and index agreement), forcing the squeeze to 1 changes no frame of `CTRY`, `BALLOON` or `BOMB`, and outside `LOCATION`'s ticks 5953 to 6161 moves no frame's SSIM by more than 0.00117. That tree predates #898 and #690: its discs were a 16-gon fill with an unsqueezed one-pixel outline (`outline.rs` outlined every non-line triangle and `push_edge` kept the disc bit on the outline corners, which the shader's line branch takes before its disc branch), so the sweep bounds that renderer, not today's 64-gon, outline-free, row-snapped discs, and on discs of tens of pixels the ring is a large share of the disc, so 0.00117 may understate the squeeze's effect on today's. The outline gap does not exist on `main`: no disc outline is drawn.
