# Journal: #478 shadow plane, altitude cutoff, view-slot shadow and `0x806C` readers

## 2026-11-14: the shadow plane writer, the view slot and the `0x806C` readers read statically

**Question**: the five questions of [#478](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/478): who writes actor slot `+0x1E` and `+0x20/+0x22`; whether the view camera's slot can reach the shadow queue; where the handbook's altitude cutoff lives; whether `0x1C0D` projects straight down, and which create handler fills slot byte `+1`; what the other `work:0x806C` readers do.

**Method**: `mise run disasm` over the unpatched load image (`build/re/playone.img` in the data root), `0x0000..0x8240` and `0x8240..0xD510`, at `4986b89`; dispatch tables read from `DGROUP` (image `0xD510`): the scenery walker's `0x554` and `0x584`, the collision walker's `0x9FC` and `0xA08`, and the VM's nine-byte slots at image `0x8240` (`imm16 + 0x8240` is the handler). Two one-off censuses of the corpus films (529 distinct by SHA-256, 7 of them not walked to the end because the scratch walker stops on a malformed opcode): a slot re-created with the same shape and only `SIF_SHADOW` changed, with the slot's last stream `y` (the create's position or the latest `interp`); and, per slice, whether the `view_obj` slot (`0x90..0x97`, `c - 0x90`) has `SIF_EXISTS`, `SIF_DRAW` and `SIF_SHADOW` and a nonzero shape. No debugger run: every question the static read could decide was decided by it.

**Result**:

* **`+0x1E` and `+0x20/+0x22`.** Their only writer is the ground and collision probe `0x240A`, called once per slot from `0x1463` (`0x1492`). `0x1463` runs for each slot whose flag byte `+1` has bit 3 (`SIF_MOVES`) and a nonzero shape, and either byte `+0` bit 3 or `SIF_DRAW`; `0x4A17` runs it on the snapshot struct `0x4888` just before the frame draw `0x0EE9` (`0x4A35`), so it fills the records the scene walk reads. `0x240A` keeps the slot in `[0x22F0]`, zeroes `+0x22`, `+0x1C` and `+0x1E`, copies the position `+4..+0xF` to `0x2308`, and walks the scenery tree from `SS:0` through a second dispatch table, `DGROUP:0x584` (the draw walk uses `0x554`). Its type-14 handler `0x2631` (ground leaf) counts edge crossings of the leaf polygon (anchors' `x` at `+4`, `z` at `+8` of the anchor table in the work segment) against the slot's integer `x` and `z` (`[0x230A]`, `[0x2312]`) and on an odd count stores the leaf's plane address, leaf `+4` (`a`, `b`, `c`, 32-bit `D`, the words the draw walk's back-face test `0x23C5` reads), at slot `+0x1E` (`0x26BE`). The walk goes on to the next leaf, so the last containing leaf in walk order wins; a slot over no leaf keeps `+0x1E = 0`. Its type-10 handler `0x257A` (object) tests the object shape's bounds and walks the shape's collision boxes (header word `+0xA`, 16-byte records, tables `0x9FC`/`0xA08`); a box whose word `+0xE` is 8 runs `0x2A5B`, which (unless the probe's `y` test there fails) stores the object's `y` plus the box's word `+6` at `+0x20/+0x22` (`0x2A8C`). The box test `0x3047` pairs words `+2/+4` with `x`, `+6/+8` with `y`, `+0xA/+0xC` with `z`, so `+6` is the box's least `y`, its top in `PLAYONE`'s `y`-down axes. Box codes 1 to 7 instead store `code · 2` at `+0x1C` (a second hit at `+0x1D`) and the object node at `+0x18`/`+0x1A` (`0x2A2B`). Slot records are 2 × 40 of `0x5A` bytes at `DS:0x2B2A` (`0x3729`, `0x3768`), zero in the load image; allocation clears only bytes `+0` and `+1` (`0x3755`), and `0x37E2`, which clears `+0x04..+0x23`, has no static caller.
* **Create handler.** Opcodes `0x00..0x3F` go to `0x3CF2`: the slot pointer `[state + 2c + 0x2E]`, then the next stream byte stored verbatim at slot `+1` (`0x3CFD`); `0x3DDA` then reads the shape when bit 1 is set, the position when bit 3 is clear and the angles when bit 4 is clear. So slot byte `+1` is the create's `type_flags`.
* **View slot.** `0x11FC` stores `[state + 2·view_obj + 0x2E]` in `[0x22DE]`; `view_obj` is `c - 0x90` from opcodes `0x90..0x97` (`0x3D72`, taken only while `state + 0x1C` is nonzero; `0x3723` sets the snapshot's to `0xFFFF`). The scene walk compares the slot with `[0x22DE]` only before the actor queue `0x1A90` (`0x1E26`), and the shadow test `0x1E2F` follows for every slot. Census: 223 (film, slot, flags) cases in 160 films where the view slot has flags `0x1F` (186) or `0x17` (37) and a shape, for a total of 226,028 ticks; the slot is 0 (the stunt aircraft) in 144 of them; none of the films is one of the seven parity reference films (`CTRY`, `INTRO`, `LAND`, `BALLOON`, `TRAINTNL`, `BOMB`, `LOCATION`).
* **Altitude cutoff.** No instruction on the shadow path reads the actor's own `y` (`+8/+0xA`): not `0x1E2F`, `0x19FC` (which reads `+4/+6`, `+0xC/+0xE`, `+0x1E`, `+0x20/+0x22`), the cull `0x1B0C` (frustum and a distance-exponent test of the shadow point, `[0x229E] + 7`), `0x1C0D` or `0x1D5F`. The probe `0x240A` compares `y` only inside the tree's cube nodes (type 2, `0x2547`) and the collision boxes, where a miss leaves `+0x1E` at 0, which draws on `y = 0`, not nothing. Census: 827 re-creates in 129 films flip only `SIF_SHADOW` on an unchanged shape, 424 setting it and 378 clearing it (25 without a stream `y`); the last stream `y` has median −0.75 for both directions, and a shadow is set at `y` as far as −8060, so the films' flag changes do not follow a fixed height.
* **Projection.** `0x1C0D` replaces row 1 of `R` (`work:0x80D8..0x80DC`) with `-(a·row0 + c·row2) / b`. `R` is row-major on column vectors and the draw matrix is `0x80C0 · R` (`0x04E5`, `0x060A`, [`memory-map.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/memory-map.md)), so row 1 gives a vertex's world `y` offset: each vertex keeps its world `x` and `z` offset and takes the `y` of the plane through the shadow point parallel to the leaf's plane. That is a projection along world `y`, straight down. The tilt comes from `+0x1E` even when `+0x20/+0x22` supplied the height.
* **`work:0x806C` readers**, by VM slot (handler = slot `imm16 + 0x8240`): `0x92B2` is opcode `0x15F`'s entry and `0x93C2` opcode `0x132`'s, both normal builders that on their first run rewrite their own opcode word to `0x1241` (the `0x156` handler `0x9481`); in shadow mode they skip `4 + 6·count` bytes without computing or rewriting. `0x9EC5` is in `OP_DISC` (`0x105`, entry `0x9EBF`): with the mode set it skips its two operands, else it scales the radius by `work:0x8070` over the centre's depth, builds the span table with `0xD11D` and fills it flat through `0:8010`, after the register flag test that `OP_ANIMATE` also makes. `0x9F29` is the entry of `0x25B` (`OP_DISCSL`) and `0xA05C` the entry of `0x264` (`OP_PERFDISCSL`): each builds the same span table as its plain op (`0xD11D`; `0xA08E` for the perforated disc) and, instead of filling, takes a fourth operand `n`, stores the table's start at `work:0x423E + n` and advances the span arena pointer `work:0x1952` past it (`xchg`, `0x9F8E`, `0xA06C`); in shadow mode, or when the build fails, it skips its operands and stores 0 at `0x423E + n`. The ops at `0xA47F..0xA518` (`0x276`..`0x2AC`) read and combine those entries. `0xA9DC` is `OP_ANIMATE`'s entry (`0x1F8`), as [`sod-animate.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-animate.md) has it. The earlier text that called `0x92B2` and `0x93C2` parts of `OP_ANIMATE` was wrong.

**Verdict**: adopted as findings. `+0x1E` writer, `+0x20/+0x22` writer, create handler, `0x806C` readers and the projection direction `[Established]` (static; nothing run). That the view slot reaches `0x19FC` in the census films is `[Supported Inference]` (static path plus the stream census; no frame observed). No altitude test on `PLAYONE`'s shadow path `[Established]` (static); that the films' `SIF_SHADOW` flips do not encode one `[Bounded Observation]` (the census above). Where the handbook's cutoff lives stays open. The renderer differences (view-slot shadow, the plane for slots without `SIF_MOVES`, the collision-box height) go to a ready-for-human issue; nothing in the renderer changed here.
