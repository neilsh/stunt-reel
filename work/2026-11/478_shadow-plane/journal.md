# Journal: #478 shadow plane, altitude cutoff, view-slot shadow and `0x806C` readers

## 2026-11-14: the shadow plane writer, the view slot and the `0x806C` readers read statically

**Question**: the five questions of [#478](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/478): who writes actor slot `+0x1E` and `+0x20/+0x22`; whether the view camera's slot can reach the shadow queue; where the handbook's altitude cutoff lives; whether `0x1C0D` projects straight down, and which create handler fills slot byte `+1`; what the other `work:0x806C` readers do.

**Method**: `mise run disasm` over the unpatched load image (`build/re/playone.img` in the data root), `0x0000..0x8240` and `0x8240..0xD510`, at `4986b89`; dispatch tables read from `DGROUP` (image `0xD510`): the scenery walker's `0x554` and `0x584`, the collision walker's four three-entry tables at `0x9FC`, `0xA02`, `0xA08` and `0xA0E`, and the VM's nine-byte slots at image `0x8240` (`imm16 + 0x8240` is the handler). Two one-off censuses of the corpus films (529 distinct by SHA-256, 7 of them not walked to the end because the scratch walker stops on an unsupported opcode (`CORSAIR`, `JASON`, `PITTS`) or a truncated one (`MOVIE`, `ROD`, `SR71FLM`, `TUTORIAL`)): a slot re-created with the same shape and only `SIF_SHADOW` changed, with the slot's last stream `y` (the create's position or the latest `interp`); and, per slice, whether the `view_obj` slot (`0x90..0x97`, `c - 0x90`) has `SIF_EXISTS`, `SIF_DRAW` and `SIF_SHADOW` and a nonzero shape. No debugger run: every question the static read could decide was decided by it.

**Result**:

* **`+0x1E` and `+0x20/+0x22`.** Their only writer is the ground and collision probe `0x240A`, called once per slot from `0x1463` (`0x1492`). `0x1463` runs for each slot whose flag byte `+1` has bit 3 (`SIF_MOVES`) and a nonzero shape, and either byte `+0` bit 3 or `SIF_DRAW`; `0x4A17` runs it on the snapshot struct `0x4888` just before the frame draw `0x0EE9` (`0x4A35`), so it fills the records the scene walk reads. `0x240A` keeps the slot in `[0x22F0]`, zeroes `+0x22`, `+0x1C` and `+0x1E`, copies the position `+4..+0xF` to `0x2308`, and walks the scenery tree from `SS:0` through a second dispatch table, `DGROUP:0x584` (the draw walk uses `0x554`). Its type-14 handler `0x2631` (ground leaf) counts edge crossings of the leaf polygon (anchors' `x` at `+4`, `z` at `+8` of the anchor table in the work segment) against the slot's integer `x` and `z` (`[0x230A]`, `[0x2312]`) and on an odd count stores the leaf's plane address, leaf `+4` (`a`, `b`, `c`, 32-bit `D`, the words the draw walk's back-face test `0x23C5` reads), at slot `+0x1E` (`0x26BE`). The walk goes on to the next leaf, so the last containing leaf in walk order wins; a slot over no leaf keeps `+0x1E = 0`. Its type-10 handler `0x257A` (object) tests the object shape's bounds and walks the shape's collision records (header word `+0xA`; tables `0x9FC`, `0xA02`, `0xA08`, `0xA0E` by record type 0, 2, 4; types 0 and 4 are 12-byte records, type 2 the 16-byte box, tested by `0x2979` and `0x3047`); a box whose word `+0xE` is 8 runs `0x2A5B`, which (unless the probe's `y` test there fails) stores the object's `y` plus the box's word `+6` at `+0x20/+0x22` (`0x2A8C`). The box test `0x3047` pairs words `+2/+4` with `x`, `+6/+8` with `y`, `+0xA/+0xC` with `z`, so `+6` is the box's least `y`, its top in `PLAYONE`'s `y`-down axes. Box codes 1 to 7 instead store `code · 2` at `+0x1C` (a second hit at `+0x1D`) and the object node at `+0x18`/`+0x1A` (`0x2A2B`). Slot records are 2 × 40 of `0x5A` bytes at `DS:0x2B2A` (`0x3729`, `0x3768`), zero in the load image; allocation clears only bytes `+0` and `+1` (`0x3755`), and `0x37E2`, which clears `+0x04..+0x23`, has no static caller.
* **Create handler.** Opcodes `0x00..0x3F` go to `0x3CF2`: the slot pointer `[state + 2c + 0x2E]`, then the next stream byte stored verbatim at slot `+1` (`0x3CFD`); `0x3DDA` then reads the shape when bit 1 is set, the position when bit 3 is clear and the angles when bit 4 is clear. So slot byte `+1` is the create's `type_flags`.
* **View slot.** `0x11FC` stores `[state + 2·view_obj + 0x2E]` in `[0x22DE]`; `view_obj` is `c - 0x90` from opcodes `0x90..0x97` (`0x3D72`, taken only while `state + 0x1C` is nonzero; `0x3723` sets the snapshot's to `0xFFFF`). The scene walk compares the slot with `[0x22DE]` only before the actor queue `0x1A90` (`0x1E26`), and the shadow test `0x1E2F` follows for every slot. Census: 223 (film, slot, flags) cases in 160 films where the view slot has flags `0x1F` (186) or `0x17` (37) and a shape, for a total of 218,633 ticks (the 29 slices whose tick byte is the `0xFF` end sentinel not counted); the slot is 0 in 144 of the cases, not always the stunt aircraft (`CHASE.FLM`'s slot 0 is shape `0x4131`); none of the films is one of the seven parity reference films (`CTRY`, `INTRO`, `LAND`, `BALLOON`, `TRAINTNL`, `BOMB`, `LOCATION`).
* **Altitude cutoff.** No instruction in the shadow draw's setup reads the actor's own `y` (`+8/+0xA`): not `0x1E2F`, `0x19FC` (which reads `+4/+6`, `+0xC/+0xE`, `+0x1E`, `+0x20/+0x22`), the cull `0x1B0C` (frustum and a distance-exponent test of the shadow point, `[0x229E] + 7`), `0x1C0D` or `0x1D5F`'s setup. The probe `0x240A` compares `y` only inside the tree's cube nodes (type 2, `0x2547`) and the collision boxes, where a miss leaves `+0x1E` at 0, which draws on `y = 0`, not nothing. Census: 827 re-creates in 129 films flip only `SIF_SHADOW` on an unchanged shape, 435 setting it and 392 clearing it (11 and 14 of them with no stream `y` yet). 397 of them, in 55 films, have `SIF_DRAW` clear, so the walk skips them at `0x1E13` (196 sets, 201 clears, median `y` −0.75 each). The 430 on drawn shapes, in 104 films, are 239 sets and 191 clears, all with a stream `y`, medians −4.30 and −4.72, reaching −8060 and −6034: the films' flag changes do not follow one height. The display-list run inside `0x1D5F` was not reread for a `y` test.
* **Projection.** `0x1C0D` replaces row 1 of `R` (`work:0x80D8..0x80DC`) with `-(a·row0 + c·row2) / b`. `R` is row-major on column vectors and the draw matrix is `0x80C0 · R` (`0x04E5`, `0x060A`, [`memory-map.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/memory-map.md)), so row 1 gives a vertex's world `y` offset: each vertex keeps its world `x` and `z` offset and takes the `y` of the plane through the shadow point parallel to the leaf's plane. That is a projection along world `y`, straight down. The tilt comes from `+0x1E` even when `+0x20/+0x22` supplied the height.
* **`work:0x806C` readers**, all fifteen (handler = the opcode's VM slot `imm16 + 0x8240`). Gateways, not opcodes: `0x7FD2` (line, `DI = 0` for `work:0x41EA`), `0x7FFB` (returns without calling driver slot `[0x2248]`; the one-pixel draws call it: the radius-0 branches of `OP_SPHERE` `0x0FC` at `0x9E21` and opcode `0x2D9` at `0xA476`, and opcode `0x2D0` at `0xA3FC`), `0x8010` (test at `0x8015`) and `0x8040` (flat polygon, `CX = 0` for `work:0x41E8`). Polygon handlers taking the flat branch (`call [0x8080]`, `lcall 0:8010`): `0x995C` (`GVPOLY`, `0x168`) and `0x9A24` (`GVPOLY16`, `0x2BE`), which then step over their shade words (`count & 0xF` words); `0x97B1` (opcode `0x333`, handler `0x97AA`) and `0x9896` (`0x33C`, handler `0x988F`), which act only while `work:0x806B` is set (else `0x333` skips ten bytes and `0x33C` eight) and after the fill go straight to `es lodsw; jmp ax`, with no shade words. Skippers: `0x92B2` and `0x93C2`, the entries of `0x15F` and `0x132`, normal builders that on their first run rewrite their own opcode word to `0x1241` (the `0x156` handler `0x9481`) and in shadow mode skip `4 + 6·count` bytes without computing or rewriting; `0x9EC5` in `OP_DISC` (`0x105`, entry `0x9EBF`), which skips its two operands, else after its register flag test (`0x9ECC`) scales the radius by `work:0x8070` over the centre's depth, builds the span table with `0xD11D` and fills it through `0:8010`; `0xA040`, `OP_PERFDISK` (`0x10E`), which skips its three; `0xA9DC`, `OP_ANIMATE` (`0x1F8`), as [`sod-animate.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-animate.md) has it. Sliced discs: `0x9F29` is the entry of `0x25B` (`OP_DISCSL`) and `0xA05C` of `0x264` (`OP_PERFDISCSL`); each builds its plain op's span table (`0xD11D`; through `0xA08E` for the perforated disc) and, instead of filling, takes a last operand `n` (the third for `0x25B`, the fourth for `0x264`), stores the table's start at `work:0x423E + n` and advances the span arena pointer `work:0x1952` past it (`xchg`, `0x9F8E`, `0xA06C`). Each stores 0 at `0x423E + n` in shadow mode and when its build fails: `OP_DISCSL` on its centre register's flag (`0x9F36`), `work:0x4218 = 0` (`0x9F5E`) or an off-screen `0xD11D`; `OP_PERFDISCSL` when `0xA08E` sets carry (centre depth under `0x80`, the tests at `0xA156`/`0xA1AB`, or `0xD11D` off screen). Of the ops at `0xA47F..0xA517`, `0x276`/`0x27F` push and pop `work:0x1952`, `0x288` fills one entry through `0:8040` and skips a zero one (`0xA498`), and `0x291`..`0x2AC` combine entries. `OP_DISC` is the only disc op that tests the mode: `OP_SPHERE` (fill at `0x9DF3`), opcode `0x2D9` (`0xA448`) and opcode `0x252`'s span table do not. The earlier text that called `0x92B2` and `0x93C2` parts of `OP_ANIMATE` was wrong.

**Verdict**: adopted as findings. `+0x1E` writer, `+0x20/+0x22` writer, create handler, `0x806C` readers and the projection direction `[Established]` (static; nothing run). That the view slot reaches `0x19FC` in the census films is `[Supported Inference]` (static path plus the stream census; no frame observed). No altitude test in the setup of `PLAYONE`'s shadow draw, `0x1E2F` to `0x1D5F`'s, `[Established]` (static; the display-list run inside `0x1D5F` not reread), though the probe's walk tests `y` and can leave a high actor on the `y = 0` plane; that the films' `SIF_SHADOW` flips do not follow one height `[Bounded Observation]` (the census above). Where the handbook's cutoff lives stays open. The renderer differences (view-slot shadow, the plane for slots without `SIF_MOVES`, the collision-box height) go to a ready-for-human issue; nothing in the renderer changed here.
