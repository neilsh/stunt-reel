# Journal: PLAYONE's back-face cull

## 2026-11-20: the polygon span builder read for a facing test

- **Question**: Where does `PLAYONE.EXE` decide that a shape polygon faces away, if anywhere; is the polygon's outline skipped with its fill; how is a zero-area or edge-on polygon treated; and does a two-vertex polygon have a facing test ([#850](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/850))?
- **Method**: The unpatched image read with `mise run re-tool -- disasm` at `0e281a4e`: the clipping variant `0xC50E..0xC9D3` (with its edge clip `0xC6AF`, corner insertion `0xC65A` and cover test `0xC88E`), the edge builder `0xC9D4` with its vertex-rejection exit `0xC9CB`, the span-table builder `0xCA5D..0xCF22` (both walks of the `GVPOLY` entry, `0xCAE7` and `0xCB96`, and of the `POLYZ` entry, `0xCC53` and `0xCDAE`), the handlers `0x995C` (`OP_GVPOLY`), `0x9A24` (`GVPOLY16`), `0x9AF4..0x9B76` and `0x9B78` (`POLYZ`), the gateway `0x8010`, and the fillers `0x7571` and `0x75DB`. `mise run re-tool -- xref 0x445A` for the vertex flag byte. No live trace, no DOSBox-X session and no capture.
- **Result**: No routine on these paths computes a normal, a cross product or a signed area. The span-table builder walks the vertex list backward from the topmost vertex into each row's left word (`+0x0A`, stride 4, at `[work:0x1952]`) and forward into its right word (`+0x0C`), and both fillers skip a row whose right word is less than its left (`0x75A1` `jl`; `0x7612` and `0x76DC` `inc ax; jg`). So a polygon whose vertices run anticlockwise on screen fills no row where its two edges are apart. In the `POLYZ` walks the backward walker writes the left end of each row's run of the edge and the forward walker its right end (`0xCCBB`, `0xCD40`, `0xCE17`, `0xCEA2`), so a back-facing polygon still draws a row where those ends cross: the row of a lone top or bottom vertex (at least the vertex's own pixel) and any row where the face is narrower than its edges' runs. Polygon spans are the only edges `PLAYONE` draws for a polygon, so its "outline" goes with the fill. A polygon of one row (`[0x41D0] == [0x41D2]`) is drawn by `0xCAB4` as one span from its least to its greatest column whatever its order. A two-vertex polygon's two walks run along the same edge, the backward walker taking each row's left end and the forward walker its right end, so it draws as a line whatever its vertex order: it has no facing. The clipper `0xC50E` keeps the stream's vertex order, inserts screen corners only clockwise (`0xC65A` steps the side index up), and joins the same builder at `0xCA5E`; a polygon it finds covering the view (`0xC88E`) becomes the four screen corners in clockwise order and fills the view whatever its own order. The vertex-rejection exit `0xC9CB` tests the vertex's clip-flag byte (register `+0x0A`), not facing: `POLYZ` then draws nothing and `GVPOLY`'s Gouraud route retries through the flat route. The `GVPOLY` entry's forward walk jumps to the `POLYZ` forward walk at `0xCDE6` unless the top row is clipped (`0xCBA6`), so § 8.9's "left equals right on every row" holds only for a top-clipped `GVPOLY`.
- **Verdict**: control (no renderer change). The cull is the span test, not a facing test; the outline goes with the fill except where the ends cross; an edge-on polygon is a line.
