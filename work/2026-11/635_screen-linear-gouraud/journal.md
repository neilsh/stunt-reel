# Journal

## 2026-11-16: The screen-linear ratio for the clipper's Gouraud polygons

- **Question**: does rebuilding a clipper-broken Gouraud polygon's `shade` as the ratio of two perspective-correct varyings, `shade * w` and `w`, make lavapipe draw the GPU's screen-linear gradient at BALLOON 145 (where #595's perspective copy left 1,090 of 4,044 pixels one index off), while leaving the RTX 4070's render stores byte-identical to `main`'s (#635)?
- **Method**: dsh-built branch `neilsh/635-screen-linear-gouraud`, based on `origin/main` `3a7afb76`, then merged with `857f80a8` (merge `762680e0`) and with `e6fb29ce`, #650's eye-relative transforms (merge `e6951d21`, which also changes the test to project through `rotation_proj`). GPU: full `mise run parity` on the pre-merge tree (`ba40ea63`) and on the merge, SHA-256 of each film's `frames.zst`, `layers.zst` and `palettes.zst` against `main`'s stores at `3a7afb76` and `857f80a8`, and `mise run judge --track evidence`. Lavapipe (mesa-dist-win 25.2.8, `llvmpipe (LLVM 21.1.7, 256 bits)`): `mise run parity -- --software-only` on `ba40ea63` against the `3a7afb76` baseline, `mise run parity-residue` over the index planes against the GPU's, and a local sentinel build (non-finite `shade` draws 250, reverted) marking the clipper-broken pixels at BALLOON 145. The GPU test `a_gouraud_ground_polygon_under_a_low_eye_keeps_its_gradient` compares every ground pixel more than one pixel from the quad's diagonal against the affine function of pixel position through the quad's projected corners, allowing a one-index flip only within 0.02 of a rounding boundary. Figures are the PR's "Measurements" section (#648) and the dsh worker's reports.
- **Result**:
  - GPU: all three files byte-identical on all seven films, 21 of 21, against `3a7afb76`'s stores (on `ba40ea63`), against `857f80a8`'s (on the merge `762680e0`) and, after #650 (eye-relative transforms), against `origin/main` `e6fb29ce`'s stores (on the merge `e6951d21`; the baseline is the coordinator's GPU render, the six films other than LOCATION rendered one film at a time and LOCATION alone under the parity lock); the judge moves no scored frame.
  - BALLOON 145 on lavapipe: 4,044 clipper-broken pixels; before, 2,954 match the GPU and 1,090 are one index lower (977 are 169 against 170, 113 are 170 against 171), 1,097 pixels of the frame differ; after, 4,044 match and 7 pixels of the frame differ. The GPU draws 170 on 3,784 of them, 169 on 147 and 171 on 113 (3,784 + 147 + 113 = 4,044).
  - Lavapipe against the GPU, mean differing index-plane pixels per tick before then after: only BALLOON moves, 41.0 to 40.0 (largest 1,097 at 145 to 241 at 144); the other six films, and LAND 227, BOMB 1910, LOCATION 2599 and 2859, have unchanged differing-pixel counts. Diffing the two lavapipe stores directly (`mise run parity-residue`, before against after, over the 1,179 BALLOON ticks) gives 1,092 differing pixels on BALLOON and none on the other six films: 1,090 at tick 145, all inside the 4,044 sentinel-marked pixels, and 2 at tick 143, where lavapipe's 49 becomes the GPU's 50 (that tick's differences from the GPU go from 50 to 48), not traced to a broken polygon. `mean_ssim_scored` and `mean_iou_scored` differ by 0.0000000 (seven decimals) on all seven films and no scored frame moves; lavapipe was not re-rendered after the merges with `857f80a8` and `e6fb29ce`.
  - The GPU test fails on lavapipe with #595's shader (47,518 of 47,680 ground pixels) and passes with this one (0 of them), on lavapipe and on WARP.
  - `mise run parity-repin -- --class lavapipe` on a clean tree would move the seven lavapipe SSIMs by -0.0000370 (BALLOON) to +0.0000082 (LOCATION), the mesa-dist-win against Ubuntu spread of [`lavapipe.md`](../../../research/parity/lavapipe.md) section 4, and swap the rows' adapter to LLVM 21.1.7. It was not committed; the owner decided on 2026-11-16 that local runs do not repin the `lavapipe` class (the coordinator's brief for this note; #649 measures how far builds differ pixel by pixel).
  - Not run: the issue's optional experiment (the ratio everywhere on the GPU, counting index-plane pixels that differ from `main`), because the parity lock went to other full runs.
- **Verdict**: adopted, on the evidence-backed track of #595: no scored frame moves on either backend, and the change follows PLAYONE's screen-linear filler rule ([`terrain-shade.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/terrain-shade.md) section 6), moves BALLOON 145 (rendered, not scored) to the GPU's pixels, and makes the gradient test pass on lavapipe. The judge's winner line on both backends is `none adopts (best run=candidate: no film rises)`.
