# Journal: ZLINETO lines on a coplanar facet draw under it

## 2026-11-17: lines lying on an earlier facet are decals of it

- **Question**: Since the flat-ground pass ([ADR 0045](../../../docs/adr/0045-flat-ground-is-painted-in-walk-order.md)) and the ordered decal pass ([ADR 0046](../../../docs/adr/0046-coplanar-decals-are-drawn-in-an-ordered-pass.md)) landed, are `0x037C`'s yard lines at `LOCATION` tick 4665 still buried, and by what? Does marking a `MOVETO`/`ZLINETO` segment that lies on an earlier facet of its shape as a decal, the issue's `line_lies_on_facet`, still cost `INTRO` tick 848?
- **Method**: track **evidence**: `PLAYONE` draws a line when `ZLINETO` (`0x9696`) runs, through `0xBBD4`, the gateway `0x7FD2` and the driver's Bresenham line `0x7511`, with no depth test ([`sod-vm-generators.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm-generators.md) section 8.10), so a line later in the stream than the facet it lies on covers it, as a later coplanar polygon does (`POLYZ` `0x9B78`, [`render-model.md`](../../../research/parity/render-model.md) section 3). Switch `DBG_LINE_DECAL` (not committed) in `identify_coplanar_decals` (`crates/stunt-core/src/sod/decal.rs`): `1` flags a line segment as a decal when both its ends lie within 0.1 mm of the plane of an earlier non-line polygon of the stream and the bounds meet; `0` is `main`. On lavapipe (`--software-only`, llvmpipe), base `ce9fe79`. Pictures: `mise run frame -- --film location --ticks 4665 --software-only --var DBG_LINE_DECAL=0,1`, the same for `intro` tick 848. Targets, quoted before the sweep runs:

  `--track evidence --target location:4600-4730 --target intro:837-859 --target traintnl:12-133`

  Round 2, after round 1's BOMB falls (below): same track and targets, `DBG_LINE_DECAL=0,1,2`, where `2` flags a line only when the earlier facet it lies on is itself a decal (the issue's case: `0x037C`'s lines after the `0x027A` decal, `TRAINTNL`'s rails after the `132` tie).

- **Result**:
  - **`0x037C`'s lines are still buried after #446.** At `LOCATION` tick 4665 the field shows no yard line on `main` and all of them with `DBG_LINE_DECAL=1`, as in the capture ([picture](images/location-4665-line-decals-variant1.png): capture, `0`, `1`). The cause is the line's classification, not the base-then-decal grouping as such: a line that is no decal is drawn in a pass, or a ground-pass node, before its decal's, and once it is a decal the ordered decal pass and the ground pass both keep stream order, so nothing else needed changing.
