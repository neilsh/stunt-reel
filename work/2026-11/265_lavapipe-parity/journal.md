# Journal: lavapipe parity beside the GPU

## 2026-11-15: Lavapipe beside the GPU on one crates tree

- **Question**: why local lavapipe (mesa-dist-win 25.2.8, `llvmpipe (LLVM 21.1.7, 256 bits)`) scored LOCATION 0.6344817 against the GPU's 0.8291597 and the cloud row's 0.8291972, while the other six films sat within 0.002 of both; whether local and cloud lavapipe agree; whether the CTRY 422 shadow is solid on the GPU; what the clipper bug does to Gouraud polygons; and whether Mesa 26.2.6 still makes the NaN (#265 steps 2-6).
- **Method**: `crates` tree `52d84e6` (branch `neilsh/265-lavapipe-beside-gpu` at `ac145f04`), RTX 4070 laptop. The six films' and the first LOCATION run's stores and reports are PR A's (`f87673f9`, same tree). Index planes read from the render stores (`RenderStore.frame`) and compared pixel by pixel, GPU against lavapipe. Single ticks rendered through `parity_pipeline.render_film` (as `mise run frame` does, `--at-ticks`) and a `--tick-range 12..=600` LOCATION span, with `VK_DRIVER_FILES` naming only the lavapipe ICD and `--software-only`. A diagnostic build in a separate target directory read `DBG_SHADE` (never committed): 1 removes the guard, 2 is a sentinel (non-finite `shade` 250, below -0.5 251, above 255.5 252), 3 draws every polygon at its flat `palette_index`. Each mode rendered CTRY 400, 422 and LOCATION 3994, 4005, 4445, 4467 on the GPU, mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 and 26.2.6 (`mesa3d-26.2.6-release-msvc.7z`, SHA-256 `b2e00615feaba45942343c265cc8c5f576142ca682c1ac6661922d4b72492877`, matching GitHub's digest; `llvmpipe (LLVM 23.1.4, 256 bits)`); BALLOON 145, LAND 227, BOMB 1910 and LOCATION 2599, 2859 on the GPU and 25.2.8. `draw_pipe_clip.c` fetched from `gitlab.freedesktop.org` at `mesa-25.2.8` and `mesa-26.2.6`, and its commit list on `main` from the GitLab API, from this machine. LOCATION re-rendered whole on 25.2.8 with `mise run parity -- --films location --software-only --no-build`, with this checkout's own guarded release build.
- **Result**:
  - The first LOCATION run's store is guard-free. It differs from the GPU on 8,250 of 8,251 ticks, 6,038 of them by more than 1,000 pixels, the 15 largest index pairs all to index 0, over ocean, terrain and props alike (layer planes unchanged), yet single-tick renders of 3994, 4005, 4445 and 4467 with the guard differ from the GPU by 17 to 25 pixels, and so does the 12..=600 span (no index-0 mass). With the guard removed, those four ticks reproduce the first run's damage exactly: 3994 195→0 43,216, 193→0 1,147, 162→0 217; 4005 41,512, 773, 103; 4445 195→0 41,061, 163→0 37; 4467 195→0 41,056. The other worktree's `target/release/stuntreel.exe` was built at 18:16:24, during the guard-removed GPU run, and the LOCATION run started about 18:27 with `--no-build`; that exe's embedded `fragment_index` has no guard branch (this checkout's has it).
  - Re-rendered with the guard, LOCATION on 25.2.8 scores 0.8292054 (IoU 0.9784023, index agreement 0.8405), +0.0000457 from the GPU's 0.8291597 and +0.0000082 from the cloud's 0.8291972; 2682 s, adapter `llvmpipe (LLVM 21.1.7, 256 bits)`; its store differs from the GPU's by 147.4 pixels per tick on average.
  - Local against cloud lavapipe, `mean_ssim_scored` local minus cloud: CTRY +0.0000068, INTRO +0.0000006, LAND +0.0000001, BALLOON -0.0000370, TRAINTNL -0.0000002, BOMB +0.0000030; IoU differences at most 0.0000090 (BALLOON). Close, not identical at seven decimals.
  - Guard removed, index 0 (sentinel: all non-finite) on lavapipe: CTRY 400 41,933 (25.2.8) and 41,934 (26.2.6); CTRY 422 43,117 and 410; LOCATION 3994 44,580 and 44,427; 4005 42,389 and 42,388; 4445 41,098 and 41,122; 4467 41,057 and 100. The sentinel draws 250 on all of them but the index-0 pixels that differ from the GPU with the guard on too (0 to 5 per tick: CTRY 400 0 and 1, CTRY 422 2 and 5, LOCATION 0). Sentinel 251 and 252: 0 everywhere, both builds; on the GPU 250, 251 and 252 are 0 at every tick rendered, and its guard-removed render equals its guarded one. `interp()` is the same text at both tags; the only hunk between them is `SWAP` in `do_clip_tri`, and the file's last commit on `main` is `8a1a4103` (2025-07-23, "treewide: use SWAP macro").
  - CTRY 422: the hero's shadow is solid on the GPU, 25.2.8 and 26.2.6 (index-0 pixels in actor layer 16: 1,342, 1,343, 1,346); lavapipe differs from the GPU by 25 and 23 pixels in the whole frame. The current CTRY reference holds 419 and 430, not 422.
  - Remaining differences, guard on, PR A's stores: mean differing pixels per tick CTRY 22.6, INTRO 29.3, LAND 119.2, BALLOON 43.5, TRAINTNL 62.5, BOMB 98.6. Largest solid regions: BALLOON 145 (GPU 170/169/171, lavapipe 168 on 4,044 pixels; sentinel 250 on exactly those 4,044), LAND 227 (13→195 on 3,222; sentinel 250 on 2,686 of the GPU's 13 pixels, the other 536 still 13→195 with the sentinel), BOMB 1910 (7→35 on 506 in actor layer 17; sentinel finite there; all-flat mode still 7 against 35). INTRO's largest is 115 interior pixels at 2723. LOCATION (rerun): 2599 and 2859, 14 and 169 swapped on 5,519 and 5,626 prop-layer pixels; at 2599 the sentinel marks only 11 pixels non-finite (none of the swapped ones), at 2859 18,847, the 5,626 swapped pixels among them; in all-flat mode lavapipe still differs from the GPU by 5,638 and 5,736 pixels, as with the guard.
- **Verdict**: control (no renderer change). LOCATION's loss was a stale guard-removed binary, not a lavapipe fault the guard misses; with the guard, local lavapipe is within the 0.002 margin of the GPU on all seven films. Mesa 26.2.6 still makes the NaN. Gouraud polygons the clipper breaks draw flat (BALLOON 145, LAND 227); BOMB 1910 and LOCATION 2599 and 2859 draw another polygon on top, not another shade of the same one; at LOCATION 2859 the polygon lavapipe draws there is one whose shade the clipper made non-finite, so whether the clipper also moved its depth is open. The CTRY 422 patches do not recur on this tree.
