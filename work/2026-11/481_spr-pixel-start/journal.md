# Journal: SPR pixel start

## 2026-11-14: where an SPR image's pixels start

- **Question**: Is byte 4..5 of an image a third header word (pixels from byte 6, no trailer), as `PLAYONE`'s `OP_ANIMATE` blitter reads it, or the first pixels with a trailer word at the end, as `spr.ksy` had it? Do `STUNT.EXE` and `MAKEONE.EXE` read it the same way?
- **Method**: Static, on `origin/main` at `4986b897`. A scratch scan (`mise exec -- uv run python`) of every `.RES` item, `.ALL` chunk and `.SPR` file under the data root, deduplicated by content: the word at byte 4 and the last word of each `width × height + 6`-byte chunk; then every chunk with a non-zero word at byte 4 walked as span rows (below). The SpriteMaker samples decoded against their `.PCX` sources. `mise run disasm` on the unpatched `PLAYONE` load image at `0x7C00..0x7FD0`; `STUNT.EXE` and `MAKEONE.EXE` (unpatched and Patch #3) and Patch #3 `PLAYONE.EXE` unpacked with `mise run unlzexe` to scratch and searched for the blitters' byte sequences, their loaders disassembled with `re_tool.py disasm --image`.
- **Result**:
  - 128 unique chunks are `width × height + 6` bytes. Of the 125 unique ones in the archives that hold a zero word at byte 4, 50 end in a non-zero word (39 in `FASTSPR.RES`, 11 in `SPRITES.RES`; for example the ten 7×8 frames `0x8013`..`0x801C` end in `0x3E3E`). A trailer word of 0 is therefore not a property of the format; a zero third word is.
  - Three chunks have a non-zero word at byte 4: `dethstar.spr` (`0xFFFF`), `MACLOGO.SPR` (`0x0101`) and `DSTAR.SPR` (`0x3434`, also item `0x1E00` of `SITools/GameReady/Death.RES.RES`). All three end in `0x0000`. `MACLOGO.SPR` and `DSTAR.SPR` equal their `.PCX` sources' pixels from byte 4, byte for byte (`MacLogo.PCX`, `DStar.PCX`); `dethstar.spr` differs from `DethStar.PCX` in 74 of 250,000 bytes from byte 4, and in 114,387 from byte 6. So `PCX2SPR` writes width, height, the pixels and a zero word.
  - `PLAYONE`'s unscaled draw tests the word: `0x7C2E` `cmp word [es:6], 0` (chunk byte 4), and a zero word goes to `0x7C39`, which copies `width` bytes a row with `rep movsb` from `es:8`; a non-zero one goes to `0x7CBA`, which reads each row from `es:8` as spans (a count byte, a `u16` column, `count` pixels, a zero count ends the row; the top-clip skip at `0x7CD9` steps the same way). The scaled `OP_ANIMATE` blitter `0x7DE6` never reads `es:6`.
  - 58 unique chunks with a non-zero word at byte 4 (all of them `2`; 8 in `FASTSPR.RES`, 50 in `SPRITES.RES`) parse as `height` span rows that end exactly at the chunk's end, every span inside the width.
  - The rewritten `spr.ksy` parses each of the 125 raw and 58 span-coded unique archive images to exactly its length.
  - The flag test, both entries and the scaled blitter's opening bytes recur in all six images: `PLAYONE` unpatched `0x7C2E`/`0x7C39`/`0x7CBA`/`0x7DE6`, Patch #3 `0x6F37`/`0x6F42`/`0x6FC3`/`0x70EF`; `STUNT` unpatched `0xEF83`/`0xEF8E`/`0xF00F`/`0xF13B`, Patch #3 `0xF1AE`/`0xF1B9`/`0xF23A`/`0xF366`; `MAKEONE` unpatched `0x7F01`/`0x7F0C`/`0x7F8D`/`0x80B9`, Patch #3 `0x716A`/`0x7175`/`0x71F6`/`0x7322`. `MAKEONE`'s loader adds 2 to the chunk size and sets `DI = 2` (`0x4C24`, `0x4C31`) as `PLAYONE`'s does; `STUNT`'s adds 2 and reads with `DX = 2` (`0x9D99`, `0x9DAA`).
- **Verdict**: control (static reading). The game's layout is width, height, an encoding word, then the body from byte 6, with no trailer: `[Established]` for `PLAYONE` from the instructions; `[Supported Inference]` for `STUNT` and `MAKEONE`, whose identical byte sequences were matched, not traced from a caller. `PCX2SPR`'s layout differs, so the game draws a SpriteMaker sprite two pixels to the left, its last two pixels transparent: `[Supported Inference]`, not observed.
