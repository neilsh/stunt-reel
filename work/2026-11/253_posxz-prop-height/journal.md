# Journal: props reached only through SP_POSXZ

## 2026-11-11: how PLAYONE gives an SP_POSXZ prop its height

- **Question**: How does `PLAYONE.EXE` give a height to a prop that the scene tree reaches only through `SP_POSXZ` nodes, which add no `y` ([`[BK-MAP-07]`](../../../research/formats/auxiliary/scenery.md#4-open-questions--analysis-backlog), [#253](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/253))? Does it sample the terrain at the prop's `(x, z)`? Which props in `CTRY.FLM` and `INTRO.FLM` does this affect?
- **Method**: `mise run disasm` over the walker's start `0x1E3A..0x1E89`, the `SP_POS` and `SP_POSXZ` handlers `0x1E89..0x1EC0`, `SP_LEAF` `0x220B..0x2290`, `SP_GROUND` `0x2360..0x23D0`, the actor draw `0x1D5F..0x1D96` and the second walker `0x2430..0x2640` (jump table `DS:0x0584`), at `70cf324a`. `mise run xref -- 0x49F8` and `-- 0x49F6` for every write of the accumulated `y`. A scan of the disassembly below `0x8240` and of the VM range `0x8240..0xB85C` for reads of `[es:6]`. A scratch script walked the image's tree (`scene_tree.tree_segment`), marked each placement whose path holds no `SP_POS` with a non-zero `y`, and compared each prop with the `SP_GLEAF` polygons under it, fanned from their first corner. Each shape's lowest vertex came from `stuntreel export-gltf --shape`, whose vertices include the header lift. Archives: the vault captures' `CTRY.ALL` and `INTRO.ALL`.
- **Result**:
  - The walk starts at `0x1E43` with the accumulated position `DS:0x49F2..0x49FC` set to the negated view eye `0x2A68..0x2A72`. `0x1EA8` (`SP_POSXZ`) adds `[bp+2]` to `0x49F4` and `[bp+4]` to `0x49FC` only. Writes to `0x49F8` in this walker come only from `SP_POS` (`0x1E93`) and the save and restore around subtrees (`0x1EC7/0x1EDF`, `0x1F6E`, `0x1FA5`, `0x1FD7`); `SP_GROUND` (`0x2360`) culls against the eye but leaves the position unchanged.
  - `SP_LEAF` (`0x2216`) calls `0x4C41`, which returns the shape segment in `ES`, then adds `−[es:6] >> [es:8]` as a 16.16 value to `0x49F6/0x49F8` (`0x2219..0x2235`) before projecting at `0x31CF`. The shape's header sits at `ES:2` (the stream starts at `ES:0x000C`), so `[es:6]` is `ground_y_offset` and `[es:8]` is `scale_index`. The far `SP_1NODE` stand-in does the same at `0x2160`. The collision walker (`0x2434`, table `0x0584`) treats `SP_POSXZ` the same way (`0x24A0`) and tests against the same `y − ground_y_offset >> scale_index` (`0x25A1`, `0x26F3`). No instruction in either walker reads the terrain or the anchor table.
  - The actor draw `0x1D5F` reads `[es:2]`, `[es:4]` and `[es:8]` and never `[es:6]`. Nothing in the VM range reads `[es:6]`.
  - Census over the image tree (140,922 distinct placements; 140,174 have no `SP_POS` `y` on their path). For shapes in `CTRY.ALL`, 22,214 of 22,225 placements set no `y`, across 118 shapes; for `INTRO.ALL`, 73,057 of 73,750, across 219 shapes. All but a few sit at `y = 0`, and the Ground under them is also at sea level. With the header lift applied, the lowest vertex of a prop over a polygon, rounded to whole metres above the polygon nearest it, falls in these bins: `CTRY` 0 m 14,610, 1 m 5,130, 2 m 2,332, 6 m 127; `INTRO` −1 m 1,496, 0 m 36,041, 1 m 25,855, 2 m 9,341, 3 m 8, 4 m 120, 6 m 169, plus five `OP_TAP` shapes far below. Every prop more than 1.5 m off is listed here. Above: shapes `0x4A6`/`0x4A8` at 6.2 m, a run of `0x590`–`0x593` segments at 6.0 m, `0x24E`, `0x253`–`0x259` and `0x58C` at 3.5 to 3.7 m, a few `0x75E`/`0x769`–`0x76D` and `0x503`/`0x509` at 1.7 to 2.4 m. Below: only `OP_TAP` shapes. The ground lookup takes one fanned polygon per point, so a prop on a slope or between overlapping polygons can read up to a metre off. The lookup takes, for each prop, the polygon under it nearest in height to its lowest vertex. Shape `0x0401` (`ground_y_offset` 2624 at scale 6, so 41.0 m), a vertical face 300 m wide spanning 0 to 82 m, stands at `(1685, 0, 2301)` over polygons at sea level and at 41.0 m. The props that do sit below the Ground at their origin are the `OP_TAP` hill shapes `0x0202`–`0x0208` and `0x0402`–`0x0408`, which take their corners from the anchor table.
- **Verdict**: control. `PLAYONE` samples no terrain. An `SP_POSXZ` prop's height is its ancestors' `SP_POS` `y` plus the header's `ground_y_offset >> scale_index`, and the tree and the shapes already carry the right heights. `world.rs` places props the same way (the decoder adds the lift to every vertex; a prop is unrotated, so this is the same thing). So no `CTRY`/`INTRO` prop is misplaced by this rule, and the hypothesis is rejected. A test pins the walk (`test_traverse_scn_posxz_keeps_the_ancestors_height_and_samples_no_terrain`). No render changed, so no parity score was taken. The actor path's missing `[es:6]` read is the `PLAYONE` rule behind the heuristic in [`framing-and-actors.md`](../../../research/parity/framing-and-actors.md) section 1 that draws actors without the lift.
