# Journal: OP_PERFDISK rows against `0xD11D`

## 2026-11-22: the disc's rows read out of `0xD11D`, and three candidates read at the wheel rows

- **Question**: Why do `INTRO.ALL` `0x7F04`'s wheel discs draw 1 to 3 rows taller than the capture, reaching 1 to 2 rows lower and up to 2 px wider ([#898](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/898))?
- **Method**: local-windows, RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, `origin/main` `7728e1e` (no other worker rendering at the time; GPU idle before the first render). Static: `mise run re-tool -- disasm 0xD11D 0xD510` (the span builder) and `disasm 0xA040 0xA2D0` (the handler and its builder `0xA08E`), unpatched image. Rows: temporary, uncommitted switch `DBG_DISC_HALFOPEN` in `outline.rs` (both `append_outline` and `append_decal_fans`), `scene_renderer.rs` (`outlines_in_world`) and a shader-source substitution in `pipeline.rs`; `mise run frame -- --film <film> --ticks <ticks> --var DBG_DISC_HALFOPEN=…`, then a scratch script printing, per row of a box, the first and last x of the given palette indices in the capture's index plane and each store's, the row and column extents, and an *edge error*: the sum over the box's rows of `|left − capture left| + |right − capture right|`, a row present on one side only counting its width. Variants:
  - `0`: `main`.
  - `1`: disc rim vertices (`VERTEX_FLAG_DISC`) left out of every outline, so a disc draws its filled triangles alone (candidate 1 of the brief).
  - `2`: as 1, and every rim vertex moved up half a pixel.
  - `3`: as 1, and every rim vertex moved by the offset that puts the disc's projected centre where `PLAYONE` puts it, as below: `x` to `160 + trunc(ox) + 0.5`, `y` to `floor(rows / 2) + trunc(oy)`, with `ox` and `oy` the centre's offset in pixels from the projection centre (`160`, `rows / 2`).
  - `4`: as 3, and every rim vertex pulled half a pixel toward the centre (row reads only, no sweep).
  - `5`: as 3, and every rim vertex's vertical offset from the centre `dy` scaled by `1 − 0.5 / d`, `d` its distance from the centre in pixels, so the top and bottom of the rim come half a pixel in.
  - `6`: as 5, with the disc drawn as a 64-gon (`DISC_SEGMENTS`) instead of a 16-gon.
- **Static result** (`[Established]` for the instruction sequences, static; nothing observed live):
  - `0xA08E` ends by calling `0xD11D` (`0xA2B7`) with `AX` = centre `x`, `BX` = centre `y`, `DX` = `H`, `CX` = `A` and `SI:DI` a 16.16 shear. The centre's two coordinates are `idiv` quotients plus `work:0x41CA` (`0xA235..0xA239`) and `work:0x41C8` (`0xA25A..0xA25E`), the projection centre (160, 92), so each is truncated toward that centre; `H` (`0xA27B`) and `A` (`0xA2A6`) are `idiv` quotients made positive, so truncated.
  - `0xD11D` writes the table's row bounds as `y − H` and `y + H − 1` (`0xD1D3..0xD1EF`), clipped to `work:0x41C4`/`0x41C6`. It writes the centre row and the `H − 1` rows below it from `0xD440` (left `x + shear − w`, right `left + 2w`, `w` starting at `A` and stepped down by the decision variable), then copies each of those rows, from the centre row down, to the rows from `y − 1` up, mirrored through the centre (`0xD249..0xD274`). So the disc covers rows `y − H` to `y + H − 1`, `2H` rows symmetric about the boundary above row `y`, and each row from `x − w` to `x + w`, `2w + 1` pixels symmetric about pixel `x`'s centre; at the centre row `w = A`.
  - The capture fits this: the discs read for #841 are `2A + 1` wide and `2H` high where unoccluded (tick 1101 hub 19 × 18, `A = H = 9`; tyre 27 × 26, `A = H = 13`; tick 1244 hub 9 × 8, `A = H = 4`), and `BOMB`'s wheel at tick 2058, index `0xE0`, is 61 × 60.
  - Our disc, a 16-gon of radius `r` pixels drawn with a one-pixel outline, covers about `2r + 1` rows and columns about its projected centre, which sits half a pixel below the projection's row `92` (the symmetric projection's centre is row `92.5`). Against `0xD11D` that is: one row more from the half-open rows, `2·frac(r)` rows and columns more from truncating `H` and `A`, and the centre offset by its truncation. So ours is `1 + 2·frac(r)` rows taller (1 to 3) and `2·frac(r)` px wider (0 to 2), its top within a row of the capture's and its bottom 1 to 2 rows lower: the #898 offsets.

- **Verdict**: The cause is `0xD11D`'s rule against our outlined, untruncated, uncentred 16-gon. Candidate 1 alone (no outline) is ruled out: it takes a row off the top, not the bottom, so the hubs stay 1 to 2 rows tall, reaching 1 to 2 rows below the capture. Variants 2 and 3 move the disc toward the capture but leave the hubs 1 to 2 rows tall, and 3 and 5 lose `LOCATION`'s ground-disc ends, the 16-gon's chord sag the outline had hidden. Variant 6 brings every `INTRO` disc read under it within a row of the capture's rows (the hubs and the 17.76° tyre exactly), gives `BOMB`'s wheel the capture's 60 and 58 rows and 61 and 59 columns (one row lower, as under 0), puts `LOCATION`'s near rim on row 151 at both ticks and its free ends within a pixel of the capture's on the rows a stone does not cover, and loses the 6161 slivers left of the stones at rows 106 and 124, and its far rim gains a row above the capture's at both ticks (1 px at 6161; 9 px at 6062, where `main` has 1). Adopted, evidence-backed; the brief's candidates 3 (the 16-gon's vertex phase) and 4 (`(radius · work:0x8070) >> 16` rounding) were not tested: with variant 6 the rows they could move already match.
