# Journal

## 2026-11-11: type-8 shapes face the view

- **Question**: Why does `PLAYONE` draw the flat type-8 trees from both sides of the island, where the renderer drops them from one side ([#399](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/399))? The issue's hypotheses: (1) no back-face cull on these quads, (2) a second, reversed face `vm.rs` drops, (3) something else.
- **Method**: `mise run vm-handler -- 0x00F3` and `mise run disasm` over `POLYZ` (`0x9B78`), the display-list entry `0x9100..0x917F`, the edge builder `0xC9D4..0xCD00`, the flat filler `0x7571..0x75DA`, the type-8 handler `0xB4B5..0xB54B`, the view setup `0x1230..0x1300`, the scenery draw `0x2160..0x21F0` and `0x22F0..0x2330`, the actor entry `0x1CF0..0x1D60`, the queue `0x1A90..0x1B0B` and the cull `0x1B0C..0x1B74`, with `mise run xref` for `0x8080`, `0x808A`, `0x809C` and `0x80C0`, on the unpatched load image. `stuntreel sod-trace` over every `INTRO.ALL` shape found the type-8 users; a scratch script read the 10-byte header of every `0x000C`-magic chunk of `INTRO.ALL` and `CTRY.ALL`. A throwaway switch drawing every `POLYZ` with both windings was rendered at `INTRO.FLM` ticks 2080 and 3301 with `mise run frame`, then reverted. The fix was scored with `mise run parity` on its head (`crates/` tree `b6ce1c0`, commit `cdf56ae`) against base `fcbc017`, RTX 4070 Laptop.
- **Result**: (1) is ruled out for the non-clipping path (`0xC50E` was not read): `POLYZ` fills through `0xC9D4`, whose left edge always walks backward from the topmost vertex, and the filler `0x7571` skips a row whose right end is left of its left (`0x75A1`), so a face draws only where it runs clockwise on screen. (2) is ruled out: the pines `0x03A3` and `0x03A7` are one type-8 quad and two `POLYZ` triangles wound the same way. Drawn with both windings, the hill pines at tick 2080 came out as thin edge-on slivers, not the capture's full triangles, so the trees were turned wrong, not culled wrong. (3): the view setup builds a second view matrix `0x809C` with the yaw word zeroed (`xor si,si` at `0x12C2`); the scenery draw uses it in place of `0x80C0` when `[es:9] & 1` (`0x21A8`), and the actor entry when entry byte `+0x0F` bit 0, filled from `[es:9]` (`0x1CFC`, `0x1B6C`). `[es:2]` is the stream start (the magic `0x000C`), `[es:4]` the cull radius and `[es:8]` the scale, so `ES` is the header less two and `[es:9]` is `header_flags`. In `INTRO.ALL` bit 0 is set by all 18 type-8 shapes and by `0x0105`..`0x0107`; in `CTRY.ALL` by its 8 type-8 shapes and `0x0107`; by no other `0x000C` chunk of either archive. With the builder's `Ry(−yaw) · Rx(pitch) · Rz(roll)`, such a shape is turned in the world by the camera's yaw alone. The renderer now draws these props per frame turned by the look direction's heading, behind their `SP_1NODE` gates, and such actors without their own rotation. At tick 2080 the hill pines stand facing the camera as in the capture; tick 3301 keeps its foreground pines. `mean_ssim_scored` base -> head: CTRY 0.89960 -> 0.89962, INTRO 0.84177 -> 0.84172, LAND 0.86539 -> 0.86539, BALLOON 0.80298 -> 0.80299, TRAINTNL 0.85181 -> 0.85181, BOMB 0.81009 -> 0.81014, LOCATION 0.80682 -> 0.80800; IoU unchanged except LOCATION 0.97825 -> 0.97872 and BOMB 0.90923 -> 0.90924. Per frame, INTRO tick 2080 falls 0.023 and 783 falls 0.017 (the pines are now drawn but smaller than the capture's and off its positions, as the hill under them is), 771 rises 0.013; LOCATION's ticks 6590..6656 rise about 0.03.
- **Verdict**: adopted, evidence-backed (image `0x21A8`, `0x1CFC`, `0x12C2`): no film falls by more than 0.002 and LOCATION rises by 0.0012. The CTRY and INTRO moves are under the 0.002 floor; the change rests on the instructions, not on those scores. `references.toml` expected tables updated.
