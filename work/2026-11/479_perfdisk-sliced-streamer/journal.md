# Journal: #479 sliced discs, the type-6 streamer and the disc's skips

## 2026-11-17: sliced discs, the type-6 registers and the disc skips read statically

- **Question**: Which shapes use `OP_PERFDISCSL` (`0x264`, entry `0xA05C`), and can `OP_PERFDISK` perforate? What do the skips at `0xA156` and `0xA1AB` test, and does `vm.rs` match them? Which registers does `OP_IPG` type 6 (`0xB58D`) take from its caller, and does any model reach it ([#479](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/479))?
- **Method**: On `ce9fe79`, in a cloud session, `mise run re-tool -- vm-handler` for the 15 sliced opcodes (`0x240`..`0x2AC`, `0x345`, `0x3BA`), `mise run re-tool -- disasm` over `0xA040..0xA2D1`, `0x9FAB..0xA040`, `0xA47F..0xA518`, `0x965F..0x968D` and `0xB58D..0xB61D`, and `mise run re-tool -- xref` for `0x423E`, `0x41B0` and `0x334D`, all on the unpatched load image. A throwaway `stunt-cli` example (not committed) collected every unique `0x000C`-magic model in the data root's `corpus/` and `captures/*/archives/` (2,428), walked each with `trace_sod`, counted the steps of `OP_IPG` by type, `SPHERE`, `PERFDISK` and the sliced opcodes, and searched every even offset of each model for a sliced opcode word and for the word pair `0x0063 0x0006` (`OP_IPG` type 6), noting whether each hit lies inside an instruction the walk decoded. `mise run sod-census` over the same `corpus/` (31 archives, 2,425 models).
- **Result**:
  - **The sliced ops.** The handlers are `0x9B59` (`0x240`), `0x9B87` (`0x249`), `0x9E2A` (`0x252`), `0x9F29` (`0x25B`), `0xA05C` (`0x264`), `0xA2D1` (`0x26D`), `0xA47F`..`0xA4FF` (`0x276`..`0x2AC`), and, above `0x333`, `0xB14C` (`0x34E`) and `0xB109` (`0x3C3`), the numbering of the unpatched image. Each builder stores its span table's start at `work:0x423E + n`, or 0. `0x276` pushes and `0x27F` pops the arena pointer `work:0x1952`. `0x288` (`0xA48F`) fills one slot's table through the gateway `0:8040` and skips a slot holding 0. `0x291` (`0xA4A6`) passes slots `a` and `b` to `0xBCE0` and stores the result in a third slot; `0x29A` (`0xA4C7`) passes `a` and `b` to `0xBDC7` and stores nothing; `0x2A3` (`0xA4E0`) passes them to `0xBDA5` and writes the result back to `a`; `0x2AC` (`0xA4FF`) passes `a` to `0xBE7D` and stores the result in `b`. The table's names for them are `INTSL`, `SUBSL`, `ADDSL` and `DUPSL`; `0xBCE0`, `0xBDC7`, `0xBDA5` and `0xBE7D` were not read. The word search and the "15 sliced opcodes" use [`sod-opcodes.md`](../../../research/formats/sod-opcodes.md)'s numbering, which names `0x345` and `0x3BA`; on the unpatched image those two numbers dispatch to `0xB13E` and `0xB0E9`, which are flat fills (`call 0xB170; jb; call 0:8010`, and `call 0xCF39; call 0:8010`), and the sliced handlers, storing `[bx + 0x423E]`, are `0x34E` and `0x3C3`, nine above (checked with `re-tool -- disasm` and `vm-handler` on `builds/unpatched`). That is [`patch3.md` § 4.1](../../../research/playone-exe/patch3.md#41-the-patched-build-has-one-dispatch-slot-fewer): from `0x33C` up the unpatched opcode `n` is the patched `n - 9`. Which numbering the shipped shapes use above `0x333` is [`patch3.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/patch3.md#open-questions) open question 1 (still open; [#480](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/480)), so the figure of 15 rests on `sod-opcodes.md`'s numbering.
  - **`OP_PERFDISK` draws one disc.** `0xA040` calls the builder `0xA08E` once and, unless it returns carry, the flat gateway `0:8010` once. No slot is read or written on that path. So a perforated disc needs the sliced ops: `OP_PERFDISCSL` builds the same disc into a slot, and a hole would come from combining slots before `0x288` fills one.
  - **No model uses a sliced op.** No walk of the 2,428 models steps on any of the 15 sliced opcodes. Every word `0x0264` at an even offset (3,362) lies inside an instruction the walk decoded, as an operand. The words of the other sliced opcodes that lie outside every decoded instruction (34 of `0x0240`, 3 of `0x0252`, 2 of `0x02AC` and one each of `0x0288`, `0x029A`, `0x03BA`) all lie past the last word any walk of their shape reaches. Of their 19 shapes, only `PLANES.RES` `CESSNA 172` has `BVGT`s. Its three `BVGT`s branch, when taken, to `0x13C6`, `0x1FE4` and `0x2000`, instructions the walk decodes; the hit lies at `0x2940`, past the walk's last word at `0x2922`. `mise run sod-census` reports no shape ending on an unimplemented opcode.
  - **The two skips.** `0xA08E` reads the centre register's view depth `z + work:0x8088` into `BP` and returns with carry when it is below `0x80`, signed (`cmp bp,80h; jl` at `0xA09A`). It shifts the radius `r` and the centre `(x, y, z)` to one scale (`0x9FD9`) and the normal `n` to another (`0x9FAB`). It then saves `SP` at `work:0x41AA` (`0xA0CD`). The exit `0xA2BC` restores it and returns with carry, so `0xA040` fills nothing. It forms `|n|²` (`0x4220`) and `D = hi(z²) · hi(|n|²) − hi(nx² + ny²) · hi(r²)` (`0x4224`, `0xA136..0xA152`), where `hi` is a product's high word, and jumps to the exit unless `D`'s high word is above 0 (`0xA156`). `D`'s high word (`0x4226`) is the divisor at `0xA235`, `0xA25A` and `0xA27B`. A second term's high word (`0x422A`, `0xA185..0xA1A7`) must be nonzero (`0xA1AB`) and is the divisor at `0xA1FC` and `0xA2A6`. With exact arithmetic, `D > 0` says `z · |n| > r · √(nx² + ny²)`: the rim's least view depth, `z − r · √(nx² + ny²) / |n|`, is above 0, so the whole disc lies in front of the eye. A zero normal (the axis register equal to the centre register) makes `|n|²` and `nx² + ny²` 0, so `D` is 0 and the disc is skipped. `CTRY.ALL` shape `0x064A` has two such discs (`PERFDISK 01C8 03E7 01C8` and `01C8 0BB5 01C8`). `disc_polygons` in `vm.rs` returns no polygon for a normal shorter than `1e-6`, so `vm.rs` drops them too.
  - **Type 6's registers.** The dispatcher `0x965F` reads the type word into `AX` and `BX` and calls `[BX + 0x1954]`; it sets no other register. `0xB58D` stores `BX`, `DX` and `BP` at `work:0x41B0`, `0x41B2`, `0x41B4` and adds them to the x, y and z of each of the seven points it writes into registers 4 to 10 (`0x4480 + 12i`; `0xB5FD..0xB605`). So `BX` is 6, and `DX` and `BP` hold whatever the handler run before `OP_IPG` left there. No code reference to `0x334D`, its table entry, was found, so the table `0x1954` is the only route found to it. No model of the 2,428 holds the words `0x0063 0x0006` at any even offset, so none reaches type 6 on any path.
  - **Tallies.** Walked steps: `OP_IPG` type 0 2,206, type 2 322, type 4 152, type 8 57, type 6 none; `SPHERE` 1,584; `PERFDISK` 2,601, over the 2,428 models. [`sod-vm-generators.md` § 8.8](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm-generators.md#88-model-frame-the-other-generators) quotes 2,192, 320, 152, 57, 1,584 and 2,531 over the 2,425 of `corpus/` alone; the Method scopes this walk to `corpus/` plus `captures/*/archives/`, which is the likely source of the three extra models, but which three they are was not checked.
- **Verdict**: control (static reading and a census). No code changes. `OP_PERFDISK` never perforates; a hole would need the sliced ops, which no model of the 2,428 uses. `vm.rs` matches the zero-normal skip. The eye-plane skip and the `< 0x80` centre-depth skip depend on the view, which `vm.rs` does not have. Type 6 is out of reach of every model searched.
- **Lives in**: [`sod-vm-generators.md` § 8.8](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm-generators.md#88-model-frame-the-other-generators); offsets in [`memory-map-vm.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/memory-map-vm.md).

## 2026-11-17: the sphere winding scored and seen beside the capture

- **Question**: Does the clockwise sphere winding `vm.rs` adopted in [#272](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/272), which draws each sphere's near half, match the capture better than the counter-clockwise winding it replaced, which drew only the far half seen from inside?
- **Method**: On `ce9fe79`, a temporary `DBG_SPHERE_CCW` switch in `sphere_polygons` (`crates/stunt-core/src/sod/vm.rs`) reversed each face's vertex order, giving the old winding. `mise run sweep -- --var DBG_SPHERE_CCW=0,1 --jobs 2` on the seven reference films, on lavapipe (llvmpipe, LLVM 20.1.2; a cloud session has no GPU). Then `mise run frame -- --film location --ticks 1651,1717 --software-only --var DBG_SPHERE_CCW=0,1`. The switch was removed afterwards.
- **Result**: `mean_ssim_scored`, current winding then old: CTRY 0.9186 / 0.9185, INTRO 0.8593 / 0.8593, LAND 0.8935 / 0.8935, BALLOON 0.8172 / 0.8172, TRAINTNL 0.8598 / 0.8598, BOMB 0.8446 / 0.8444, LOCATION 0.8287 / 0.8285; IoU unchanged on every film. With the old winding, 10 frames fell by more than 0.005, all in LOCATION, the worst 1717 (−0.0363), 1651 (−0.0350) and 3422 (−0.0288). At tick 1717 the camera looks over a field of round tree crowns. The capture draws each crown as a filled disc. The current winding draws filled crowns. The old one draws several near crowns hollow, showing their far faces and edge lines from inside ([`location-1717-sphere-winding.png`](images/location-1717-sphere-winding.png): capture, current winding, old winding).
- **Verdict**: control. The current winding stays. `[Bounded Observation]`: the film means differ by at most 0.0002, which does not tell the windings apart, so the evidence is 10 LOCATION frames that fall by more than 0.005 with the old winding (up to 0.036) and one frame's picture (tick 1717). On that evidence the current winding stays and the old one is not supported.
- **Lives in**: [`sod-vm-generators.md` § 8.8](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm-generators.md#88-model-frame-the-other-generators).
