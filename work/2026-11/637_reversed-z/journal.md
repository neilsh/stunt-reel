# Journal

What was measured: the index-plane pixels per tick on which lavapipe (mesa-dist-win 25.2.8) and the RTX 4070 differ over the seven reference films, and GPU parity, under reversed, infinite depth in `Depth32Float` and in `Depth24Plus`, against standard depth on `main`.
`Depth32Float` raised the difference on five films, and on all seven on the canonical lavapipe after #904 and #922; `Depth24Plus` lowered it on all seven, halved the largest tie left (LOCATION 3815, 3,131 to 1,490 pixels) and moved the next largest (LOCATION 3830, LAND 415 and 416) by under 6%.

## 2026-11-16: The decal bias constant on single ticks

- **Question**: which constant the `[I5]` decal bias needs once depth is reversed `near / w` in `Depth32Float`, where the constant counts units of `2^(e - 23)` of the triangle's largest depth instead of `2^-24` of depth.
- **Method**: branch `neilsh/637-reversed-z` on PR #650's head (the code later committed as `feat(render): draw with reversed, infinite depth in Depth32Float`), with a temporary `DBG_DECAL_BIAS` switch for the constant and `slope_scale: 2.0`. `mise run frame -- --film <film> --ticks <ticks> --var DBG_DECAL_BIAS=<values>` on the RTX 4070, then the same with `--software-only --no-build` and `VK_DRIVER_FILES` naming the mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 ICD (`llvmpipe (LLVM 21.1.7, 256 bits)` in each store). Index planes compared pixel by pixel between the variants on each backend, and between the backends for each variant, by a one-off script over `RenderStore.frame(tick).indices`. Ticks: LAND 227 and 415; BOMB 1500, 1910, 2113, 2971 and 3000; CTRY 300, 700 and 1102. The `stunt-render` GPU tests (`mise run test-gpu`'s binary) were also run with constants 0, 1, 2 and 16 on both backends.
- **Result**:
  - Constants 2, 16 and 64 (LAND, BOMB 1910, 2113, 2971) and 0 and 16 (BOMB 1500, 1910, 2113, 2971, 3000; CTRY 300, 700, 1102) drew identical index planes on each backend. A constant of -100,000,000 changed 1,668 (GPU) and 1,666 (lavapipe) pixels at BOMB 2971 and 1,781 and 1,785 at 3000, so the switch reached the pipeline.
  - Lavapipe against the GPU, every constant alike: LAND 227 14 pixels, LAND 415 1,903; BOMB 1500 3, 1910 1, 2113 7, 2971 115, 3000 37; CTRY 300 4, 700 36, 1102 17. On #650's tree the PR reported LAND 227 17 and BOMB 1910 1, and LAND 415 2,084 in its full-film stores.
  - Every GPU test passed with every constant on both backends, the 2-degree grazing ground decals of `a_ground_decal_a_kilometre_and_a_half_away_still_shows` included.
- **Verdict**: control. No sampled frame separates the constants, so the slope term and the compare's equality settle these ties; the constant was set to 32 from the worst-case rounding bound in that draft of ADR 0054, not by score.
- **Lives in**: the `Depth32Float` draft of ADR 0054 (the first commit of #637's PR), superseded by the entries below.

## 2026-11-20: Reversed depth in Depth32Float over every tick, both backends

- **Question**: whether reversed, infinite depth in `Depth32Float` (`main` `5fbfe844` plus the commit `feat(render): draw with reversed, infinite depth in Depth32Float`) lowers the index-plane pixels per tick on which lavapipe and the GPU differ, against `main` `5fbfe844` (`crates` `bf72b96`), and what it does to GPU parity.
- **Method**: track heuristic, no target frames. `main`'s binary built by `mise run build-at -- 5fbfe844`; on each tree `mise run parity` on the RTX 4070, then `mise run parity -- --software-only --no-build` with `VK_DRIVER_FILES` naming only the mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 ICD (`llvmpipe (LLVM 21.1.7, 256 bits)` in every store). Residue: `mise run parity-residue -- <gpu root> <lavapipe root>` on each tree. Scores: `mise run judge -- <main gpu reports> <branch gpu reports> --track heuristic`. A one-off script tallied each residue pixel's layers on both backends and which backend's index plane moved from `main`.
- **Result**:
  - Mean differing pixels per tick, `main` then branch: CTRY 12.2 → 12.0, INTRO 12.8 → 12.1, LAND 35.7 → 46.5, BALLOON 19.9 → 44.6, TRAINTNL 20.7 → 30.9, BOMB 14.7 → 26.6, LOCATION 62.0 → 92.7.
  - The new differences are mostly between polygons of one layer, and mostly lavapipe's: per tick, lavapipe's own index plane moved from `main` on 47.6 pixels on BALLOON (the GPU's on 3.2), 24.7 on BOMB (6.6), 29.0 on LAND (10.9) and 39.0 on TRAINTNL (19.7). On BALLOON the same-layer residue is the balloon's actor layer 17 (14.8 → 40.1 per tick), on index pairs such as 168 against 48 both ways.
  - GPU judge: no film falls by 0.002; 3 frames fall by more than 0.005 (LOCATION 4203 −0.0162, 4214 −0.0123, 3895 −0.0071); winner line `none adopts (best run=candidate: film means short of +0.002 each or +0.004 on one)`.
- **Verdict**: rejected. Reversed depth in `Depth32Float` makes the backends disagree more on five of seven films.
- **Lives in**: [ADR 0054](../../../docs/adr/0054-depth-is-reversed-and-infinite.md), Considered Options; [`lavapipe.md`](../../../research/parity/lavapipe.md) section 5 and Ruled out.

## 2026-11-20: Bias or format at 13 ticks

- **Question**: whether the decal bias, whose unit changes with a float format, or the depth format itself, drives the extra disagreement of the entry above.
- **Method**: a temporary `DBG_DEPTH` switch (not committed) on the `Depth32Float` tree of the entry above: `a` `Depth32Float` with `constant: 32, slope_scale: 2.0`; `b` constant 0; `c` slope 0; `d` both 0; `e` `Depth24Plus` with `constant: 2, slope_scale: 2.0`, the same reversed, infinite projection; `f` constant 1,024; `g` slope 8. `mise run frame -- --film <film> --ticks <ticks> --no-build --var DBG_DEPTH=a,b,c,d,e,f,g` on the GPU, then with `--software-only` and lavapipe, at BALLOON 851, 1017, 1130; TRAINTNL 218, 922, 1127; BOMB 2113, 2372, 2971; LAND 227, 416; LOCATION 2599, 3820 (the ticks with the most residue in the entry above, plus four from `lavapipe.md` section 5). Differing pixels between the two backends' stores per variant per tick by a one-off script.
- **Result**: GPU against lavapipe, variant `a` then `e`: BALLOON 176 → 31, 170 → 22, 191 → 37; TRAINTNL 203 → 13, 265 → 35, 217 → 25; BOMB 7 → 8, 183 → 93, 115 → 36; LAND 12 → 6, 2,176 → 2,075; LOCATION 147 → 8, 2,856 → 182. `b` and `f` matched `a` within 2 pixels at every tick and `g` within 7; `c` and `d` raised it (LOCATION 3820 4,603 and 4,961, BOMB 2372 326 and 462). `main` at the same ticks: BALLOON 85, 30, 45; TRAINTNL 23, 50, 127; BOMB 9, 73, 76; LAND 13, 2,096; LOCATION 15, 2,120.
- **Verdict**: the format, not the bias. Reversed depth in `Depth24Plus` disagrees less than `Depth32Float` at 12 of the 13 ticks, and less than `main` at 12 of them, so it went to a whole-film measurement.
- **Lives in**: [ADR 0054](../../../docs/adr/0054-depth-is-reversed-and-infinite.md), Considered Options.

## 2026-11-20: Reversed depth in Depth24Plus over every tick, both backends

- **Question**: whether reversed, infinite depth in `Depth24Plus`, with the decal bias at `constant: 2, slope_scale: 2.0` toward the viewer, lowers the residue against `main` on every film and keeps GPU parity.
- **Method**: track heuristic, no target frames. Variant `e` of the entry above, rendered and scored as in the first entry of this date, against the same `main` stores. The committed code is the same variant without the switch; its runs are in the next entry.
- **Result**:
  - Mean differing pixels per tick, `main` then `e`: CTRY 12.2 → 6.3, INTRO 12.8 → 8.9, LAND 35.7 → 26.2, BALLOON 19.9 → 8.9, TRAINTNL 20.7 → 5.2, BOMB 14.7 → 11.6, LOCATION 62.0 → 29.8. Ticks with any difference fell on six films (LOCATION 7,786 → 7,535, TRAINTNL 1,208 → 1,124) and rose on BOMB (3,431 → 3,433).
  - The ties named in `lavapipe.md` section 5, `main` then `e`: LAND 227 13 → 6, LAND 415 1,867 → 1,858, LAND 416 2,096 → 2,075, BOMB 1910 1 → 1, LOCATION 2599 15 → 8, 2859 5 → 6, 3815 3,131 → 1,490, 3830 2,603 → 2,470.
  - GPU judge: no film falls by 0.002 and no frame by more than 0.005 (worst −0.0049); 7 frames move by more than 0.005, all up (LAND 1, LOCATION 6); winner line `none adopts (best run=candidate: film means short of +0.002 each or +0.004 on one)`. `mean_ssim_scored` per film is unchanged to four decimals but LAND 0.9235 → 0.9236 and LOCATION 0.8719 → 0.8720.

## 2026-11-22: Depth32Float again, after the 0.1 m near plane and the outline clip band

- **Question**: whether `Depth32Float` still makes the canonical lavapipe and the GPU disagree more than `Depth24Plus` now that the near plane is 0.1 m (#904) and the outline line lists are clipped to a band around the view (#922), both of which changed what the 2026-11-20 entries measured.
- **Method**: track heuristic, no target frames. Baseline `main` `44a3bf31` (`crates` `d6edce87`); candidate the same plus commit `d9759e0e` on branch `claude/project-thread-4kd7by` (`crates` `4917af11`): `DEPTH_FORMAT` `Depth32Float` and the decal bias `constant: 32, slope_scale: 2.0`, the values of the first entry of 2026-11-20. On each tree, in its own worktree on Legion: `mise run parity` on the RTX 4070 Laptop (driver 610.88), then `mise run parity -- --software-only` in WSL 2 on the canonical lavapipe (`llvmpipe (LLVM 20.1.2, 256 bits)`, `mesa-vulkan-drivers` `25.2.8-0ubuntu0.24.04.5`, in every report), then `mise run agreement -- measure --gpu <root> --lavapipe <root>`. Scores: `mise run judge -- <main reports> <candidate reports> --track heuristic`, once for each class.
- **Result**:
  - Mean differing pixels per tick, `main` then `Depth32Float`, with the largest and its tick: CTRY 3.09 (14 at 861) → 9.55 (148 at 811), INTRO 2.22 (25 at 3044) → 5.73 (140 at 3043), LAND 5.23 (31 at 589) → 27.36 (264 at 382), BALLOON 4.12 (17 at 1163) → 46.23 (217 at 837), TRAINTNL 3.66 (20 at 1135) → 29.16 (231 at 875), BOMB 8.40 (77 at 2272) → 27.62 (219 at 2956), LOCATION 8.59 (584 at 3824) → 80.23 (2,987 at 3817).
  - Ticks differing by more than 500 pixels: `main` 3, all LOCATION (3824, 3828, 3830); `Depth32Float` 156, all LOCATION, nearly every tick of 3812 to 3968 and 4684, 4784, 4803, 4810. Against the pinned `agreement.toml` the candidate flags every film's mean and LOCATION 3812 to 3950 (46 ticks up by over 1,000 pixels); `main` flags nothing.
  - GPU judge: winner line `none adopts (best run=candidate: film means short of +0.002 each or +0.004 on one)`; 15 frames fall by more than 0.005, all LOCATION, the worst 3840 by 0.0152.
  - Lavapipe judge: the same winner line; 38 frames fall by more than 0.005 (24 LOCATION, 14 BALLOON), the worst LOCATION 3829 by 0.0410; BALLOON's mean 0.9059 → 0.9040, LOCATION's 0.8725 → 0.8719.
  - `STUNT_REQUIRE_GPU=1 mise run test-gpu`: 50 passed and the same 3 failed on both trees, each with `no compatible GPU adapter (one with clip distances)`, so the format does not cause them.
  - Index planes of both trees and both classes at each film's worst tick, inspected by eye (a one-off script drawing GPU, lavapipe and their difference side by side): at LOCATION 3817 and 3824 most of `Depth32Float`'s difference is whole ground triangles of the castle's green surround drawn over its moat water, differently on each class, where `main` keeps the moat on both at 3817; at 3824, `main`'s worst tick, the GPU alone already draws one-row green stripes across the moat. Elsewhere (CTRY 811, LAND 382, BALLOON 837, TRAINTNL 875, BOMB 2956) the extra pixels are one-pixel runs along shared edges, such as roof eaves against walls and the balloon's panel seams, with neither class visibly wrong.
- **Verdict**: rejected again. `Depth32Float` makes the backends disagree more on all seven films, by 2.6 to 11 times, where on 2026-11-20 it was five. `[Supported Inference]`: the moat and its surround are a near-coplanar tie that the 24-bit step mostly turns into an exact one, settled alike by the compare function on both classes, which `main`'s GPU stripes at LOCATION 3824 show it does not always do; the tie, not the format, is the defect.
- **Lives in**: [ADR 0054](../../../docs/adr/0054-depth-is-reversed-and-infinite.md), Considered Options; [`lavapipe.md`](../../../research/parity/lavapipe.md) section 5 and Ruled out.
