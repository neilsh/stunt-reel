# Journal: #628, high actors' shadows in a captured frame

## 2026-11-20: THE.FLM's high shadows in the captured pixels

**Question**: do the shadows of `THE.FLM` slots 3 to 16, which reached the shadow draw `0x1C0D` at ticks 884 and 885 with their actors 181 to 270 m above raised ground ([the #512 raised-ground breaks](../shadow-raised-ground/journal.md#2026-11-16-raised-ground-and-a-high-actors-shadow-in-view)), show as index-0 pixels in the frame; and why did the 2026-11-16 `capture-ref` run write nothing?

**Method**: at `79e309f`, on this machine (DOSBox through `dosbox-automation`). Film `corpus/siarchive/Films/MultiFLM/Unknown/THE.FLM` with its sibling `THE.ALL` (archive `70192a9a…`).

1. `mise run capture-ref -- --film <that film> --game-root builds/unpatched --out <scratch>/the-s11 --max-frames 85`: stride 11, so the clock pass stops at tick `1 + 11·85` = 936.
2. A control: [`shadow_control.py`](shadow_control.py) `trees` builds two game trees from `builds/unpatched`, `unpacked` (PLAYONE.EXE unpacked from LZEXE, `4e68d100…`) and `noshadow`, the same file with one byte changed: the scene walk's `je short 1E38h` at image `0x1E33`, which skips the shadow call when `SIF_SHADOW` is clear, made `jmp short` (`83e814ba…`), so `0x1E35` never calls the shadow queue `0x19FC`. `shadow_control.py capture` ran the step 1 capture on each (`--max-frames 85`), and `compare` diffed the pages tick by tick.
3. Which slots carry a shadow: [`high_shadow_census.py`](../512_altitude-cutoff/high_shadow_census.py)'s `high_slices` on `THE.FLM` with no height threshold, slices from tick 820 to 940.
4. Where the drawn entries land: the view points the #512 breaks read at ticks 884 and 885 (`x` −901 to −787, `y` 2360 to 2581, depth 8365 to 9058) put through the frustum's screen slopes, column `160 + 256·x/z` and row `92 + 256·y/z` (centre `0x41C8`/`0x41CA` = 160, 92; slopes `0x22A0`/`0x22A2` = 160 and 92 pixels per unit of depth, times 256: [`lod.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/lod.md)).
5. The 2026-11-16 command again: `--max-frames 1200`, timed.

**Result**:

* **The capture runs.** Step 1 wrote 83 frames, film ticks 34 to 936, in 81 s on the unpatched build (2 of 85 windows held no usable page); the two control captures also wrote 83 frames each.
* **Where the entries project.** Step 4 gives columns 132.4 to 137.8 and rows 158.7 to 171.0: left of centre near the bottom of the 185-row view.
* **The frames.** From tick 837 to 904 the camera holds still on a hill's peak with sky above; in the frames looked at (837, 859, 870, 881, 892 and 904) no helicopter of the formation is in the view, the one object a small brown one on the peak. A thin black mark, palette index 0 (RGB 0, 0, 0), lies on the hill's slope and slides down it: 23 pixels at columns 135 to 137, rows 135 to 145 at tick 837; 28 at rows 145 to 157 at tick 859; 31 at rows 150 to 163 at tick 870; 37 at columns 134 to 137, rows 155 to 170 at tick 881; 37 at rows 169 to 184, the view's bottom row, at tick 892 (`unpacked` capture; the step 1 capture's counts are within 4 pixels of these at its ticks 837, 860, 870, 881 and 892). It is the only index 0 in the view at those ticks. From tick 903 there is none.
* **The control.** In the `noshadow` capture the view holds no index-0 pixel at ticks 837, 859, 870, 881 and 892, and at each of those ticks `unpacked` and `noshadow` differ in exactly the mark's pixels and nowhere else (23, 28, 31, 37 and 37 pixels, all index 0 in `unpacked`). So the shadow call draws the mark, and the pages are otherwise identical. At tick 826, before the cut to the hill, the two differ in 107 pixels, all index 0, at rows 155 to 163, columns 0 to 282.
* **Which shadows.** In the stream from tick 837 to 935 the shadowed, drawn slots are 0 (stream `y` −0.3), 3 to 16 (stream `y` −573.5 to −326.8) and 34 (−100). Stream `y` is the slice's absolute `y` in metres, `y` down, not the height above the plane: at ticks 884 and 885 the breaks put the ground under slots 3 to 16 at `y` −259 to −327, which is how stream `y` near −528 and −509 gives 181 to 270 m. The control removes every slot's shadow, so it cannot tell slots 3 to 16 from slots 0 and 34, whose shadows were not placed. The attribution is by position: interpolated to tick 884 between ticks 881 (rows 155 to 170) and 892 (169 to 184), the mark spans about rows 159 to 174 at columns 134 to 137, against step 4's rows 159 to 171 and columns 132 to 138. The mark covers 23 to 41 pixels in the three captures.
* **Replay variance.** At ticks 925 and 936 `unpacked` and `noshadow` differ in 3 and 5 pixels at columns 136 to 139, rows 115 to 129, none of them index 0; the packed and unpacked builds differ there too (3 pixels at 925), so this is run-to-run variance, not the shadow. Before tick 830 the pages vary more: `unpacked` and `noshadow` differ at some shared ticks in hundreds of pixels that are not index 0 (1,698 pixels at tick 452, none of them index 0 in `unpacked`), and the step 1 capture and `unpacked`, the same program, differ there too (242 pixels at tick 815).
* **The 2026-11-16 run was not a relaunch loop.** Step 5, the same command, finished in 1,004 s with 1,192 frames (film ticks to 13,201; 8 of 1,200 windows held no usable page). Its clock pass ran 284 s; `--max-frames 1200` then makes 1,200 windows in 30 window passes of 40 (`MAX_DUMPS`), run up to 8 at a time (`DEFAULT_PARALLEL_PASSES`), and each pass launches its own DOSBox, writing a new `dosbox-<n>.conf` into the stage folder: 31 launches in all, by the log's `lua: script 'unnamed' started` stamps the clock pass at 02:51:20, eight window passes at 02:56:05 and 02:56:06, then the other 22 one at a time as passes ended, the last at 03:03:16; the last pass exited at 03:08:00. `capture-ref` prints all 30 `window pass n/30` lines when it queues the passes and no per-pass progress line after that, only DOSBox's own launch chatter (log lines 118 to 2637), and writes the frames only after every pass has returned, so a run stopped at 20 minutes had written no frame and reported no pass done. The 2026-11-16 run's own log is gone, so that it behaved this way is an inference from the same command's behaviour today. Its frames at ticks 837 to 892 have the index-0 counts of step 1's capture (23, 26, 31, 33 and 41 at ticks 837, 848, 870, 881 and 892).

**Verdict**: adopted as a finding. In `THE.FLM` the shadows of slots 3 to 16 show in the frame from tick 837 to 892, as a black mark on the hillside below and left of centre, while their actors are out of the view: the one-byte control removes exactly those index-0 pixels, which makes them shadow, and their position makes them these slots' (`[Bounded Observation]`, one film, one formation). With the #512 breaks, this licenses rewriting "the view cull is the whole of the handbook's cutoff" from `[Hypothesis]` to `[Bounded Observation]` within the films and heights observed: shadows with the ground point in view are drawn and seen (at ticks 837 to 892, with the actors' 181 to 270 m read at 884 and 885), and those with it out of view are culled. Not settled: whether the mark's disappearance after tick 892 is the cull (no break was set there; the mark reaches the view's bottom row at 892). Ruled out, by inference from the rerun: a relaunch loop as the cause of the 2026-11-16 run's lack of frames, which is the window passes' cost and `capture-ref`'s lack of per-pass progress lines while they run.
