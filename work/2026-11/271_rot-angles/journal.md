# Journal: articulated parts and the angle bank

## 2026-11-17: the ROT handlers read and the bank driven

- **Question**: How do `ROTX`, `ROTY` and `ROTZ` turn the parts after them, and does driving them from the angle bank (film clock, film variables 10 and 12, the animate word; [`angle-bank.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/angle-bank.md)) move the reference films' scores?
- **Method**: Static, at `16eb34e`, on the unpatched load image: `mise run re-tool -- disasm 0xA807 0xA9D0` and `0x8725 0x8760`. Code: the VM keeps the draw matrix's `ROT*` part and the light under `PUSHRELMAT`/`POPRELMAT` and loads `VECTORS` and every `IPG` quad through it; a decode takes an `AngleBank`; the scene fills each actor's bank from `angle_bank` (var 10 of the previous frame taken 3 ticks back, `PREVIOUS_FRAME_TICKS`) masked to the operands the shape's `ROT*` read; the renderer decodes an actor's shape again in that pose, as it already did for its light. `stuntreel sod-trace` on the hero aircraft. Scored with `mise run sweep -- --var DBG_ROT=0,1 --jobs 4` (a temporary switch that forces the rest pose at 0), cloud session, lavapipe; track evidence (the handlers at `0xA807`, `0xA8F3`, `0xA906`, `0xA99A`, `0xA9B8`).
- **Result**:
  - `ROTX` (`0xA8F3`) and `ROTY` (`0xA906`) read the bank word, skip 0, and call the builder `0x8725` with the angle in `BP` (pitch) or `SI` (yaw) and the other two angles 0, then `0x884A` forms `0x808A · R` into `0x808A`: `R = Rx(a)` and `Ry(−a)`. `ROTZ` (`0xA807`) turns columns 0 and 1 of each row of `0x808A` in place, `(c₀ cos + c₁ sin, c₁ cos − c₀ sin)` (`0xA823..0xA8B8`), `R = Rz(a)`, and then the light words `0x8072`, `0x8074` the same way (`0xA8BC..0xA8EB`), which `ROTX` and `ROTY` do not touch. Each indexes the sine table by `angle >> 4`. `PUSHRELMAT` (`0xA99A`) pushes `0x808A`'s nine words and `0x8072..0x8076`; `POPRELMAT` (`0xA9B8`) pops them.
  - `CTRY.ALL`'s `0x7F37` and `INTRO.ALL`'s `0x7F19` each run one `ROTZ 0x14` (the propeller, inside `PUSHCENTRE; MOVECENTRE; PUSHRELMAT`) followed by `BVGT 0x12, 0x1F40`, the solid-or-line blade choice ([#624](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/624)), which this change leaves reading 0.
  - Scores, `mean_ssim_scored`, `DBG_ROT=0` (rest) → `DBG_ROT=1` (posed): CTRY 0.9186 → 0.9183, INTRO 0.8593 → 0.8608, LAND 0.8935 → 0.8942, BALLOON 0.8172 → 0.8172, TRAINTNL 0.8598 → 0.8597, BOMB 0.8446 → 0.8463, LOCATION 0.8287 → 0.8314; mean 0.8602 → 0.8611. IoU unchanged or up (INTRO 0.9863 → 0.9865, BOMB 0.9118 → 0.9129, LOCATION 0.9784 → 0.9786). No film fell by more than 0.0003; no frame was lost.
  - 36 frames fell by more than 0.005, all LOCATION but `traintnl:650` (−0.0059) and `intro:2773` (−0.0050); the worst is `location:7800` (−0.1099). There the helicopter's four-blade main rotor and its tail rotor now turn, and the solid blades sweep across pixels where the capture has the line arm at another phase ([`location-7800-rotor.png`](images/location-7800-rotor.png): capture, rest, posed). A scratch decode of `0x3E57` gives the same extent at bank words `0` and `0x4000` and a smaller one at `0x2000`, a rotor with four-fold symmetry, not a part drawn twice. The solid arm is [#624](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/624)'s `BVGT`; the phase depends on `PREVIOUS_FRAME_TICKS`, a hypothesis.
  - Pictures: [`ctry-496-propeller.png`](images/ctry-496-propeller.png) (crop of the biplane, ×3; the capture draws a pale blur at the nose, ours the solid propeller, turned a little from rest) and [`intro-1299-propeller.png`](images/intro-1299-propeller.png) (the foreground triplane's propeller: vertical at rest, slanted when posed; the capture's is slanted too, its angle not measured).
- **Verdict**: adopted, evidence-backed track (the handlers above): INTRO, LAND, BOMB and LOCATION rise, no film falls by more than 0.002. Each rise is under the 0.002 noise floor except LOCATION's +0.0027, so the scores alone do not attribute it; the rule does. One hypothesis round.
