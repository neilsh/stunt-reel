# Journal: OP_PERFDISK on a third shape, at another lens

## 2026-11-20: INTRO's triplane wheels at the default lens and at 17.76°

- **Question**: Do `OP_PERFDISK`'s row widths agree with the capture on a shape other than `BOMB`'s wheel and `LOCATION.ALL` `0x07C6`, preferably at a lens other than the default (42.67°) and 50.95° ([#841](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/841))?
- **Method**: local-windows, RTX 4070 Laptop GPU (`render.json` adapter `NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU`, class `hardware`), `origin/main` `111970f` (`crates/` tree `b3bcfa6`). A local, uncommitted edit to `vm.rs`'s `perfdisk` XORed the disc's colour with `0x55` under `DBG_DISCTAG=1`; every captured tick of `CTRY` (121), `LAND` (103), `INTRO` (347), `LOCATION` (745) and `TRAINTNL` (112) was rendered with `mise run frame -- --film <film> --ticks <all captured> --var DBG_DISCTAG=0,1`, and a scratch script counted, per tick and per lens (`render.json`'s `fov_degrees`), the pixels the two stores differ on (the discs), then split them into same-index connected components. The shape was found with `stuntreel sod-trace --archive INTRO.ALL --shape 0x7F04`. Row spans of the disc colours were read in a box around the wheel from the capture's index plane and from the `DBG_DISCTAG=0` store (the code of `main`). A second local edit forced `camera.disc_depth_scale()` to 1 under `DBG_DISCSQ=0` and served as the squeeze's sensitivity test: `mise run frame -- --ticks … --var DBG_DISCSQ=1,0`, pixels differing between the two stores counted over the 320 × 185 viewport.
- **Result**:
  - *The search.* The largest disc frame at each lens, in disc pixels: `CTRY` only 42.67°, 14 px; `LAND` 42.67° 112 px, 11.16° 101 px (tick 540: jet wheels of 5 to 10 px and two discs one or two rows high); `INTRO` 42.67° 875 px (tick 1101), 17.76° 195 px (tick 1244), 8.28° 106 px; `TRAINTNL` 42.67° 228 px, 14.84° 356 px (tick 243: roof discs about 12 × 8 px, but at ticks 232, 243 and 254 our train is not where the capture's is, so the camera or the placement does not match); `LOCATION` 50.95° 9,776 px (`0x07C6`, the ground disc already read), 42.67° 187 px, 22.10° 81 px, and at most 2 px at 46.93°, 56.97° and 58.33°. So the largest disc at a lens other than 42.67° and 50.95° where the camera matches is `INTRO`'s wheel at 17.76°.
  - *The shape.* `INTRO.ALL` `0x7F04`, the triplane: on the `IFVIS` branches these frames draw, a wheel is a tyre `PERFDISK` of radius `0x0580` in colour `0x06` and a hub of radius `0x0400` over it in colour `0x15` or `0x17`; other branches draw a lone disc of radius `0x0580` in colour `0x03`. Its normal runs along the axle, so the wheel stands upright, as `BOMB`'s does.
  - *Tick 1101, 42.67° (zoom 1).* The struts and the wheel tops line up in the two panels. Near hub (index `0x17`): rows 119 to 136 in the capture, 119 to 138 in ours; widest 19 px (x 93 to 111, rows 125 to 130) in the capture, 20 px (x 92 to 111, rows 126 to 130) in ours. Near tyre (indices `0x06`, `0x15`, `0x17`, its right end free): top rim row 115 in both, bottom rim row 140 in the capture and 141 in ours (4 px at 141); rows 129, 130 and 131 are x 89 to 115 (27 px) in the capture and 88 to 115, 88 to 114, 88 to 114 (28, 27, 27 px) in ours; rows 134, 136, 138 and 140 are 25, 21, 17 and 11 px in the capture and 25, 24, 20 and 15 px in ours.
  - *Tick 2399, 42.67°.* Ours sits 1 to 2 px left of the capture. Near hub: rows 120 to 137 in the capture, 119 to 139 in ours; widest 21 px in both (x 89 to 109 against 88 to 108, rows 126 to 130). Near tyre, rows 129 to 132: 27 px (x 86 to 112) in the capture, 28 px (x 84 to 111) in ours.
  - *Ticks 1244 and 2553, 17.76° (zoom 2.50).* The plane is parked and both ticks read the same. Tyre (`0x06` with `0x17`): rows 166 to 178 in the capture, 167 to 180 in ours; widest x 191 to 206 (16 px, rows 170 to 173) in the capture, x 191 to 207 (17 px, rows 172 and 173) in ours. Hub (`0x17`): rows 169 to 176 in the capture, 169 to 178 in ours; widest 9 px (x 196 to 204) in the capture, 11 px (x 196 to 206, row 174) in ours.
  - *Squeeze sensitivity.* With `DBG_DISCSQ=0`, 3 px of the frame change at `INTRO` 1244, and the tyre and hub rows read the same; at `LOCATION` 6062 (50.95°) 3,487 px change, so the switch fires. At `LAND` 11.16° ticks 518 to 573 it changes 0 to 30 px, at `TRAINTNL` 14.84° ticks 221 to 254 11 to 31 px, at `INTRO` 8.28° ticks 2740 to 2784 0 to 22 px, and at `LOCATION` 22.10° ticks 375, 452 and 463 none.
- **Verdict**: control, no code change. On `INTRO.ALL` `0x7F04`'s wheels, our widths are within 2 px of the capture's at their widest rows (equal to 1 px wider at 42.67°, 1 to 2 px wider at 17.76°), so the radius scale holds on a third shape and at a telephoto lens to that precision. Ours reaches 1 to 2 rows lower at the bottom rim of each disc whose bottom rim was read, 1 to 3 rows taller in each disc read, and tick 1101's tyre is 3 to 4 px wider than the capture's on its lowest rows read (136 to 140); filed as [#898](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/898). These upright, nearly face-on wheels barely move under the lens squeeze (3 px at 1244), so they do not test the squeeze or the normal's depth rescale; on the ticks tested at lenses other than 42.67° and 50.95°, the squeeze moved at most 31 px, against 3,487 px on `LOCATION`'s ground disc at 50.95°.
