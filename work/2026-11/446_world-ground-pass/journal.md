# Journal: flat ground in a painter-ordered pass

## 2026-11-13: A WORLD_GROUND painter pass whose outlines stay off line runs

- **Question**: Does drawing flat `y = base_y` ground (the ocean, flat `MAP.BIN` patches, flat prop triangles) first, in `PLAYONE`'s walk order, through a `CompareFunction::Always` pass with depth write, raise the reference films without the `TRAINTNL` distant-shot loss, once a mesh's line triangles are drawn after the outline of the fill before them?
- **Track**: evidence-backed (ADR 0007): `PLAYONE` has no depth buffer, and `SP_2NODE` (`0x1EC0..0x1EEE`) visits its first child then its second with no eye-side swap ([`world-scene.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/world-scene.md) section 6.6); `0x06CD..0x06CF` draw bed, ties, then `40` rails last.
- **Sweep flags** (quoted before the run): `mise run sweep -- --var DBG_GROUND=0,1,2 --track evidence --target traintnl:12-133`. `DBG_GROUND=0` is `main` (no ground pass), `1` the issue's prototype (one ground node per prop mesh, its outline drawn after all of it), `2` one ground node per run of line or fill triangles, each run's outline drawn right after it.
- **Method**: On `main` b489f84d (expected tables measured on `crates` tree `9fefa9e`, no render change since), RTX 4070 Laptop GPU: the sweep above, its switch kept as [`switches/DBG_GROUND.diff`](switches/DBG_GROUND.diff); then `mise run parity` on `main` and on commit 5bda7950 (`crates` tree `16b814b`), compared per film tick; `mise run frame -- --var DBG_GROUND=0,2` on the worst and best ticks; `stuntreel export-gltf` of `TRAINTNL.ALL` shapes `0x06CE` and `0x06CF`.
- **Result**:
  - **Sweep.** `DBG_GROUND=0` (`main`) / `1` / `2`, `mean_ssim_scored`: `CTRY` 0.9049 / 0.9122 / 0.9122, `INTRO` 0.8469 / 0.8536 / 0.8535, `LAND` 0.8666 / 0.8927 / 0.8928, `BALLOON` 0.8137 / 0.8174 / 0.8174, `TRAINTNL` 0.8572 / 0.8563 / 0.8568, `BOMB` 0.8219 / 0.8376 / 0.8376, `LOCATION` 0.8161 / 0.8257 / 0.8258; mask IoU unchanged to four decimals except `INTRO` (0.9863 to 0.9864, +0.0000012). Winner line: `winner: DBG_GROUND=2 adopts (evidence, film means; target idx -0.0003 attribution; worst target frame -0.0132 traintnl:100; 55 frames down >0.005, listed) (noise floor +/-0.002; winner is ~within noise)`; the noise note is the margin of `2` over `1`, not over `main`.
  - **Full `mise run parity`, `main` to the change** (`mean_ssim_scored`): `CTRY` 0.9048628 to 0.9121942 (+0.0073314), `INTRO` 0.8469210 to 0.8535342 (+0.0066132), `LAND` 0.8666185 to 0.8928127 (+0.0261942), `BALLOON` 0.8136836 to 0.8173612 (+0.0036776), `TRAINTNL` 0.8571628 to 0.8567724 (-0.0003904), `BOMB` 0.8218551 to 0.8376118 (+0.0157567), `LOCATION` 0.8161204 to 0.8257916 (+0.0096711). Mask IoU moves by at most 0.0000012 (`INTRO`).
  - **`TRAINTNL` target ticks**, SSIM `main` to the change: 12 0.8347 to 0.8260, 34 0.8249 to 0.8132, 100 0.9133 to 0.9001, 111 0.9076 to 0.8966, 133 0.9364 to 0.9262. The prototype's solid tie band is gone (the rail shows where variant `1` drew brown), but the ties still cover most of the rail: `0x06CE` and `0x06CF` decode to `SOD_BASE` (bed triangles and the rail lines) then `SOD_DECAL` (the tie), so the tie paints after the rails whatever the run split does. The close-up ticks rise (826 +0.0118, 837 +0.0110, 903 +0.0091).
  - **Largest rises**: `LOCATION` 7272 +0.249 (the ocean no longer shows through the hills), `BALLOON` 276 +0.101, `BOMB` 2938 +0.092, `LAND` 881 +0.091, `INTRO` 2916 +0.038, `CTRY` 739 +0.030.
  - **Frames down by more than 0.005** (guard 2; 55 listed by the sweep): `LOCATION` 3037 (-0.041) and 1992 (-0.030) draw the parking-lot lines, and the roads and lawns, the capture shows, a few pixels off the capture's; `BOMB` 1915 to 2048 (to -0.038) are frames where the capture shows a billboard close up and ours the runway, so the newly drawn markings land on the wrong scene; `INTRO` 871 to 936 (to -0.015) paint sand over more of the capture's green (open question 6 of the note); the `TRAINTNL` opening shot as above; the rest are under 0.016.
  - **`LOCATION` tick 2124**: the capture's grey roads and lighter lawns are still missing after the change (but see the next entry).
- **Verdict**: Adopted on the evidence-backed track (`PLAYONE` paints without a depth buffer; `SP_2NODE` `0x1EC0..0x1EEE`): no film falls by more than 0.002, six rise. Variant `1` (the issue's prototype) is set aside for `2`, which scores 0.0005 higher on `TRAINTNL` and within 0.0001 elsewhere at four decimals; both ran with the double-lift bug of the next entry, so this is not a rejection by score. The fresh review then found the double-lift bug of the next entry; the tables are repinned there.

## 2026-11-13: Snapped decals lost their lift twice and missed the ground pass

- **Question**: The fresh review of the entry above found that a ground-snapped prop's decal vertices had their `[I5]` lift taken off twice (once by the snap, again when tested for the ground plane), so they sat 1 mm off the plane and stayed in `WORLD_DECAL`. With that fixed, does the pass still adopt, and do `TRAINTNL`'s distant ticks recover?
- **Track**: evidence-backed, as above; the fix makes the code do what the entry above and ADR 0045 describe.
- **Judge flags** (quoted before the run): `mise run judge -- build/scratch/base-report build/parity/report --track evidence --target traintnl:12-133`, the baseline being `mise run parity` on `main` b489f84d, the candidate `mise run parity` on the fixed commit.
- **Method**: `mise run parity` on commit ef214921 (`crates` tree `fdd208b`, the fix d0e60158 plus doc comments), RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, judged against the `main` b489f84d run of the entry above; `mise run frame` on the worst and best ticks.
- **Result**:
  - **Judge**: `winner: run=candidate adopts (evidence, film means; target idx -0.0007 attribution; worst target frame -0.0141 traintnl:100; 72 frames down >0.005, listed)`.
  - **`mean_ssim_scored`, `main` to the fix**: `CTRY` 0.9048628 to 0.9161684 (+0.0113056), `INTRO` 0.8469210 to 0.8534789 (+0.0065579), `LAND` 0.8666185 to 0.8928874 (+0.0262689), `BALLOON` 0.8136836 to 0.8173704 (+0.0036868), `TRAINTNL` 0.8571628 to 0.8566446 (-0.0005182), `BOMB` 0.8218551 to 0.8394078 (+0.0175527), `LOCATION` 0.8161204 to 0.8287817 (+0.0126612).
