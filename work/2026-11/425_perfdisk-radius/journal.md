# Journal: OP_PERFDISK radius

## 2026-11-12: the disc's size against a same-camera BOMB capture

- **Question**: Is `vm.rs`'s `OP_PERFDISK` radius, raw register units, the size `PLAYONE` draws, given the handler's `>> 16` where `OP_SPHERE` shifts by 8 ([#425](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/425))?
- **Method**: `mise run frame -- --film bomb --ticks 2025,2036,2048,2058,2070` on `origin/main` (`crates/` tree `bf979d3`, RTX 4070), then the disc's row spans read off its flat palette colour in the capture and ours. The handler `0xA040..0xA2BC` re-read with `mise run disasm`. A 64-gon and a 32-gon variant of `DISC_SEGMENTS`, then `mise run parity` on the 32-gon (3 min).
- **Result**: Ticks 2025, 2036 and 2048 show a different scene in the two panels. Tick 2058 matches: the car body's left edge and the disc's centre column agree to 2 px. There, at rows 172 and 184, the capture's disc is 77 and 91 px wide, the 16-gon 72 and 87 px, the 64-gon 1 to 2 px under the capture. Tick 2070 agrees likewise (capture 51, 67, 77, 85 px at rows 6, 12, 18 and 24 below its top; 64-gon 49, 65, 76, 84). The 16-gon is 4 to 6 % narrow and the 64-gon closes most of the gap; the rest is unexplained. The `0x8108` rescale makes the normal's depth component share the x and y components' scale (`0xA0DB..0xA0E8` against [`memory-map.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/memory-map.md)); `vm.rs` keeps the normal in the model frame, so it needs none. The capture's disc is about 2 px taller than ours at 2058; not isolated. 32-gon SSIM against the pinned 16-gon tables: CTRY +0.000003, INTRO -0.00003, LAND -0.00003, BALLOON 0, TRAINTNL -0.000003, BOMB -0.00014, LOCATION -0.00011.
- **Verdict**: radius agrees with the BOMB wheel disc at ticks 2058 and 2070 to about 2 px (other shapes and depths unchecked), code unchanged. The 32-gon is rejected: evidence-backed track needs one film to rise, and only CTRY does, by 0.000003, which is not attributed. No `references.toml` change.
