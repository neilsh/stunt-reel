# Journal: OP_PERFDISK radius

## 2026-11-12: the disc's size against a same-camera BOMB capture

- **Question**: Is `vm.rs`'s `OP_PERFDISK` radius, raw register units, the size `PLAYONE` draws, given the handler's `>> 16` where `OP_SPHERE` shifts by 8 ([#425](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/425))?
- **Method**: `mise run frame -- --film bomb --ticks 2025,2036,2048,2058,2070` on `origin/main` (`crates/` tree `bf979d3`, RTX 4070), then the disc's row spans read off its flat palette colour in the capture and ours. The handler `0xA040..0xA2BC` re-read with `mise run disasm`. A 64-gon and a 32-gon variant of `DISC_SEGMENTS`, then `mise run parity` on the 32-gon (3 min).
- **Result**: Ticks 2025, 2036 and 2048 show a different scene in the two panels. Tick 2058 matches: the car body's left edge and the disc's centre column agree to 2 px. There, at rows 172 and 184, the capture's disc is 77 and 91 px wide, the 16-gon 72 and 87 px, the 64-gon 1 to 2 px under the capture. Tick 2070 agrees likewise (capture 51, 67, 77, 85 px at rows 6, 12, 18 and 24 below its top; 64-gon 49, 65, 76, 84). The 16-gon is 4 to 6 % narrow and the 64-gon closes most of the gap; the rest is unexplained. The `0x8108` rescale makes the normal's depth component share the x and y components' scale (`0xA0DB..0xA0E8` against [`memory-map.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/memory-map.md)); `vm.rs` keeps the normal in the model frame, so it needs none. The capture's disc is about 2 px taller than ours at 2058; not isolated. 32-gon SSIM against the pinned 16-gon tables: CTRY +0.000003, INTRO -0.00003, LAND -0.00003, BALLOON 0, TRAINTNL -0.000003, BOMB -0.00014, LOCATION -0.00011.
- **Verdict**: radius agrees with the BOMB wheel disc at ticks 2058 and 2070 to about 2 px (other shapes and depths unchecked), code unchanged. The 32-gon is rejected: evidence-backed track needs one film to rise, and only CTRY does, by 0.000003, which is not attributed. No `references.toml` change.
- **Lives in**: [`sod-vm.md` § 8.8](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm-generators.md#88-model-frame-the-other-generators).

## 2026-11-18: a LOCATION ground disc read on lavapipe

- **Question**: Does the radius agree on a shape other than `BOMB`'s wheel, at another depth ([#709](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/709), the lavapipe half of [#689](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/689))?
- **Method**: Cloud session, lavapipe (`llvmpipe (LLVM 20.1.2, 256 bits)`), commit `9c9c6a5` (`crates/` tree `a00c4e7`). To find a large disc, every captured tick of `TRAINTNL` and `LOCATION` was rendered with `mise run frame -- --software-only --var DBG_DISCTAG=0,1` against a local, uncommitted edit that XORed each `OP_PERFDISK` colour with `0x55` under `DBG_DISCTAG=1`; the pixels the two stores differ on are the discs. `TRAINTNL`'s are wheels of at most a few hundred pixels across many discs; the 20 largest in `LOCATION` are one disc of 8,693 to 12,696 px at ticks 5953 to 6161. A logging edit gave its operands (radius `0x2400`, normal `(0, −10000, 0)`), and the bytes `0E 01 0C 00 00 24` found it in `LOCATION.ALL` shape `0x07C6` (48 bytes, colour `0x4A`). Row spans of index `0x4A` were then read from the capture and from the `DBG_DISCTAG=0` store (the code of `main`) at ticks 6062 and 6161, every sixth row.
- **Result**: The camera appears to match: the stones line up and the far rim is row 76 in the capture, 77 in ours. Stones stand on the rim, so on each wide row one end is a stone's edge in both panels (both read 40 to 43 on the left at 6062, 282 on the right at 6161, seen in `--crop 0,70,320,170 --scale 2` pictures); only the free ends are quoted. 6062, right end of rows 106, 112, 118: capture 298, 300, 299; ours 299, 305, 309. 6161, left end of rows 106, 112, 118, 124: capture 22, 19, 20, 23; ours 17, 14, 11, 8. Near rim: capture row 151, ours 166.
- **Verdict**: ours is larger on this ground disc: it agrees at the far rim, reaches 1 to 15 px further at the sides and 15 rows nearer the camera; cause open (normal tilt and the `0x8108` rescale, `0xD11D`'s stepped ellipse, or the placement). No code change. For [#689](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/689)'s GPU re-read: `mise run frame -- --film location --ticks 6062,6161`.
- **Lives in**: [`sod-vm-generators.md` § 8.8](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm-generators.md#88-model-frame-the-other-generators).
