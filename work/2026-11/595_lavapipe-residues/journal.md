# Journal

## 2026-11-16: Which polygon each backend draws at the four residue ticks

- **Question**: at LAND 227, BOMB 1910 and LOCATION 2599 and 2859, which polygon each backend draws on the pixels where lavapipe differs from the GPU, at what depth, and why the other polygon wins; whether LAND 227 is a Gouraud polygon drawn flat ([`lavapipe.md`](../../../research/parity/lavapipe.md) section 5 before this entry, #595 steps 1 and 2).
- **Method**: `origin/main` `03356d6e` plus an uncommitted diagnostic patch, RTX 4070 laptop beside mesa-dist-win 25.2.8 (`VK_DRIVER_FILES` naming only the lavapipe ICD, `--software-only`). `fs_main_plane` wrote one diagnostic word into the RGBA8 colour target while the plane target kept its normal `layer << 8 | index`, and `OffscreenTarget::capture` dumped both raw before the store write. Words, selected by a pipeline-overridable constant: (1) the provoking vertex's `vertex_index` (a flat varying; base vertex included) and the pipeline that drew the fragment (ground, ground edge, base, decal, edge, edge-decal, shadow; the edge-decal pipeline's label matched the decal one, so both read as decal); (2) `@builtin(position).z` scaled to 24 bits, and the pipeline; (3) the bits of the `linear` `shade`; (5) the flat `palette_index`; (6, 7) the provoking vertex's clip-space z and w, as flat varyings, so straight from the vertex stage. A second constant discarded every fragment but one pipeline's (ground or base or decal), to read a polygon's depth where another covers it. One `mise run frame` process per tick, adapter and word. Both adapters take `X8_D24_UNORM_PACK32` for `Depth24Plus` (`vulkaninfo --show-formats` lists it with `DEPTH_STENCIL_ATTACHMENT` on both; wgpu-hal 30 maps `Depth24Plus` to it when supported).
- **Result**:
  - Differing pixels and the polygon each backend draws there (pipeline, provoking `vertex_index`, index):
    - LAND 227, 3,222 pixels: GPU a world prop (base, 17501 and two neighbours, 13, `shade` 13.0); lavapipe a world ground polygon (ground, 368, 195). Drawn alone, the ground polygon's depth is the same on both (0.9940678 against 0.9940680 median, lavapipe minus GPU 1.8e-7 to 3.0e-7); the prop's is 0.9940665 on the GPU and 5.8e-6 further on lavapipe (median; 3.0e-7 to 8.8e-6). The ground pipeline compares `Always` and writes depth, so the prop's `Less` test decides: it passes by 1.3e-6 on the GPU and fails on lavapipe. The ground polygon's `shade` on the GPU is 195.0 to 195.00003 over all 37,179 of its pixels, so every vertex carries 195: flat in effect. On lavapipe it reads NaN on 21,981 of its 40,377 pixels (2,686 of the 3,222) and 195.0 on the rest; the guard draws 195 either way.
    - BOMB 1910, 506 pixels in actor layer 17: GPU a decal polygon (decal, 368, 7); lavapipe the base polygon under it (base, 0, 35). Drawn alone (`position.z`, bias as rasterised): base 0.4169038 on the GPU, 0.4170929 on lavapipe (+1.8e-4 median); decal 0.4166681 and 0.4171583 (+4.9e-4). The decal sits 2.4e-4 in front of the base on the GPU and 6.5e-5 behind it on lavapipe, so it fails `LessEqual`. Its provoking vertex leaves the vertex stage at clip z 0.35546875 on the GPU and 0.35595703 on lavapipe, w 0.85546875 on both.
    - LOCATION 2599, 5,519 pixels: GPU the ground (ground, 2610344, 14, `shade` 13.999999); lavapipe a prop (base, 2614678, 169, `shade` 169.0 finite). Drawn alone: ground 0.9941396 on the GPU, +2.7e-6 on lavapipe; prop 0.9941428 on the GPU, -4.2e-6 on lavapipe. The prop is 3.2e-6 behind the ground on the GPU and in front of it on lavapipe.
    - LOCATION 2859, 5,626 pixels: GPU the prop (base, 2614678, 169); lavapipe the ground (ground, 2610344, 14, `shade` NaN on 5,673 of the 5,736 differing pixels). Drawn alone: ground 0.9942693 on the GPU, -4.9e-6 on lavapipe (-5.8e-6 to -4.0e-6); prop 0.9942664 on the GPU, +2.0e-6 on lavapipe. The prop is 2.9e-6 in front on the GPU and behind on lavapipe.
  - The vertex stage differs: comparing each polygon visible on both adapters by its provoking vertex's clip z and w bits, z is identical in 173 of 306 polygons at LAND 227 (largest difference 2,048 ulp), 224 of 313 at BOMB 1910 (16,384 ulp) and 164 of 231 at LOCATION 2859 (128 ulp); w in 179, 228 and 158 (1,024, 8,192 and 256 ulp).
  - Polygons the side planes cannot have clipped differ too: over polygons drawn by the ground or base pipeline at the same `vertex_index` on both adapters, with at least 30 such pixels, the median of each polygon's largest depth difference (D24 units of 2^-24) is, for polygons whose pixels stay off the viewport's edge against polygons that reach it: LAND 227 986 over 22 against 5 over 7; LOCATION 2599 67 over 77 against 16 over 16; LOCATION 2859 74 over 66 against 120 over 11; BOMB 1910 33 over 2 against 1,034 over 10. A polygon wholly in front of the eye whose pixels stay off the edge has every vertex inside the side planes; one that crosses the near plane need not. LOCATION 2859's ground, which the clipper breaks, moves by 4.9e-6 (about 82 units); unclipped interior polygons in the same frame move by a median 74 and up to 259.
- **Verdict**: control (diagnostic, not committed). All four residues are near-coplanar polygons whose order on the depth test is decided by less than 2.5e-4 (BOMB) or 1.3e-6 to 3.2e-6 (LAND, LOCATION); lavapipe's depths differ from the GPU's by more than that, and the provoking vertices already differ out of the vertex stage. LAND 227 is not a Gouraud polygon drawn flat: lavapipe draws a flat ground polygon over the GPU's prop. Why the vertex stage rounds differently (for example fused multiply-adds in one compiler and not the other) is not shown.

## 2026-11-16: A perspective copy of the shade for Gouraud polygons the clipper breaks

- **Question**: does reading a `perspective` copy of `shade` where the `linear` one is not finite stop lavapipe drawing a Gouraud polygon flat, while leaving the GPU's render stores byte-identical and no film's lavapipe score worse (#595 step 3, `lavapipe.md` Open question 2)?
- **Method**: `fragment_index` falls back to `@interpolate(perspective) shade_perspective` when `shade` is not finite, and to `palette_index` when both are not. BALLOON 145 and LAND 227 rendered with and without it on both adapters (the diagnostic build of the entry above, a constant switching it). `mise run parity -- --software-only --no-build --jobs 4` on all seven films, without then with, in that build; reports compared with `mise run judge -- <without> <with> --track heuristic`. GPU: `mise run parity` on all seven films with the committed shader and with `origin/main`'s, comparing `frames.zst`, `layers.zst` and `palettes.zst` byte for byte. A GPU test, `a_gouraud_ground_polygon_under_a_low_eye_keeps_its_gradient` (a 120 m square from index 40 to 100 under the 3 m eye of `a_ground_polygon_under_a_low_eye_keeps_its_index`), run on lavapipe with and without the change.
- **Result**:
  - BALLOON 145 on lavapipe: the 4,044 pixels of the broken Gouraud polygon change from a flat 168 to 170 (2,920) and 169 (1,124); 2,954 now match the GPU and 1,090 are one index off (the `perspective` weight against the GPU's screen-linear one). Pixels differing from the GPU: 4,051 to 1,097. LAND 227 and the GPU are unchanged.
  - Lavapipe, seven films: `mean_ssim_scored` and `mean_iou_scored` identical with and without on every film (difference 0.0); the judge lists no frame moved by more than 0.005 and "none adopts" (no film above +0.002). BALLOON 145 is in the store (rendered with the fallback) but not a scored frame. Render time 1,941 s without, 1,927 s with (seven films, `--jobs 4`).
  - GPU: `frames.zst`, `layers.zst` and `palettes.zst` byte-identical with and without on all seven films. `mean_ssim_scored` with it: CTRY 0.9185, INTRO 0.8535, LAND 0.8932, BALLOON 0.8174, TRAINTNL 0.8599, BOMB 0.8444, LOCATION 0.8292. Render and score time, INTRO and LOCATION, `--no-build --jobs 1`, one run each: 323 s without, 336 s with.
  - The test fails on lavapipe without the change ("the ground draws only [40]") and passes with it.
- **Verdict**: adopted. Both of #595's conditions hold: the GPU's stores are byte-identical on every reference film's captured ticks, and no film's lavapipe score moves. No scored frame changes, so the adoption rule's score branches do not apply; the change is kept for the frames it fixes off the scored set (BALLOON 145) and for the test it makes pass on CI's adapter.
- **Lives in**: [`lavapipe.md`](../../../research/parity/lavapipe.md) sections 3 and 5.
