# Journal: seven stock shapes decode to fragments or no geometry

## 2026-11-11: each shape traced, and the two-vertex stop tested by skipping it

- **Question**: Why do `0x7F24`, `0x7F34`, `0x010D`, `0x07C6`, `0x3A80`, `0x3C01` and `0x7F4B` of the superset archive not export a whole model: an opcode `vm.rs` does not run, a mis-sized operand, or a branch it does not take ([#273](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/273))? Does a corpus film place any of them?
- **Method**: `mise run superset-all`'s archive (`SUPERSET.ALL`, 2,669 chunks, built from the game's `RES/`), then `stuntreel sod-trace --archive SUPERSET.ALL --shape 0xNNNN` for each (the lenient walk the renderer uses), the chunk bytes read with a throwaway script, and `stuntreel export-gltf` for node counts. For the two-vertex stop, a throwaway local edit of `vm.rs` that read a polygon's operands (count words for `POLYZ`, `2 * count + 1` for `GVPOLY`/`GVPOLY16`) and went on instead of stopping, run through `sod-trace`, `export-gltf` and `sod-census`; the edit was reverted, not committed. Film use: the census table of `sod-opcode-coverage.md` § 3 (2026-11-01, 719 films), and a throwaway print of every placed `shape_id` over the 22 films of this machine's corpus.
- **Result**: `0x3C01` and `0x7F4B` are not `.SOD` models: their chunks do not start with `0x000C`, so `chunk_name` calls them `RES_3C01.BIN` and `RES_7F4B.BIN` and `sod-trace` finds no shape. `0x3C01` is `LETTERS.RES` item 1, the text `Letters and Numbers` and a table of 16-bit character codes. `0x7F4B` is `PLANES.RES` item `0x4B`, 400 bytes of table data; the file holds 55 models (`0x7F02`..`0x7F38`) and 55 such tables (`0x7F39`..`0x7F6F`, 74 to 400 bytes) beside the 704-byte `0x7F01`. `0x010D` and `0x3A80` are byte-identical (288 bytes). Both run `CLRV`, `PUTV`, `VECTORS` (ten vertices), `PERS` and stop at offset `0x0058` on `OP_ANIMATE` (`0x01F8`), the opcode of issue #222. `0x07C6` is 48 bytes: `VECTORS`, `PERS`, `COLOR 0x4A`, `PERFDISK 0x000C 0x2400 0x0000`, `RETURN`. It walks clean and draws nothing, because `vm.rs` handles `OP_PERFDISK` as three skipped words ([#272](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/272)). `0x7F24` stops at `0x10A0` and `0x7F34` at `0x0F86` on a polygon of two vertices. The stream after each is in step: the two operand words are followed by a valid `IFVIS` or `GVPOLY16`, and with the stop removed `0x7F24` reaches `RETURN` (24 of them, 1,166 steps) and `0x7F34` reaches `RETURN` (26, 947 steps) with no further stop. The skipped sites are `POLYZ` at `0x10A0` and `0x10C8` and `GVPOLY16` at `0x26BE` in `0x7F24`, and `GVPOLY16` at `0x0F86`, `0x0F94`, `0x1062` and `0x1070` in `0x7F34`. `export-gltf` goes from 13 to 100 nodes for `0x7F24` and from 38 to 99 for `0x7F34`. On this machine's 31-archive corpus the same edit takes the census's models that step over an opcode from 12 to 9, all in group A (`ANIMATE`). Local films place `0x3A80` (`SCENE01.TKE`) and `0x7F24` (`PLANES.FLM`, `PLANES.TKE`); none of the 22 places `0x010D`, `0x07C6`, `0x3C01`, `0x7F34` or `0x7F4B`.
- **Verdict**: adopted: the walk does not desynchronise at a two-vertex polygon, so the stop is `vm.rs`'s own `count < 3` check. Not adopted as code: whether `PLAYONE` draws such a polygon is not read, so `vm.rs` is unchanged.
