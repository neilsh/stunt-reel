# Journal: #512 raised ground and a high actor's shadow in view

## 2026-11-16: raised ground and a high actor's shadow in view

**Question**: the two cases the first entry left open. Over raised ground (a leaf plane with `a`, `c` or `D` nonzero), does a high actor keep its leaf's plane at slot `+0x1E`, or does the probe's cube-node `y` test (`0x2547`) drop it to the null plane? And in a frame where a high actor's shadow ground point is in view, is the shadow drawn?

**Method**: at `531195d`. Static: the scene tree at image `0x22860` of `build/re/playone.img`, read with `stunt_reel.scene_tree` (ground leaves, their planes at leaf `+4..+0xD`, the image's anchor table), and the probe's dispatch table `DS:0x584` (image `0xDA94`, found by its words) and handlers, with `mise run disasm`. Offline: [`ground_probe_replay.py`](ground_probe_replay.py), run as `mise exec -- uv run python work/2026-11/shadow-raised-ground/ground_probe_replay.py <data root> cubes|census|points X,Y,Z …`. It replays the probe's ground walk by the handlers below (types 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, 16, 18, 22; type 10, objects, and type 20, lists, end a branch, as they do for the ground plane), with the sphere radius taken as 0 and the slot's position as its centre. `census` walks the corpus films' slices as [`high_shadow_census.py`](../512_altitude-cutoff/high_shadow_census.py) does, keeping every shadowed drawn slot's stream `x`, `y` and `z`, compares the replay at each slot's `y` with the replay at `y = 0`, and joins the slots more than 20 m above a non-level leaf to that leaf's polygon (`scene_tree.covers`) and plane. Live: `mise run debug-x`, unpatched build, on `corpus/siarchive/Films/MultiFLM/Unknown/THE.FLM` with two plans (not committed) built from [`shadow-cull.toml`](../../../tools/dosbox-x/plans/shadow-cull.toml): breaks at `0x1A18` (plane, capturing `DS:SI` 0x24 bytes and `SS:DI` 10), `0x1A12` (null plane), `0x1A8F` (shadow culled) and `0x1AD8` (queued, capturing `stack:1`: the return is `0x1E38` from the shadow call at `0x1E35` and `0x1E2F` from the actor call), armed at tick 830 (hits from tick 859); then `0x1AD8`, `0x1C0D` (the shadow draw, capturing the entry) and `0x1CE7` (shadow mode set), armed at tick 859 (hits from tick 884). Slot `n` is record `0x393A + 0x5A·n`.

**Result**:

* **Search, round 1.** The join found 58 films (of 529 distinct) with a shadowed drawn slot more than 20 m above a non-level leaf; 29 have a sibling `.ALL`, which `debug-x` needs to stage a film. `FLMOnly/HANG.FLM` (slot 0 at stream `y` −600, 532 m above leaf `0x4678`, tick 0) was refused for want of `HANG.ALL`. `THE.FLM` was chosen: at tick 826, early in the film, slots 3, 4 and 5 are at stream `y` −600, 81 to 104 m above leaf `0x6FF4`.
* **The probe's handlers.** `DS:0x584` by node type: 0 `SP_POS` `0x2481`, 2 `SP_1NODE` `0x2547`, 4 `SP_2NODE` `0x24D0`, 6 `SP_XPLANE` `0x24B8`, 8 `SP_ZPLANE` `0x24C2`, 10 `SP_LEAF` `0x257A`, 12 `SP_GROUND` `0x25F5`, 14 `SP_GLEAF` `0x2631`, 16 `SP_HSLOPE` `0x250D`, 18 `SP_VSLOPE` `0x252A`, 20 `SP_LIST` `0x2579` (a bare `ret`, so the probe does not walk lists), 22 `SP_POSXZ` `0x24A0`. Only `0x2547` (the cube: each of the accumulated `x`, `y`, `z` within its half-size `[bp+4]` plus `[0x2302]`) and the object node `0x257A` test `y` (`SP_POS` adds its `y` offset and `SP_2NODE` saves and restores it, with no compare). The x and z planes compare the accumulated `x` or `z` with the radius and walk one side or both; the slopes compare the slot's integer `x` and `z` (`0x230A`, `0x2312`) along a line; `SP_GROUND` tests a square in `x` and `z` (`0x25F5..0x2614`) before its child; `SP_GLEAF` counts crossings in `x` and `z`.
* **No ground leaf under a cube node.** Walking the tree by those handlers, the probe reaches 878 `SP_1NODE` nodes and 146 ground leaves with a polygon, the count of the draw walk's leaves in ([`world-scene.md` § 2](../../../research/playone-exe/world-scene.md#2-the-ground-leaves-and-their-colour)), and none of the 146 lies below a cube node. So the cube test gates objects only, and the plane a slot gets depends on its `x` and `z`, not its height. The replay (`cubes`, `points`, `census`) agrees with every outcome read live in `THE.FLM` (the seven positions below, planes and nulls alike), and over 255,913 distinct integer positions of shadowed drawn slots more than 20 m up in 372 corpus films it gives the same plane at the slot's `y` as at `y = 0` in every case.

Slot 0 (`0x393A`) on the level plane `0x370` and slot 34 (`0x452E`) on leaf `0x7FB0`'s (`3351, 25662, 19543, 61248467`) hit the same branch. The null branch `0x1A12` was hit at tick 919 by slot 8 at (−3441.2, −427.1, 4281.0), inside leaf `0x6FF4`'s polygon about 425 m above its plane, at tick 1031 by slot 34 at (−3435.9, −100.0, 4032.5), and from tick 1856 by slot 0 (recorded by the same run, though its plan's `stop_tick` was 1100; why it ran on was not checked) at (3414.1, −0.7, −849.8), over no leaf. The replay gives null for the first two at `y = 0` as well: the walk's `x` and `z` splits take them away from `0x6FF4`, so they are a gap between the tree's splits and the leaf's polygon, not an altitude effect.
* **A high actor's shadow in view, live.** At ticks 859 and 862 the fourteen shadows of slots 3 to 16 passed the cull: `0x1AD8` hit with return `0x1E38` and `AL = 0`, after the same slots' actor entries (return `0x1E2F`, `AL = 0x80`). At ticks 884 and 885, with slots 3 to 16 at stream `y` −528.4 and −508.6 and 181 to 270 m above leaf `0x6FF4`'s plane (still `+0x1E` = `0x6FF8`; ground `y` −259 to −327 under them, the formation having moved about 70 m along `x` towards the plane's high side), all fourteen shadow entries (byte `+0xF` = 0, entry `+4` the slot) reached `0x1C0D` and set shadow mode at `0x1CE7`, at view points `x` −901 to −787, `y` 2360 to 2581, depth 8365 to 9058; tick 888 drew slot 3's again.
* **Pixels, not done.** A frame capture of `THE.FLM` (`mise run capture-ref -- --film … --game-root builds/unpatched --out <scratch>`) relaunched DOSBox repeatedly for about 20 minutes without writing a frame and was stopped, so whether the drawn shadows show in the frame was not checked ([#628](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/628)).

**Verdict**: adopted as findings. The probe picks the ground plane by `x` and `z` alone, since no ground leaf lies below a cube node, its one ground-walk node that reads `y` (`[Established]`, static), which corrects the earlier reading that a slot above a cube's range falls to the `y = 0` plane. Over raised ground a high actor keeps its leaf's plane (`[Bounded Observation]`: `THE.FLM` ticks 859 to 865, slots 3 to 16, 129 to 230 m up, `+0x1E` = `0x6FF8`). With its ground point in view a high actor's shadow reaches the draw (`[Bounded Observation]`: `THE.FLM` ticks 884 and 885, slots 3 to 16, 181 to 270 m up, all fourteen at `0x1C0D` with shadow mode set). With the first entry's cull frames, no height test was seen hiding a shadow in `PLAYONE` at the heights observed; that the view cull is the whole of the handbook's cutoff stays `[Hypothesis]` (one film for the in-view case, pixels not checked). Ruled out: `HANG.FLM` (no `.ALL` to stage); the null planes at ticks 919 and 1031 as an altitude effect (the replay gives null at `y = 0`).
