# Journal: sprite draw order

## 2026-11-12: sprite with no depth test, in the actor's place in the sort

- **Question**: Does drawing the `OP_ANIMATE` square without a depth test, in its actor's place in `PLAYONE`'s far-to-near entry sort, put explosions over their neighbours as the capture does and raise the score?
- **Method**: Evidence-backed track (image `0xA9DC` handler, `0x7DE6` blit with no depth test, `0x1B88` entry sort, [`sod-vm.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm.md) section 9.1), on `dbfd0ed4`, RTX 4070. A temporary sprite pipeline with depth compare `Always`, three orders: `0` the adopted depth-tested draw; `1` the sprite drawn among world and actors sorted by actor eye distance, far first, with and without depth write; `3` the sprite drawn after every base and decal draw. `mise run sweep -- --var DBG_SPRITE_MODE=0,1,3 --var DBG_SPRITE_WRITE=0,1 --films ctry,intro,bomb`. The switches were not committed.
- **Result**: `ctry` / `intro` / `bomb` SSIM:
  - `0` (baseline): 0.8996 / 0.8418 / 0.8101.
  - `1`, no depth write: 0.8984 / 0.8404 / 0.8100.
  - `1`, depth write: 0.8988 / 0.8418 / 0.8101.
  - `3` (write on or off, identical): 0.8984 / 0.8422 / 0.8101.
  - IoU unchanged at four places in every variant on the three films. Mode `3` rises `intro` by 0.0004 and falls `ctry` by 0.0012; mode `1` rises no film.
  - `CTRY` tick 871 (capture beside the three orders, [picture](images/ctry_871.png)): the capture appears to draw the explosion over the tower beside it but under the large foreground hill at the left, which appears to cut it. Orders `1` and `3` both draw the whole square over the hill. `[Hypothesis]`, from this one frame and not checked in code: the tower is an actor that sorts before the explosion, and the hill is terrain in the world draw (first in both orders) that the capture draws after it.
  - The sprite's reach over the hill is the likely loss, not isolated: where the capture keeps the hill in front, the no-depth draw pays more than the tower gains.
- **Verdict**: not adopted. Mode `3` meets the evidence-backed bar on paper (one film rises, none falls by 0.002) but does not match the capture at tick 871 where the hill appears to hide the explosion; mode `1` rises nothing. That the blit takes its actor's slot is inferred from the sort (sod-vm.md 9.1) and the painter's-order wording of sod-animate.md section 3, not traced through the actor draw (`0x1C00`). Matching the capture may need the terrain in the sort too (`[Hypothesis]`), which this renderer's one world draw does not give. Scores are one run on one GPU; the baseline differs from sod-animate.md section 4's because `main` has moved since.
- **Lives in**: [`sod-animate.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-animate.md) § 3 and § Open questions.

## 2026-11-17: sprite masked by stencil against the world, over farther actors

- **Question**: Does drawing the `OP_ANIMATE` square last, with no depth test over the pixels of actors that sort no nearer than its own, but depth-tested where the world holds the pixel, keep the hill in front of the `CTRY` explosion and put the explosion over the tower beside it?
- **Method**: Heuristic track: the no-depth-test blit (`0x7DE6`) and the far-to-near entry sort (`0x1B88`) are read from `PLAYONE`, but how the world's terrain and props sit in that sort is not. Branch `agent/issue-407` from `5dac92e`, lavapipe (llvmpipe), cloud session. Depth target `Depth24PlusStencil8`, cleared to stencil 255 (world); actor draws write their far-to-near rank by eye distance (nearest 254). `DBG_SPRITE_STENCIL`: `0` the adopted depth-tested draw; `1` sprites drawn after everything, through `stencil == 255` with `LessEqual` depth and `rank >= stencil` with no depth test; `2` as `1`, with world prop pixels marked rank 0 before the sprites; `3` as `2`, with each world prop slice marked by its node's rank by distance from the eye to its box centre, sorted with the actors. Targets, quoted before the sweep: `--target ctry:859-881 --target intro:1387-1409 --target intro:2707-2729`.
- **Result**: film-mean SSIM, `0` / `1` / `2` / `3`, from `mise run sweep -- --var DBG_SPRITE_STENCIL=0,1,2,3 --track heuristic` with the targets above:
  - `ctry` 0.9186 / 0.9186 / 0.9174 / 0.9186; `intro` 0.8593, `land` 0.8935, `balloon` 0.8172, `traintnl` 0.8598, `bomb` 0.8446 and `location` 0.8287 in all four.
  - Pooled target SSIM change: `1` and `3` +0.0002 (index agreement −0.0005), no frame down by more than 0.005; `2` −0.0151 (index −0.0122), worst target frame −0.0644, three frames down by more than 0.005. IoU unchanged at four places in every variant.
  - `CTRY` tick 871 (`mise run frame -- --film ctry --ticks 871 --var DBG_SPRITE_STENCIL=...`, not committed: the folder is frozen for new files): `1` and `3` draw the same frame as `0`; `2` draws the explosion over the tower and over the foreground hill.
  - Why `1` changes nothing there: the plane target's layer ids at tick 871 put both the tower and the foreground hill in the world draw as props (layer 3, `LAYER_PROP`), not actors; the explosion is actor 1 (layer 17). So the tower is cut by the world's depth, which `1` keeps.
  - Why `3` changes nothing there: each world prop polygon is its own `WORLD_GATED` node, grouped under a `WORLD_LOD` node per placed object. By box centre, the explosion's actor is 22.3 m from the eye, the two object groups beside it 20.3 m and 21.8 m (their polygons 19.3 to 21.9 m), and the hill's 10.7 m; so a sort by eye distance puts the tower nearer than the explosion, and it still covers it.
- **Verdict**: rejected by the adoption rule (`winner: none adopts`; best `1`, film means short of +0.002 each and target index −0.0005). `2` fails the film-mean and target guards and covers the hill as the 2026-11-12 orders did. A lead for where the actors sit among the world, from notes already written and not traced here (`[Hypothesis]`): the scenery's display list draws pending-object lists at its object buckets (`OP_IPG` type 4, image `0xB54C`, heads at `DGROUP:0x2346`, handed to `0x1B75`, which sits just before the entry sort `0x1B88`), so an actor may be painted at its bucket's place in the world's painter order rather than sorted against world objects by eye distance. Whether the actor queue `0x1A90` feeds those heads, and which bucket an entry joins, is not read. Lavapipe, one run; the change touched depth testing and draw order, which `cloud.md` § Rendering sends to the GPU, but no variant adopts on lavapipe either.
