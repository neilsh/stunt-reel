# Journal: #512 the handbook's altitude cutoff for actor shadows

## 2026-11-16: the shadow's cull, the display-list run and STUNT.EXE's shadow draw

**Question**: where the handbook's altitude cutoff lives. The handbook (p.81 by its index; `SIManual.txt:5039-5066`) says of a prop's SHD option: "If the object is airborne, a shadow will only appear when it's close enough to the ground to cast a shadow." The three tests of [#512](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/512): reread the display-list run inside `0x1D5F` (`0824:0EC0`) for a `y` compare; break on `0x19FC` for a high actor over raised ground and read slot `+0x1E`; find `STUNT.EXE`'s shadow draw and look for a height compare.

**Method**: at `bcb4df8`. Static: `mise run disasm` over the unpatched `PLAYONE` load image (data root `build/re/playone.img`), and `mise run unlzexe` of `corpus/siarchive/Films/OldPLAY/STUNT.EXE` to `build/re/stunt.img` (248,460 bytes), read with `mise run disasm -- … --image`, plus byte-pattern searches of both images (scratch scripts, not committed). Live: `mise run debug-x` on DOSBox-X, unpatched build, with the plan now at [`tools/dosbox-x/plans/shadow-cull.toml`](../../../tools/dosbox-x/plans/shadow-cull.toml) and earlier drafts of it: `CTRY.FLM` from tick 1 (plane branch only, 254 hits, ticks 70 to 848) and armed at tick 100; `Films/NewPLAY/Vault/FLIP.FLM` armed at ticks 120 and 1000; an `INTRO.FLM` run from tick 1 was stopped at tick 553 (slow, every hit a level actor). A walk of the corpus `.FLM` streams ([`high_shadow_census.py`](high_shadow_census.py), recomputed with it: `mise exec -- uv run python work/2026-11/512_altitude-cutoff/high_shadow_census.py <data root>/corpus`) (529 distinct by SHA-256; a film the walker rejects counts as having none) found a drawn slot with `SIF_EXISTS`, `SIF_DRAW` and `SIF_SHADOW` more than 100 m up (stream `y` below −100) in 262 of them, and chose `FLIP.FLM`: slots 8 and 29 hold drawn shapes with `SIF_SHADOW`, slot 29 at 383.5 m wherever sampled and slot 8 at about 382 m until tick 360, then coming down to the ground by tick 1264.

**Result**:

* **Test 1, the display-list run.** `0x1D5F` passes the VM (`0824:0EC0`, image `0x9100`) the shape (`ES`, `SI = [es:2]`, the radius `BP = [es:4]`, `CH = [es:8]`), the slot's animate word (`work:0x8124`) and the pushed shape word; the slot pointer is overwritten at `0x1D81` before the far call. The point the VM draws at is `work:0x8084..0x8088`, written at `0x1CD8..0x1CE6` from the queue entry's view-space position, which for a shadow is the ground point, not the actor. The VM entry `0x9100..0x917E` compares only that view-space point and the radius, to choose between two sets of polygon routines (`0x914F` or `0x9167`), both of which run the stream. The VM code (`0x8240..0xC000`) loads `DGROUP` at 12 relocations (`0x8AF1`, `0x8B58`, `0x8BAF`, `0x8C00`, `0x8E8A`, `0xB551`, `0xB5A2`, `0xBCEF`, `0xBDAF`, `0xBDD6`, `0xBE88`, `0xBED0`): the span arena, the pending-object heads `0x2346` (`OP_IPG` type 4) and the tick `0x22B8`; its `[bp+…]` reads use `SS`, which the live breaks show is the scenery segment (`SS = 0x2AAA`, `DS = 0x1575`); none reaches a slot record. No `y` compare: ruled out.
* **Test 2, the live break.** Every sampled shadow got its leaf's plane, none the null branch: `CTRY.FLM` slot 0 (record `DS:0x393A`) at 1.2 to 12.2 m on the plane at `SS:0x033C` (`a = 0`, `b = 30933`, `c = 0`, `D = 0`, so `y = 0`); `FLIP.FLM` slots 8 and 29 (records `0x3C0A`, `0x436C`) at 382.5 and 383.5 m (ticks 71 to 156) and slot 8 at 222.6 to 225.6 m (ticks 1036 to 1043), all on the plane at `SS:0x0320` (`0, 20342, 0, 0`, again `y = 0`). So at these heights the probe's cube-node test (`0x2547`) does not drop the leaf. Raised ground was not reached: every plane read was level at `y = 0`, and no high actor over a raised leaf was located. **Every one of those shadows was then rejected by the cull** `0x1B0C` (`0x1A8F` hit after each `0x1A8A`), and no shadow entry reached `0x1C0D` (in `CTRY.FLM`, ticks 124 to 154, the one entry queued at `0x1B02` and drawn at `0x1C00` each frame was slot 0's actor, byte `+0xF` = `0x80`). Reading the cull with the registers at `0x1A8A`, its limit is `|x| ≤ (z + (r >> [0x229E])/2)·[0x22A0] / 65536 + r·[0x229C] / 65536` and the same for `y` with `[0x22A2]` (`r` the shape radius `[es:4]` shifted to the point's exponent, `z` the view depth; read live as `[0x22A0] = 40960`, `[0x22A2] = 23552`, and `[0x229C]`, `[0x229E]` = 32767, 0 in `CTRY.FLM` and 32726, 3 in `FLIP.FLM`). `CTRY` tick 131: the shadow point is at view `y = 10032`, `z = 9728`, `r = 3097`, against a limit of 5600, so below the frame's bottom edge; the actor is at `y = 36`. `FLIP` ticks 150 to 156: the shadow points have depth −262 to −283 (behind the eye; `0x1B3E`). `FLIP` ticks 1036 and 1039, slot 8: `y = 884` and `906` at `z = 1400`, `r = 15`, against a limit of 510 (with `[0x229E]` = 3 the radius term in the depth is `(15 >> 3) / 2` = 0, so 1400 · 23552 / 65536 = 503, plus 15 · 32726 / 65536 = 7). In each case the ground point lies outside the view, so the shadow is not drawn.
* **Test 3, `STUNT.EXE`.** Its scene walk and shadow queue are `PLAYONE`'s instructions, `DGROUP` offsets aside (the view eye is `0x99B6` for `0x2A68`): `PLAYONE 0x19FC..0x1A3F` is `STUNT 0x6FC3`, the walk `0x1E2F` is `0x73F6`, `0x1C41` (the plane's Y-row rewrite) is `0x7208` and the cull `0x1B0C` is `0x70D3`, each `+0x55C7`. `0x6FC3` has one caller (`0x73FC`, in that walk); the actor queue `0x7057` has a second (`0xE4EC`) with no shadow call. Its plane writer, `mov [si+1Eh],ax` at `0x7C85`, sits after a crossing count like `PLAYONE`'s `0x26BE`. The writers of the slot's shadow bit (`+1` bit 2) found by byte pattern: `xor byte [di+1],4` at `0xDB2B` (one of a run of flag toggles with `0xDB1D` `xor [di],8`, `0xDB24` `xor [di],20h`, `0xDB38` `xor [di+50h],80h`: the prop editor's options), `or al,4` at `0xCD96` from the prop's `[di]` bit `0x40` (stored at `0xCD98`), `or byte [di+1],4` at `0xBE88` (a fixed setup) and the film-stream create at `0x92C4`. None of them compares a height. The byte-pattern search covers `test`, `or`, `and`, `xor` and `mov` on `[reg+1]`; a write through another addressing form would be missed.

**Verdict**: adopted as findings. Test 1: no `y` compare in the display-list run, `[Established]` (static). Test 2: shadows of actors 1.2 to 383.5 m above level ground keep their plane, and those of actors 10 to 383.5 m up checked at the cull are lost in the cull `0x1B0C` because their ground point lies outside the view, `[Bounded Observation]` (`CTRY.FLM` ticks 131 to 144 slot 0; `FLIP.FLM` ticks 150 to 156 slots 8 and 29, ticks 1036 to 1043 slot 8); the raised-ground case was not done (no such frame located). Test 3: `STUNT.EXE` draws film shadows with the same code and sets the shadow bit only from the editor, the prop record, a fixed setup and the stream, `[Established]` for the code match and the writers listed, `[Supported Inference]` that no other writer exists (pattern search). So no height compare was found in the draw path: in the frames sampled, the handbook's "close enough to the ground" matches the view cull of the shadow's ground point. That this is the whole of the handbook's cutoff is `[Hypothesis]` (`[Supported Inference]` in `docs/SPEC.md` needs parity gates across films, which this has not passed): no frame was observed with a high actor whose ground point is in view.

## 2026-11-16: raised ground and a high actor's shadow in view

**Question**: the two cases the first entry left open. Over raised ground (a leaf plane with `a`, `c` or `D` nonzero), does a high actor keep its leaf's plane at slot `+0x1E`, or does the probe's cube-node `y` test (`0x2547`) drop it to the null plane? And in a frame where a high actor's shadow ground point is in view, is the shadow drawn?

**Method**: at `531195d`. Static: the scene tree at image `0x22860` of `build/re/playone.img`, read with `stunt_reel.scene_tree` (ground leaves, their planes at leaf `+4..+0xD`, the image's anchor table), and the probe's dispatch table `DS:0x584` (image `0xDA94`, found by its words) and handlers, with `mise run disasm`. Search: a scratch join (not committed: a new file cannot enter this folder) of [`high_shadow_census.py`](high_shadow_census.py)'s slices, every shadowed drawn slot with its stream `x`, `y`, `z`, against the 146 ground leaves' polygons (`scene_tree.covers`) and planes, keeping slots more than 20 m above a non-level leaf. A second scratch script replays the probe's ground walk (types 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 22 by the handlers below, the sphere radius taken as 0) for a slot's integer position. Live: `mise run debug-x`, unpatched build, on `corpus/siarchive/Films/MultiFLM/Unknown/THE.FLM` with two scratch plans built from [`shadow-cull.toml`](../../../tools/dosbox-x/plans/shadow-cull.toml): breaks at `0x1A18` (plane, capturing `DS:SI` 0x24 bytes and `SS:DI` 10), `0x1A12` (null plane), `0x1A8F` (shadow culled) and `0x1AD8` (queued, capturing `stack:1`: the return is `0x1E38` from the shadow call at `0x1E35` and `0x1E2F` from the actor call), armed at tick 830 (hits from tick 859); then `0x1AD8`, `0x1C0D` (the shadow draw, capturing the entry) and `0x1CE7` (shadow mode set), armed at tick 859 (hits from tick 884). Slot `n` is record `0x393A + 0x5A·n`.

**Result**:

* **Search, round 1.** The join found 58 films (of 529 distinct) with a shadowed drawn slot more than 20 m above a non-level leaf; 29 have a sibling `.ALL`, which `debug-x` needs to stage a film. `FLMOnly/HANG.FLM` (slot 0 at stream `y` −600, 532 m above leaf `0x4678`, tick 0) was refused for want of `HANG.ALL`. `THE.FLM` was chosen: at tick 826, early in the film, slots 3, 4 and 5 are at stream `y` −600, 81 to 104 m above leaf `0x6FF4`.
* **The probe's handlers.** `DS:0x584` by node type: 0 `SP_POS` `0x2481`, 2 `SP_1NODE` `0x2547`, 4 `SP_2NODE` `0x24D0`, 6 `SP_XPLANE` `0x24B8`, 8 `SP_ZPLANE` `0x24C2`, 10 `SP_LEAF` `0x257A`, 12 `SP_GROUND` `0x25F5`, 14 `SP_GLEAF` `0x2631`, 16 `SP_HSLOPE` `0x250D`, 18 `SP_VSLOPE` `0x252A`, 20 `SP_LIST` `0x2579` (a bare `ret`, so the probe does not walk lists), 22 `SP_POSXZ` `0x24A0`. Only `0x2547` (the cube: each of the accumulated `x`, `y`, `z` within its half-size `[bp+4]` plus `[0x2302]`) and the object node `0x257A` test `y` (`SP_POS` adds its `y` offset and `SP_2NODE` saves and restores it, with no compare). The x and z planes compare the accumulated `x` or `z` with the radius and walk one side or both; the slopes compare the slot's integer `x` and `z` (`0x230A`, `0x2312`) along a line; `SP_GROUND` tests a square in `x` and `z` (`0x25F5..0x2614`) before its child; `SP_GLEAF` counts crossings in `x` and `z`.
* **No ground leaf under a cube node.** Walking the tree by those handlers, the probe reaches 878 `SP_1NODE` nodes and 146 ground leaves with a polygon, the count of the draw walk's leaves in ([`world-scene.md` § 2](../../../research/playone-exe/world-scene.md#2-the-ground-leaves-and-their-colour)), and none of the 146 lies below a cube node. So the cube test gates objects only, and the plane a slot gets depends on its `x` and `z`, not its height. The scratch replay agrees with every outcome read live in `THE.FLM` (the seven positions below, planes and nulls alike), and over 255,913 distinct integer positions of shadowed drawn slots more than 20 m up in 372 corpus films it gives the same plane at the slot's `y` as at `y = 0` in every case.
* **Raised ground, live.** `THE.FLM` ticks 859 to 865: slots 3 to 16, at stream `y` −559.6 and −539.8 and 129 to 230 m above the plane under them, hit `0x1A18` with `+0x1E` = `DI` = `0x6FF8`, leaf `0x6FF4`'s plane `a = 30129`, `b = 25427`, `c = 738`, `D = −100565505` (ground `y` −322 to −411 under them: the plane slopes steeply in `x`), 35 hits. Slot 0 (`0x393A`) on the level plane `0x370` and slot 34 (`0x452E`) on leaf `0x7FB0`'s (`3351, 25662, 19543, 61248467`) hit the same branch. The null branch `0x1A12` was hit at tick 919 by slot 8 at (−3441.2, −427.1, 4281.0), inside leaf `0x6FF4`'s polygon about 425 m above its plane, at tick 1031 by slot 34 at (−3435.9, −100.0, 4032.5), and from tick 1856 by slot 0 (recorded by the same run, though its plan's `stop_tick` was 1100; why it ran on was not checked) at (3414.1, −0.7, −849.8), over no leaf. The replay gives null for the first two at `y = 0` as well: the walk's `x` and `z` splits take them away from `0x6FF4`, so they are a gap between the tree's splits and the leaf's polygon, not an altitude effect.
* **A high actor's shadow in view, live.** At ticks 859 and 862 the fourteen shadows of slots 3 to 16 passed the cull: `0x1AD8` hit with return `0x1E38` and `AL = 0`, after the same slots' actor entries (return `0x1E2F`, `AL = 0x80`). At ticks 884 and 885, with slots 3 to 16 at stream `y` −528.4 and −508.6 and 181 to 270 m above leaf `0x6FF4`'s plane (still `+0x1E` = `0x6FF8`; ground `y` −259 to −327 under them, the formation having moved about 70 m along `x` towards the plane's high side), all fourteen shadow entries (byte `+0xF` = 0, entry `+4` the slot) reached `0x1C0D` and set shadow mode at `0x1CE7`, at view points `x` −901 to −787, `y` 2360 to 2581, depth 8365 to 9058; tick 888 drew slot 3's again.
* **Pixels, not done.** A frame capture of `THE.FLM` (`mise run capture-ref -- --film … --game-root builds/unpatched --out <scratch>`) relaunched DOSBox repeatedly for about 20 minutes without writing a frame and was stopped, so whether the drawn shadows show in the frame was not checked.

**Verdict**: adopted as findings. The probe picks the ground plane by `x` and `z` alone, since no ground leaf lies below a cube node, its one ground-walk node that reads `y` (`[Established]`, static), which corrects the earlier reading that a slot above a cube's range falls to the `y = 0` plane. Over raised ground a high actor keeps its leaf's plane (`[Bounded Observation]`: `THE.FLM` ticks 859 to 865, slots 3 to 16, 129 to 230 m up, `+0x1E` = `0x6FF8`). With its ground point in view a high actor's shadow reaches the draw (`[Bounded Observation]`: `THE.FLM` ticks 884 and 885, slots 3 to 16, 181 to 270 m up, all fourteen at `0x1C0D` with shadow mode set). With the first entry's cull frames, no height test was seen hiding a shadow in `PLAYONE` at the heights observed; that the view cull is the whole of the handbook's cutoff stays `[Hypothesis]` (one film for the in-view case, pixels not checked). Ruled out: `HANG.FLM` (no `.ALL` to stage); the null planes at ticks 919 and 1031 as an altitude effect (the replay gives null at `y = 0`).
