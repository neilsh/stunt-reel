# Journal: #512 the handbook's altitude cutoff for actor shadows

## 2026-11-16: the shadow's cull, the display-list run and STUNT.EXE's shadow draw

**Question**: where the handbook's altitude cutoff lives. The handbook (p.81 by its index; `SIManual.txt:5039-5066`) says of a prop's SHD option: "If the object is airborne, a shadow will only appear when it's close enough to the ground to cast a shadow." The three tests of [#512](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/512): reread the display-list run inside `0x1D5F` (`0824:0EC0`) for a `y` compare; break on `0x19FC` for a high actor over raised ground and read slot `+0x1E`; find `STUNT.EXE`'s shadow draw and look for a height compare.

**Method**: at `bcb4df8`. Static: `mise run disasm` over the unpatched `PLAYONE` load image (data root `build/re/playone.img`), and `mise run unlzexe` of `corpus/siarchive/Films/OldPLAY/STUNT.EXE` to `build/re/stunt.img` (248,460 bytes), read with `mise run disasm -- … --image`, plus byte-pattern searches of both images (scratch scripts, not committed). Live: `mise run debug-x` on DOSBox-X, unpatched build, with the plan now at [`tools/dosbox-x/plans/shadow-cull.toml`](../../../tools/dosbox-x/plans/shadow-cull.toml) and earlier drafts of it: `CTRY.FLM` from tick 1 (plane branch only, 254 hits, ticks 70 to 848) and armed at tick 100; `FLMs/NewPLAY/Vault/FLIP.FLM` armed at ticks 120 and 1000; an `INTRO.FLM` run from tick 1 was stopped at tick 553 (slow, every hit a level actor). A scratch walk of the corpus `.FLM` streams (529 distinct by SHA-256; a film the walker rejects counts as having none) found a drawn slot with `SIF_EXISTS`, `SIF_DRAW` and `SIF_SHADOW` more than 100 m up (stream `y` below −100) in 262 of them, and chose `FLIP.FLM`: slots 8 and 29 hold drawn shapes with `SIF_SHADOW`, slot 29 at 383.5 m wherever sampled and slot 8 at about 382 m until tick 360, then coming down to the ground by tick 1264.

**Result**:

* **Test 1, the display-list run.** `0x1D5F` passes the VM (`0824:0EC0`, image `0x9100`) the shape (`ES`, `SI = [es:2]`, the radius `BP = [es:4]`, `CH = [es:8]`), the slot's animate word (`work:0x8124`) and the pushed shape word; the slot pointer is overwritten at `0x1D81` before the far call. The point the VM draws at is `work:0x8084..0x8088`, written at `0x1CD8..0x1CE6` from the queue entry's view-space position, which for a shadow is the ground point, not the actor. The VM entry `0x9100..0x917E` compares only that view-space point and the radius, to choose between two sets of polygon routines (`0x914F` or `0x9167`), both of which run the stream. The VM code (`0x8240..0xC000`) loads `DGROUP` at 12 relocations (`0x8AF1`, `0x8B58`, `0x8BAF`, `0x8C00`, `0x8E8A`, `0xB551`, `0xB5A2`, `0xBCEF`, `0xBDAF`, `0xBDD6`, `0xBE88`, `0xBED0`): the span arena, the pending-object heads `0x2346` (`OP_IPG` type 4) and the tick `0x22B8`; its `[bp+…]` reads use `SS`, which the live breaks show is the scenery segment (`SS = 0x2AAA`, `DS = 0x1575`); none reaches a slot record. No `y` compare: ruled out.
* **Test 2, the live break.** Every sampled shadow got its leaf's plane, none the null branch: `CTRY.FLM` slot 0 (record `DS:0x393A`) at 1.2 to 12.2 m on the plane at `SS:0x033C` (`a = 0`, `b = 30933`, `c = 0`, `D = 0`, so `y = 0`); `FLIP.FLM` slots 8 and 29 (records `0x3C0A`, `0x436C`) at 382.5 and 383.5 m (ticks 71 to 156) and slot 8 at 222.6 to 225.6 m (ticks 1036 to 1043), all on the plane at `SS:0x0320` (`0, 20342, 0, 0`, again `y = 0`). So at these heights the probe's cube-node test (`0x2547`) does not drop the leaf. Raised ground was not reached: every plane read was level at `y = 0`, and no high actor over a raised leaf was located. **Every one of those shadows was then rejected by the cull** `0x1B0C` (`0x1A8F` hit after each `0x1A8A`), and no shadow entry reached `0x1C0D` (in `CTRY.FLM`, ticks 124 to 154, the one entry queued at `0x1B02` and drawn at `0x1C00` each frame was slot 0's actor, byte `+0xF` = `0x80`). Reading the cull with the registers at `0x1A8A`, its limit is `|x| ≤ (z + r/2)·[0x22A0] / 65536 + r·[0x229C] / 65536` and the same for `y` with `[0x22A2]` (`r` the shape radius `[es:4]` shifted to the point's exponent, `z` the view depth; read live as `[0x22A0] = 40960`, `[0x22A2] = 23552`). `CTRY` tick 131: the shadow point is at view `y = 10032`, `z = 9728`, `r = 3097`, against a limit of 5600, so below the frame's bottom edge; the actor is at `y = 36`. `FLIP` ticks 150 to 156: the shadow points have depth −262 to −283 (behind the eye; `0x1B3E`). `FLIP` ticks 1036 and 1039, slot 8: `y = 884` and `906` at `z = 1400`, `r = 15`, against a limit of 510. In each case the ground point lies outside the view, so the shadow is not drawn.
* **Test 3, `STUNT.EXE`.** Its scene walk and shadow queue are `PLAYONE`'s instructions, `DGROUP` offsets aside (the view eye is `0x99B6` for `0x2A68`): `PLAYONE 0x19FC..0x1A3F` is `STUNT 0x6FC3`, the walk `0x1E2F` is `0x73F6`, `0x1C41` (the plane's Y-row rewrite) is `0x7208` and the cull `0x1B0C` is `0x70D3`, each `+0x55C7`. `0x6FC3` has one caller (`0x73FC`, in that walk); the actor queue `0x7057` has a second (`0xE4EC`) with no shadow call. Its plane writer, `mov [si+1Eh],ax` at `0x7C85`, sits after a crossing count like `PLAYONE`'s `0x26BE`. The writers of the slot's shadow bit (`+1` bit 2) found by byte pattern: `xor byte [di+1],4` at `0xDB2B` (one of a run of flag toggles with `0xDB1D` `xor [di],8`, `0xDB24` `xor [di],20h`, `0xDB38` `xor [di+50h],80h`: the prop editor's options), `or al,4` at `0xCD96` from the prop's `[di]` bit `0x40` (stored at `0xCD98`), `or byte [di+1],4` at `0xBE88` (a fixed setup) and the film-stream create at `0x92C4`. None of them compares a height. The byte-pattern search covers `test`, `or`, `and`, `xor` and `mov` on `[reg+1]`; a write through another addressing form would be missed.

**Verdict**: adopted as findings. Test 1: no `y` compare in the display-list run, `[Established]` (static). Test 2: shadows of actors 10 to 383 m above level ground keep their plane and are lost in the cull `0x1B0C` because their ground point lies outside the view, `[Bounded Observation]` (`CTRY.FLM` ticks 131 to 144 slot 0; `FLIP.FLM` ticks 150 to 156 slots 8 and 29, ticks 1036 to 1043 slot 8); the raised-ground case was not done (no such frame located). Test 3: `STUNT.EXE` draws film shadows with the same code and sets the shadow bit only from the editor, the prop record, a fixed setup and the stream, `[Established]` for the code match and the writers listed, `[Supported Inference]` that no other writer exists (pattern search). So no height compare was found anywhere: the handbook's "close enough to the ground" matches the view cull of the shadow's ground point, which drops the shadow whenever the camera framing the actor does not also frame the ground beneath it. That this is the whole of the handbook's cutoff is `[Supported Inference]`: no frame was observed with a high actor whose ground point is in view.
