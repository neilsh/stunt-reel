# Journal: #504, the black trapezoid in `location`

## 2026-11-16: the trapezoid is the rotor's slow arm at rest

- **Question**: what draws the black quadrilateral over `location`'s foreground at ticks 717 to 903, and is the capture's difference a frozen versus spinning blade or wrong geometry?
- **Method**: on `4d0902c9` (main after #505), `mise run parity -- --films location` on the GPU, then `mise run frame -- --film location --ticks 760,837,848,881`. `stuntreel sod-trace --archive <data root>/captures/vault-location/archives/LOCATION.ALL --shape 0x3E57`, and the same with a temporary switch forcing `BVGT` on operand `0x12` to take its branch (`DBG_BVGT=1`, not committed), rendered with `mise run frame -- --var DBG_BVGT=0,1`. Static read of the `BVGT` dispatch slot (`0x853D`, unpatched) and its handler `0x9C23` with `mise run disasm`. A live run of [`rotor-bank-location.toml`](../../../tools/dosbox-x/plans/rotor-bank-location.toml) (`mise run debug-x -- --film corpus/siarchive/Films/OldPLAY/Vault/LOCATION.FLM --build patch3`; that `.FLM` is byte-identical to `corpus/stuntisl/VAULT/LOCATION.FLM`, and its `.ALL` is not the reference capture's), breaking at the bank writer's `ret` `0x1DD4` (`0x1EE7` on Patch #3) from tick 820: 30 stops, ticks 836 to 901. Capture pixel counts from the reference frames, rows 100 to 184, indices other than 76.
- **Result**:
  - The trapezoid persists after #495: `location` scores ssim 0.8292, iou 0.9784, matching its expected row, and at ticks 837, 848 and 881 our render draws the same black trapezoid with a red top edge at the bottom centre.
  - Shape `0x3E57` turns its main rotor by `ROTY` on bank operand `0x14` inside `PUSHRELMAT`/`POPRELMAT` (stream `0x0D7E..0x0D98`), then `BVGT 0012 0FA0 000A`. Not taken, it calls `0x13E2`: the hub and two solid blades, colour 0 polygons with colour `0x17` (23) faces. Taken, it calls `0x18A0`: the hub and the blades as `ZLINETO` lines. The tail rotor (`0x0E76`) has the same pattern with `ROTX`. Our VM reads the bank words `ROT*` and `BVGT` name as 0, so it always draws the solid blades in their rest pose, pointing at the camera: the trapezoid.
  - The `BVGT` handler `0x9C23` reads the operand word into `BX`, compares `[BX + 0x810E]` with the threshold and branches by the rel word unless the bank word is below it (`jb`): taken when `bank[op] >= val`, unsigned.
  - Live, bank word `0x12` was `0x1D00` to `0x9000` on all 30 frames (var 10 moved 29 to 144 between frames, 2 or 3 ticks apart), always at or above `0x0FA0`, so under DOSBox-X at 8000 cycles `PLAYONE` drew the line arm in every frame; bank `0x14` changed from each frame to the next.
  - With the switch our render draws short lines near the horizon instead of the trapezoid. The capture has 63 to 69 index-0 pixels in rows 100 to 184 at ticks 826, 848, 859, 870 and 892, and large index-0 and index-23 shapes at 837 (7,454 of index 0, 197 of 23) and 881 (4,785 and 201). In the `mise run frame` panels (capture beside render, not kept) at 837 and 881 they are two different silhouettes leaning left, unlike our centred trapezoid at both ticks and absent at 848. `[Hypothesis]` that they are the solid blades at two angles other than our rest pose.
- **Verdict**: control, no renderer change. The cause is a frozen rotor: the angle (`ROTY` on `0x14`, [#271](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/271)) and the arm (`BVGT` on `0x12`) both come from the angle bank, which our VM holds at 0. The fix needs both arms decoded and chosen per frame, a seam-contract change, so it is filed as an issue. `[Hypothesis]`, not observed, for why the capture shows those shapes at 837 and 881: frames in which var 10 moved by less than 16 since the previous one.
