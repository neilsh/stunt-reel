## 2026-11-13: which track stand-in TRAINTNL draws at tick 12

- **Question**: At `TRAINTNL` tick 12 the capture shows two index-40 rails in rows 155 to 184. Does `PLAYONE`'s `SP_1NODE` gate pick the 16 m stand-in `0x06CE` (two `LINECOL 0x0028` lines) there, or the 96 m `0x06CF` (one line) drawn so that it shows as two?
- **Method**: on `main` `ba11061e`:
  - Walked the scene tree of the unpacked unpatched image (`mise run unlzexe`; tree segment at image `0x22860`, `stunt_reel.scene_tree`) for every path to the track leaf `0x06D1` and recorded the `SP_1NODE` chain above it.
  - Took the lens from `mise run flm-events` on `TRAINTNL.FLM` (`op 0x97`: view camera 7; `var_set 7 = 24576` at tick 0) and the tick-12 camera from the `render.json` of `mise run frame -- --film traintnl --ticks 12,34,100 --scale 3` (eye `(-1599.16, 23.94, 7302.60)`, target `(-606.80, -53.35, 7206.40)`, field of view 14.84°).
  - Evaluated the gate of [`lod.md`](../../../research/playone-exe/lod.md) § 1.1 and § 2.2 at each node: `CX = floor(depth / 2^(0x229E + 1))` against `half + ((splitdist - half) × 0x22A4 >> 16)`, with the lens words computed as `0x11FC..0x124D` computes them, at detail `0xFFFF`. Each drawn stand-in was projected to a screen row (focal length 768 px, centre row 92.5) with a throwaway script.
  - Listed the palette indices along the track in rows 64 to 184 of the capture (`vault-traintnl` frame 1, tick 12) and of our render.
  - Read the stand-ins' bytecode with `stuntreel sod-trace` on the capture's `TRAINTNL.ALL`.
- **Result**:
  - **The chain.** Above each `0x06D1` leaf: tree offset `0xAA80` (`obj 0`, `ext` 296, `splitdist` 1000), `0xAA8A` (`0x06D0`, 296, 250), `0xAAAC` (`0x06CF`, 56, 50), `0xAACE` (`0x06CE`, 16, 10), `0xAAEC` (`0x06CD`, 10, 5). The `0xAAAC` nodes repeat every 96 m along the track and the `0xAACE` nodes every 16 m; the subtrees are shared, reached through `SP_POS` nodes. The near track runs along raw `z = 7273` (our `x = -raw x`).
  - **The lens words.** Lens 24576 gives `0x229C = 0x5FFF`, `0x229E = 2` and `0x22A4 = 0xBFFD`, so `CX` is depth / 8. The thresholds are 44 for `0xAAAC` (28 + (22 × 0xBFFD >> 16)) and 9 for `0xAACE` (8 + (2 × 0xBFFD >> 16)). So `0x06CF` is drawn where its node lies at least 360 m deep, and `0x06CE` where its own node lies at least 80 m deep and the `0x06CF` node above it lies less than 360 m deep.
  - **The gate at tick 12.** The `0xAAAC` nodes at raw `x` 1538, 1442, 1346 and 1250 lie 65, 161, 256 and 351 m deep (`CX` 8, 20, 31, 43, at most 44: kept, descend). The node at raw `x` 1154 lies 447 m deep (`CX` 55): there `0x06CF` is drawn. The `0x06CE` pieces drawn under rows 154 to 185 are at raw `x` 1482, 1466 and 1450, 121 to 153 m deep (`CX` 15 to 19, above 9). The `0x06CE` pieces run from row 185 up to about row 79, where the last piece under the node 351 m deep ends (raw `x` 1202, 399 m deep). `0x06CF`, from the node 447 m deep, lies above that. Drawing `0x06CF` in rows 155 to 184 would need that part of the track more than twice as deep (beyond 360 m rather than 161 m at its node). Drawing `0x06CD` there would need it closer than 80 m.
  - **The bytecode.** `0x06CE` paints `COLOR 0x000B` (bed 11), `COLOR 0x0084` (one 132 polygon) and `LINECOL 0x0028` with two `MOVETO`/`ZLINETO` pairs (`0x00E0..0x00EC`). `0x06CF` paints `COLOR 0x000C` (bed 12), `COLOR 0x0084` and one `MOVETO`/`ZLINETO` (`0x00B2..0x00B6`). So bed colour 11 rules out `0x06CF`. It does not tell `0x06CE` from `0x06CD` or `0x06D1`, which also paint 11 (the 435 journal); the depths pick `0x06CE`.
  - **The capture.** In rows 79 to 184 the track's bed, where the tunnel does not cover it, is index 11. Index 12 appears in rows 73, 75, 76 and 78, above the switch the gate puts at about row 79. Index 40 forms two separate runs across the track in 22 of the 30 rows 155 to 184, in 17 of the 25 rows 130 to 154, and in 2 of the 30 rows 100 to 129, where the rails lie side by side as one run of 2 to 3 pixels. A typical row reads 11, 132, 40, 132, 40, 11 (row 130), with the 132 between the rails. The capture holds 76, 62 and 70 index-40 pixels in those three bands.
  - **Ours.** Our render shows the same bed 11 from row 79 down and 12 in rows 73 to 78, so its gate switches at the same row. Its gate is the same arithmetic (`lod_keeps_subtree`), so it draws `0x06CE` there too. It holds no index-40 pixel anywhere in rows 100 to 184; the track there is 11 with some 132. The rails are missing, not single. That fits [#469](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/pull/469)'s reading: `tessellate.rs` puts the rail lines in `SOD_BASE` and the tie in `SOD_DECAL`, so the tie covers the rails ([#448](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/448)). This entry did not test that.
- **Verdict**: control (research only, no render change). `PLAYONE` draws `0x06CE` in rows 155 to 184 at tick 12. The two rails are its two `ZLINETO` lines. The capture's bed colour 11 rules out `0x06CF`, and the depths pick `0x06CE` over the finer shapes. The `SP_1NODE` records are the unpatched image's. The Patch #3 image the capture ran holds the same four at image `0x2B1BA`, `0x2B1DC`, `0x2B1FE` and `0x2B21C` (checked by the fresh reviewer). Our gate already picks `0x06CE` there, so no LOD change is licensed. The missing rails are #448's. The premise that ours draws `0x06CF`'s single line in those rows is ruled out. Not done: a DOSBox-X break on the gate at `0x2133`, which would show `CX` live.
