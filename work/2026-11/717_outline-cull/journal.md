# Journal: back-facing outlines on per-frame shapes

## 2026-11-20: the outline cull judged on the evidence track

- **Question**: Does culling the outline of a back-facing polygon on per-frame shapes, as `PLAYONE` culls it with the polygon, pass the adoption rule's evidence-backed track now that [#850](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/850) cites the rule at an image offset ([#730](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/pull/730), [#717](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/717))?
- **Track and offsets**: evidence-backed. The rule is the fillers' row test: they skip a row whose right word is less than its left (`0x75A1` `jl`; `0x7612` and `0x76DC` `inc ax; jg`), the left word taken from the vertex list walked backward and the right from it walked forward by the span builder `0xCA5D..0xCF22` ([`sod-vm-lines-spans.md` § 8.12](../../../research/playone-exe/sod-vm-lines-spans.md#812-back-faces-the-span-test), `[Established]`, static, read in [#850](../850_playone-back-face-cull/journal.md)). No target frames: the round names none.
- **Method**: `mise run parity` on the RTX 4070 Laptop GPU, once per tree with the other's `crates/` checked out clean. Baseline: `origin/main` at `496a958`, `crates` tree `42cac41`. Candidate: the merge `17c2abd` of `origin/main` into the PR's branch, `crates` tree `8e4e65d` (`07a2813` and the test fix `23878da` on top of main). Then `mise run judge -- <main reports> <head reports> --track evidence`. The reports are `build/scratch/730-adopt/report-main` and `report-head` (not committed); the judge's output is `build/scratch/730-adopt/judge.txt`.
- **Result**: `mean_ssim_scored`, head minus main, in the film order of the table: ctry -0.0000006, intro -0.0000137, land +0.0000033, balloon +0.0000073, traintnl -0.0000012, bomb -0.0000028, location -0.0000003. IoU: ctry -0.0000003, land -0.0000007, bomb -0.0000004, the others 0. 0 frames fall by more than 0.005 (worst -0.0011) and none outside a target moved by more than 0.005. Per-frame index agreement, candidate better/equal/worse: ctry 0/109/5, intro 13/191/5, land 2/95/0, balloon 4/94/9, traintnl 0/112/0, bomb 9/270/37, location 4/716/12. No class loss moved.
  Two films rise, by amounts far under the 0.002 policy bound ([`method.md`](../../../research/parity/method.md) section 3), so the scores neither support nor weaken the cull: the rule is the criterion and the score guards against misapplying it.
  `winner: run=candidate adopts (evidence, film means)`.
- **Mismatch left open**: `PLAYONE` keeps a back face's row where its edges' runs cross (§ 8.12), where the renderer exempts a triangle under 1 px² of screen area, so a thin back face the renderer keeps may be one `PLAYONE` draws a row of, and a wide one it culls may differ in its crossing rows. A separate `needs-triage` follow-up covers it; this PR does not change it.
- **Verdict**: adopted, evidence track, film means (land and balloon rise, no film falls by more than 0.002, no frame by more than 0.005). Repinned in `chore(parity): repin expected scores (#717)`.
- **Lives in**: [`attribution.md` § 6](../../../research/parity/attribution.md#6-back-faces).

## 2026-11-20: the two exemptions the fix round added

- **Question**: Why did the first GPU judge of the cull (`a2e237b`) show 16 frames down by more than 0.005, worst `location:1112` -0.0251, and dashes and slivers on `location`?
- **Method**: Sweeps of the edge-on threshold over 0.5, 1 and 4 px² (round 1), then the shadow exemption added at 1 px² (round 2), each judged on the heuristic track against main's GPU reports on `crates` tree `1490c82`; GPU run on the RTX 4070 Laptop, commits `07a2813` and `23878da`.
- **Result**: Edge-on triangles (rotor blades, struts): the triangle's screen winding is rounding error, and it differs between the four corners of one outline quad and between its three edges, so some corners collapsed to the screen centre and others did not (dashes, and slivers running to the centre). A threshold of 1 px² of screen area keeps their outlines; without it `location:1112` still falls by 0.0183. Shadows: the shadow pipeline culls no face, yet the vertex shader still culled a shadow's outline, which thinned `intro`'s shadow rims (9 frames down by more than 0.005 after round 1, worst `intro:1090` -0.0097); a shadow's outline now culls nothing. After round 2: 0 frames down by more than 0.005 (worst -0.0011), `location:1112` identical to main, `intro:1090` differing by 1 pixel. The same two artefacts are absent on lavapipe (`llvmpipe`, LLVM 21.1.7; film deltas under 0.000014).
- **Verdict**: control for the findings (a visual mismatch fixed, no adoption by score); the cull's adoption is the entry above.
- **Lives in**: [`attribution.md` § 6](../../../research/parity/attribution.md#6-back-faces).
