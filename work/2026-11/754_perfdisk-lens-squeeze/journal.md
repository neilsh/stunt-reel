# Journal: OP_PERFDISK lens squeeze

## 2026-11-18: the disc squeezed along depth by the lens zoom

- **Question**: Is `LOCATION.ALL` shape `0x07C6`'s ground disc 15 rows too near at its near rim ([#754](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/754)) because `PLAYONE` builds the disc's outline in the zoomed view space, whose depth is the true depth over the lens zoom?
- **Method**: Static read of `0xA08E..0xA106` with `mise run re-tool -- disasm 0xA040 0xA2C0` (unpatched image) beside `0x9117..0x9121` (`work:0x8070`) and the view-matrix rescale of [`lod.md` § 2.2](../../../research/playone-exe/lod.md#22-how-the-lens-scales-the-projection). Candidate behind `DBG_DISCZOOM` (0 off, 1 on), cloud session, lavapipe, on `origin/main` `15a615e` plus the uncommitted candidate: `OP_PERFDISK` rim vertices carry the vector to the disc's centre, and the vertex shader scales each one's clip-space depth offset from the centre (clip `z` and `w`) by the lens zoom over the default lens, `tan(42.67° / 2) / tan(fov / 2)`. Rim rows read off index `0x4A` with `mise run frame -- --film location --ticks 6062,6161 --software-only --var DBG_DISCZOOM=0,1`, every sixth row. Evidence-backed track, scored with `mise run sweep -- --var DBG_DISCZOOM=0,1 --track evidence --record work/2026-11/754_perfdisk-lens-squeeze --topic 'perfdisk lens squeeze' --target location:5953-6161 --images work/2026-11/754_perfdisk-lens-squeeze/images`.
- **Result**: The read: `0xA092..0xA0C6` takes the centre as the register plus the object origin, `x` and `y` in the screen rows' units (the view matrix's rows 0 and 1, times `work:0x8108` in Q15) and `z` in the depth row's (shifted right by `work:0x810C`), and the radius as `(radius · work:0x8070) >> 16`, `0x8070` being `0x8108 >> work:0x806F` (`0x911A..0x9121`), so the radius shares the `x` and `y` scale. `0xA0D3..0xA0E8` rescales the normal's depth difference by `0x8108 · 2^(0x810C + 1) >> 16`, which puts it in the screen rows' units too, and `0xA0EC..0xA106` stores the three components. So the centre's depth alone is in other units: the true depth over the zoom (`0x8108 / 0x8000 · 2^0x810C`). With the projector's `256 · x / z` (`0x9CF1`), and taking the builder to outline the circle its inputs describe (not traced through `0xA10A..0xA2B7` term by term), that outline is the projection of a circle of the true radius, perpendicular to the true normal, about a centre whose depth is divided by the zoom: in true view space, the true disc with every point's depth offset from its centre times the zoom. At ticks 6062 and 6161 the lens draws at 50.95° against the default 42.67°, a zoom of 0.820, so `PLAYONE`'s disc is 18 % shallower than ours. With `DBG_DISCZOOM=1`, rows read as before: the near rim is row 153 at both ticks (capture 151, before 166); the far rim row 75 at 6062 and 76 at 6161 (capture 76, before 77); the free right end of rows 106, 112 and 118 at 6062 is x 295, 299 and 298 (capture 298, 300, 299; before 299, 305, 309); the free left end of rows 106, 112, 118 and 124 at 6161 is x 21 at each (capture 22, 19, 20, 23; before 17, 14, 11, 8). The sweep (lavapipe, `llvmpipe`): `winner: DBG_DISCZOOM=1 adopts (evidence, film means; target idx +0.0423 attribution; worst target frame +0.0297 location:6007)`; `LOCATION` 0.8320 → 0.8332 (+0.00113); `INTRO` −0.000024, `TRAINTNL` −0.000010, `LAND` −0.000001, under the 0.002 floor; `CTRY`, `BALLOON` and `BOMB` changed no frame (each at the default lens, where the squeeze is 1), so `BOMB`'s wheel at ticks 2058 and 2070 is as before; no frame fell by more than 0.005. Figures: [`sweeps/perfdisk-lens-squeeze.toml`](sweeps/perfdisk-lens-squeeze.toml). Pictures (capture, baseline, winner): [5953](images/location-5953-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png), [6007](images/location-6007-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png), [6062](images/location-6062-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png), [6106](images/location-6106-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png), [6161](images/location-6161-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png). The other films' most-fallen frames, rendered with `mise run frame -- --software-only --var DBG_DISCZOOM=0,1` (capture, off, on), where small wheel discs move by a pixel or two: [INTRO 2674](images/intro-2674-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png) (−0.0012), [INTRO 1321](images/intro-1321-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png) (−0.0010), [TRAINTNL 265](images/traintnl-265-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png) (−0.0004), [LAND 540](images/land-540-perfdisk-lens-squeeze.png) (−0.0005). `mise run parity -- --software-only` on the commit of the fix (`crates/` `da993ac`) gave the sweep's film means; the repin is in the next entry, after the rebase.
- **Verdict**: adopted, evidence-backed (`0xA092..0xA106`): the renderer squeezes each `OP_PERFDISK` disc along the view's depth about its centre by the lens zoom over the default lens, in the vertex shader. Of #754's candidates, the normal and the `0x8108` rescale were the cause, read as above: the rescale matches the normal to the screen rows, and the centre's depth is the term left unscaled. The stepped ellipse of `0xD11D` and the disc's placement are not needed for the 15-row gap: with the squeeze alone the rims are within 2 rows and the free ends within 3 px. The fix is lavapipe-only evidence; [#689](https://github.com/neilsh/stunt-reel/issues/689)'s GPU re-read confirms it on the same ticks.
