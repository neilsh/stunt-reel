# Journal: film variables 10 and 12

## 2026-11-13: the bank writer read and broken on

- **Question**: How do film variables 10 and 12 change per tick in `PLAYONE.EXE`, how do they become the bank words `0x10`..`0x1A` that `ROT*` read, and are those words per actor or per film?
- **Method**: Static, at `b489f84d`, on the unpatched load image: `mise run disasm` of `0x1D50..0x1E40`, `0x0EE6..0x0F84`, `0x1360..0x13C0`, `0x4A20..0x4A40`; `mise run xref` on `0x22BC`, `0x811E`, `0x8120`, `0x8122`, `0x8126`, `0x8128`, `0x92`, `0x96`, `0x47DC`, `0x47E0`, `0x491A`, `0x491E`, `0xBE`, `0xDE`, `0x9E`; a scratch byte scan of `0..0xD510` for near `call`/`jmp` (`E8`/`E9`) targets `0x1D96` and `0x1DD5`; the image word at `DS:0x22BC` (image `0xF7CC`). Live: `mise run debug-x -- --plan tools/dosbox-x/plans/film-vars-10-12.toml --film corpus/stisldem/VAULT/INTRO.FLM`, unpatched, DOSBox-X 2026.10.01, three runs (`<data root>/debug/INTRO/film-vars-10-12-unpatched-20261114T0323*.jsonl`, `…T032647Z`, `…T032917Z`). The first had no bank watch; the second added it armed at tick 2800, and it and the tick-2800 break never armed (the run's next stop was tick 2840); the third, the committed plan (sha256 `f42b8692…` as run; only a header comment changed after), arms the watch at 1300 and the break at 2790. A scratch script checked each break stop against the rule. `mise run flm-events` for the var 10 and var 12 keys of `CTRY.FLM` and `INTRO.FLM`.
- **Result**:
  - `0x1D96` (with `BX` the film state): `0x8126 = [bx+0x96]` (var 12), `0x8128 = −var 12`; `dx = [bx+0x92]` (var 10), `xchg ax,[0x22BC]` leaves the previous value in `ax`, `dx −= ax`, then `neg dh; sbb dh,dh; or dh,dl; xor dl,dl` makes `0x8120 = (d < 256 ? d : 0xFF) << 8`; `0x8122 = (previous var 10 & 0xFF) << 8`; `0x811E = [bx+0x0E] << 12`.
  - Callers: `0x1D96` only from `0x1DD6`, `0x1DD5` only from `0x0F77` in `0x0F4A`, which `0x4A2F` reaches through `0x0EE9` with `BX = 0x4888`. `0x1DD5` then loops over `[bx+0x2E]` (count `[bx+0x1E]`) drawing objects (`0x1E07..0x1E3A`).
  - `+0x92`, `+0x96` and the fixed DS addresses of vars 10 and 12 are named only at `0x1D9B` and `0x1DA9`; the start/end buffers only by the generic `var_set`/`before` handlers, the slice copy `0x3C9D` and the interpolation `0x136F`. `0x22BC` has one reference and image value 0.
  - Break stops at `0x1DD4`: 34, 24 and 36 in the three runs, 94 in all, `BX = 0x4888` on each, none off the rule. Successive frames 2 to 5 ticks apart. Var 12 0 at ticks 1337–1452, 16384 at 2804–2840 with the bank holding `0x4000`/`0xC000`. `0x12` ranged `0x0600`..`0xD400`, plus `0xFF00` at the first stop of the tick-2800 window.
  - The frame step `0x4A2F` and the bank stops alternated strictly in every run; the actor draw `0x1D66` stopped 16 to 18 times in each complete frame its 60-stop cap allowed (ticks 1334–1382).
  - Third run's bank watch (`linear 0x2609E`, 12 bytes; work segment `0x1DF8` from `ES` in the first run), ticks 1364–1418, 40 changing writes: `0x1DBD` 13, `0x1DC6` 12, `0x1D69` 15. No write was attributed to `0x1DD0`, though the break stops show `0x811E` following the clock.
  - Watch on `DS:0x491A` (var 10, `0x4888` struct): writer `0x13AC` on 24 of 24 in each run. Watch on `DS:0x47DC` (var 10, `0x474A` struct): no change after tick 1300 in any run.
  - Hypotheses this tested: (a) the bank words are set per actor — rejected; (b) `0x8120` is the sign of var 10's change, as `palette-fade.md` had it — rejected, it is the saturated change; (c) `0x8122` is the current var 10's low byte, as `sod-vm.md` § 8.7 had it — rejected, it is the previous frame's. One round; no further hypotheses needed.
- **Verdict**: control (static reading and breakpoints). `[Established]` for the rule and its once-a-frame film-level scope; `[Bounded Observation]` for the 2-to-5-tick frame spacing (one film, one emulator setting).
